# Fam Jam Season 4 — podium subject data

Reference data for generating podium artwork for the top three finishers of a private,
family-and-friends music competition (Music League). Eleven players, twelve rounds, ended
2026-08-30. The artwork appears on a "Season Results" podium in the season recap.

Use this file to understand **who each person is**. Translate that into colour, geometry,
texture, rhythm and mood. Do **not** translate it into objects — see `DO NOT DEPICT` under
each person, which is binding.

---

## SUBJECT: Emily — "Em"

- **Card name:** Em · **Initial:** `E` · **Finished:** 1st, 125 points — **the champion**

### Her season
- Won the league, and it was an upset. Nobody had her picked. The group chat's verdict:
  "Upset of the decade," "Historic season," "I feel a Netflix doc coming."
- **She won on floor, not ceiling.** Her worst round of the whole season was 6 points —
  nobody else in the league had a floor above 4. She is the only player who never posted
  a round below 5.
- Top three in 7 of 12 rounds, top five in 10 of 12 — both the best in the league.
- Her single biggest round (16 points) was only the **seventh**-largest score of the season.
  Five players out-peaked her. Nobody out-steadied her.
- She chose the theme for one round herself — an electronic-music round — and then won it,
  with a track the league immediately complained barely fit the theme. "Which is why it won."
- Her written verdicts on other people's songs are famously minimal: "Fuunn." "Rad."
  "😍😍😍😍😍😍"
- On winning: *"Wow guys never thought I'd see the day!"* — then immediately redirected the
  praise to another player.

### The person
- Art dealer by profession.
- Loves electronic music.
- Vegetarian.
- **Relentlessly, sincerely kind** — the most positive voice in a chat otherwise built on
  insult comedy. Notably more upbeat than the group around her.
- An excellent athlete; a point guard — a facilitator, the one who sets other people up.
- Married to Brianna, the younger sister of the runner-up. The two are just beginning an
  Italian holiday for a significant birthday.

### Evoke
Expansive · warm · rhythmic · buoyant · generous · steady · radiant. Something that grows
outward from the centre and never contracts. Consistency as a virtue, not as dullness.

### DO NOT DEPICT
Basketballs, hoops, courts, sports of any kind, DJ decks, turntables, headphones, speakers,
musical notes, food, vegetables, art galleries, picture frames, Italy, landmarks.

## SUBJECT: Mara — "missmara"

- **Card name:** missmara · **Initial:** `M` · **Finished:** 2nd, 122 points

### Her season
- Led the league for **five consecutive rounds** and finished three points short.
- Scored the season's joint-highest single song: 22 points, in a round themed around
  music that "doesn't taste like dirt."
- **She runs the league.** She writes the themes, compiles the playlists, keeps the
  spreadsheets, and formally assigns every player their "biggest critic": *"Mom, your
  biggest critic is Jo. Bri, yours is Ari."*
- Votes for the reasoning behind a song rather than the song, and says so out loud:
  *"My principles are at war. I am constitutionally opposed to voting for songs I can't
  bear to listen to. But I respect and accept your reasons for submitting it."*
- Recurring refrain when awarding points she doesn't want to award:
  *"points have to go somewhere."*
- On a rival's submission: *"Every time I hear a new Halsey song in music league, I
  discover a new Halsey song I dislike."*

### The person
- Runs a business helping high-school students apply to college.
- Listens to rap — **especially indie rap**.
- **Feisty.** Combative in a fond way; the sharpest voice in the room.
- **Hates talking about her feelings.** Deflects sincerity with a joke or an edge.

### Evoke
Sharp · high-contrast · defiant · controlled · guarded · exacting. Order that is holding
something in. Tension, structure under strain, an edge that doesn't soften.

### DO NOT DEPICT
Graduation caps, diplomas, mortarboards, schools, campuses, books, microphones, boomboxes,
gold chains, spreadsheets, gavels, clipboards.

## SUBJECT: Johanna

### Her season
- **Won three rounds — more than anyone else in the league**, including the very first
  round of the season and the finale itself.

- Married to a cousin of the other two players on this podium — she married into this family.

- A young mother of two.
- Dry, warm, and quietly funny; delivers a hard verdict wrapped in affection.

### DO NOT DEPICT
Looms, yarn, thread spools, knitting, sewing, fabric swatches, tapestries, Swedish flags,

## Universal rules for all three

1. **No faces, no people, no portraits, no hands.**
2. **No text of any kind** except the single capital initial named for that subject.
3. **Nothing literal.** If a viewer could name the person's job or hobby from the image,
   it has failed. The likeness is emotional, not descriptive.
4. The three subjects must feel like **one set** when a single style is applied to all of
   them — same technique and treatment, differing in palette and geometry.
5. These are real people who will each see their own card. Flattering or neutral only.
   No caricature, no visual joke at their expense.
