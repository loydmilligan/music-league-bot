## How to use the data file

Find the section for the named SUBJECT. Read their season, their personality, and their
**Evoke** line. Translate that into **colour, geometry, texture, rhythm, weight and

## The styles

**STYLE A — RISOGRAPH POSTER**
Flat two-colour risograph print. Coarse paper grain, visible halftone dots, deliberate
2mm ink misregistration, bold overlapping flat shapes. The subject's initial is enormous
and forms the composition itself, constructed out of the geometry that suits that person.
Two inks overprinting into a third colour where they cross, on a deep charcoal ground.
Graphic, punchy, poster-like.

**STYLE B — PIGMENT BLOOM**
Abstract macro photograph of ink blooming into water against near-black. Pigment unfurling
in whatever motion suits the subject — expanding, turbulent, or settling in slow layers.
Lit so the pigment glows against the dark. The subject's initial appears as clean negative
space that the pigment flows around and never enters. Atmospheric, organic, painterly. No
visible container, surface or background objects.

**STYLE C — STRUCK MEDAL**
A square commemorative medal struck in metal, photographed straight on under raking light.
The subject's initial is deeply struck into the surface, surrounded by an engraved
guilloché pattern whose geometry suits that person. Brushed metal texture, crisp bevelled
edges, deep shadow in the recesses, highlights on the raised areas. Ceremonial and
trophy-like. Metal colour by rank: **1st = warm gold · 2nd = cool silver · 3rd = warm
bronze with a faint patina** (the data file gives each subject's finishing position). Very
dark background; the metal itself provides the light.

## Image parameters — apply to every generation

- **Square, 1:1, 1024 × 1024, PNG.**
- **It will be displayed small — about 200 pixels wide.** Use bold, large-scale shapes and
  high contrast. No fine detail, no thin lines, no small elements. This is the most common
  way these fail.
- It sits on a **very dark charcoal background (#141921)** in a dark editorial magazine
  layout. It must read clearly against near-black, and must not be a flat white rectangle.
- **Keep the top-left corner quiet** — roughly the top-left 20% × 20% is overlaid by a small
  rank badge. Nothing important there.
- The card is displayed **next to real album artwork**, so it should hold its own without
  imitating an album cover.
- **No human faces, no people, no portraits, no hands.**
- **No words, text, captions, watermarks or logos of any kind** — the only glyph is the
  single capital initial given for that subject in the data file.
- If you cannot render the letter cleanly, produce the image **without** it and say so; the
  letter can be added afterwards.
