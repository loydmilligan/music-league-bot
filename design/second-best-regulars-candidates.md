# Second Best "The Regulars" — candidate motifs

Mined from the full `Music League chat for Second Best and Friends` WhatsApp
history (2026-05-06 → 2026-08-09; 2,538 messages). Quotes are verbatim (edited
re-sends and near-duplicate repeats deduped). Times/dates converted to PDT
(UTC-7). Excluded per brief: **Matt Mariani / Mashew** (commissioner + the
digest's reader).

**Scope caveat up front:** unlike the sssc corpus (9+ months, Discord), this
league has only ~3 months of chat and the roster is a tight friend-group of
Bakersfield/Garces-High-class-of-'97 guys plus spouses. The register is
relentless affectionate ribbing. Two structural facts shape the motifs:
1. The whole crew shares a **Bakersfield/Oildale/Garces nostalgia** bit — it is
   group texture, not one person's signature (see caveats).
2. Matt posts **AI-generated videos/recaps**, so "AI" reactions recur across
   several players; only Jac has turned it into a personal bit.

Evidence is overwhelmingly in **chat**, not vote comments. I checked vote
comments for every candidate motif term — they returned almost only false
positives (e.g. "cat" inside "category"/"catchy"/"Thundercat"). So every seed
below is sourced to `chat` only, on purpose.
