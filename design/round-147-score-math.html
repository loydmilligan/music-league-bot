Where the numbers come from

Round 147 · “I Hope You Shit Your Pants at Target” · worked examples: Radio (13 pts, 1st) and The Story of Adidon (0 pts, 9th)

The scoring rules in play

  • Every voter gets 8 upvote points and 2 downvote points, spent in whatever chunks they like across the round's songs.
  • 9 people voted this round (Koziol didn't), so the total pool on the table was 72 upvote points and 18 downvote points. All 90 were spent.
  • You can't vote on your own song — so each song faced 8 eligible voters, not 9. You can leave a comment on it.
  • A row worth 0 points is a comment, not a vote. It moves nothing. Silence is a separate thing: no row at all.
  • Failing-to-vote penalty: if you don't vote, your song's upvotes are voided and its downvotes stand. Neither song below is affected — both submitters voted. (This is what took Koziol's Alanis from a raw 0 to a final −5.)
