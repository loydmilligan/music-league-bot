## Why this format fits
The shortest treatment in the set and the second-strongest. `Previously On…` is
structurally identical to `receipts` — juxtaposition, no commentary — but the TV framing
buys a rhythm and a hard out that the bare-cards version doesn't have, at the cost of a
tiny bit of AI whimsy in the recap-music convention.

Notably this bit contains **no current-round content whatsoever**. It is 100% archive.
That makes it the clearest proof of the spike's central hypothesis: a digest section can
be built entirely out of retrieval, with the generator contributing only sequencing.
