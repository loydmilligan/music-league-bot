## Why this format fits
The premise of the incident is *jurisprudence in a group chat* — a man who legislated in a
vote comment. Institutional framing is not decoration here; the format and the source
material are the same joke. The court can also read two real quotes side by side without
narration, which is exactly what the incident needs and what Breaking News couldn't do.

## Reservations
Longest of the set at ~32s and the closing line is a *written* joke rather than a
retrieved one — it is the one moment where the AI, not the league, supplies the punchline.
It earns its place only because the two exhibits under it are real. If the format recurred
weekly, the offence names would degrade into a list very fast.
