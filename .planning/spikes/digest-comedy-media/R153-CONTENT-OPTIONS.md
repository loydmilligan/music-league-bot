## The feature you were thinking of — it exists, and it has a gap

You remembered right, with one correction: it produces **5–8 story ledes**, not two, and it
runs **after a round ends**, not during. That's why nothing exists for R153 yet.

| Piece | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/digest-qa/generate_bridge.py` | Writes the context-forward file → `digest_bridges`: `headline_stories`, `running_bits`, `callbacks_planted`, `notable_quotes`, plus a summary of every section | after a digest is finalised |
| `scripts/digest-qa/generate_ledes.py` | Reads the round's ballots, comments, chat window **and the previous round's bridge**, then asks headless Claude for *"5–8 candidate story ledes: the angles the digest could focus on, each with verbatim evidence"* → `digest_ledes`, rated on the `/hil` page | right after a round ends, before punch-up |

**The gap: R149 has ledes but no bridge.** Boarz bridges exist only for R147 and R148.

That matters because `generate_ledes.py` for R153 will look up the previous round by voting
deadline — **R149** — and find nothing. The continuity this round is supposed to inherit
(the Regulars, the Consul, Sebastian James, the carrot-box thread) is not written down
anywhere the generator can reach.
