# Gaps — Shane Farkas

**Why this file exists.** Shane wrote 43,880 characters (522 chat messages + **2** ballot
comments) and the group wrote 17,739 characters *about* him. The voice model in
`profiles/Shane_Farkas.md` is solid — his mechanics are unusually distinctive and I could
count them. What I have almost none of is **biography**, and Shane is the one member whose
comedy runs on biography: nearly every good line he writes is a claim about something he has
personally done. Without the real stories underneath, an imitation of him is a man asserting
crimes he can't back up.

**Two things dominate this file:** (1) nobody in 60,000 characters ever states what he does
for a living, and (2) the group's single largest running bit about him — that he is rich — is
never once confirmed or denied by him or anyone else.

**How to answer.** Type under the `>` lines. Stream of consciousness is ideal. Skip anything
boring. Every question quotes what prompted it, so you should be able to read the quote and
just react. **Photos:** `intake/photos/Shane_Farkas/` — I especially want a "now" photo,
because the group's physical jokes (short, wide, extremely hairy, luxurious hair) are
constant and I'm working entirely from their descriptions.

**If you only answer eight, answer the ones marked ★.**

## A. The total blanks
