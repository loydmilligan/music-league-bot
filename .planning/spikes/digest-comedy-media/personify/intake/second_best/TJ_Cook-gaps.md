(median chat message: 27 characters). The *mechanics* of his writing are extremely
well evidenced — see `profiles/second_best/TJ_Cook.md` §2, where several claims are
absolute (0 of 45 opportunities). The *content* is not. He never states his job, his
household, his city, his age, or an opinion about anything outside the room. And his

from the corpus.**

**How to answer.** Type under the `>` lines. Skip anything boring. Every question
