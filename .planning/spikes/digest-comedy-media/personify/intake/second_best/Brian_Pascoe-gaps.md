# Gaps — Brian Pascoe ("BP")

**Status of the dossier.** `profiles/second_best/Brian_Pascoe.md` is built on 476
messages (~39.5k chars) plus 77 mentions (~10k chars) — enough evidence that the *style*
sections are solid. What the corpus cannot supply is (a) biography, (b) the meaning of
about fifteen Bakersfield references he uses as if everyone knows them, and (c) the
direction of one or two things that read ambiguously. Those are below.

**How to answer.** Type under the `>` lines. Stream of consciousness is fine. Every
question quotes the message that prompted it. Skip anything boring.

**If you only answer eight, answer the six marked ★ plus Q7 and Q9.**

## A. Biography — what the corpus gestures at but never states

**Q1. ★ What does he do for a living, and what is "hotel"?**
He mentions work twice, both empty: *"Happy Tuesday @all , just trying to do anything but
tackle my next work task"* and *"Trying to explain to my employees en español how good
Music League chat is getting."* Jon Black locates a hotel with him: *"on the beach in
front of BP's hotel."* BP himself says *"added to hotel playlist"* and *"a jam sesh
happened yesterday at hotel."* Does he own/run a hotel in Todos Santos? How many
employees? Is that the whole job?
>

**Q2. ★ The eye.**
Jon Black, twice: *"BP only has one eye, which makes voting hard"* and *"nice to see BP
when he still had both eyes."* BP's only reference is *"Dropped a couple just about my eye
surgery"* (2026-06-07). What happened, when, and is it a joke the group makes to his face
or something they handle carefully? This matters because it determines whether an
imitation can touch it.
>

**Q3. Household — the wife.**
Three references, all functional, never named by him: *"Wife flipping her shit because we
cant find Mana and Shakira"*, *"had to clean up my wifes barf that this song provoked"*,
*"Wife always trying to play Mac Miller during intimacy"*, *"Just making lunch for my
Certified Head Doctor."* Elsewhere Matt writes *"the BP wedding… in the fairy meadow with
nary a porta potty"*, Jon writes *"when BP's original wedding date got cancelled… it was in
2014"*, and BP writes *"Ana at Burning Man."* Is Ana the wife? Is "Certified Head Doctor"
her profession (psychologist?) or a sex joke? Kids?
>

**Q4. Where is he actually from and where does he live now?**
Corpus says: Bakersfield / Garces High, class of ~1999 (*"Did any of us 99's varsity in
anything?"*), now Todos Santos, Baja California Sur, since at least 2010
(*"Im probably 7000 pesos in the hole, since 2010"*). Jon: *"BP the only Mexican in any of
your leagues? He's an émigré to boot."* Layous: *"Pascoe's basque ass."* Is he
Mexican-American, Basque, both? When did he move, and is Todos Santos permanent?
>

**Q5. Music — does he play?**
He says *"i made an effort to have a guitar available everywhere i spend time. probably
pick one up twice a week for last few months"*, *"I learned Mungo Jerry on guitar"*,
*"@Matt Mariani and I listened to this album un chingo while we were learning to play
guitar"*, and *"played a couple bass notes"* on a cello. Jon Black's ballot fantasy casts
him as **Second Best's bassist** and Jon lists him among *"Flea, Les, BP, Ray Manzarek."*
Was he actually in the band Second Best? Bass or guitar? Is Jon's whole ballad a bit or
built on a real fact?

## C. Direction-of-read questions the corpus leaves 50/50

**Q11. Is the mock-formal register deliberate comedy or just how he talks?**
About twenty messages lift into legalese or naturalist prose with no wink:
*"Validity of Liens in Narco controlled regions is dubious"*, *"Commish posish not for
lemmings"*, *"Only egregious errors warrant shakedowns in Todos Santos"*, *"Was he standing
his ground; guarding a set a values?"*, *"Thats secondbest. Underachieving moderately"*,
*"Battle to see who could have the eldest teenagers on their sports rosters"*.
The dossier calls these his best jokes. Does the group hear them as jokes?
>

**Q12. The apology — sincere or a bit?**
*"Apologies to the Music Leaguers not interested in the pretty birds from the class of 97
/ I will try to keep it on topic"*, then *"back to the weather"* as a self-reset. Nobody
had complained. Is he genuinely self-conscious about hijacking the chat, or is the
apology itself the joke?
>

**Q13. Why the 2026-08-13 lowercase run?**
Ten consecutive messages that day (the guitar-repair-shop thread) are almost entirely
lowercase — *"not for shopping"*, *"for repairing and adjusting"*, *"no with tha fingas"*
— against ~94% capitalisation everywhere else. Different device, laptop, dictation, or
just a mood? The dossier currently treats it as an anomaly and tells imitators not to
copy it.
>

**Q14. Is the near-total absence of self-deprecation real?**
One instance in 476 messages. Every other profiled member in this project has
self-deprecation as a major mode. Is BP genuinely un-self-mocking in person, or is that an
artefact of him mostly writing *about other people's* history?
>

**Q15. Layous.**
Michael Layous is the only person who takes shots at BP — *"Pascoe is fake"*, *"Pascoe
loves his rich blondes"*, *"Pascoe's basque ass wants votes for Kovac… but don't give it to
him"* — and BP returns fire (*"Wonder if Layous got DUI DOS"*, *"Layous gonna be pissed"*,
*"next best would be Layous performing Wet Dream"*). Is this an actual rivalry with
history, or just two guys who enjoy each other?
>

**Q16. The May–June disappearance.**
27 chat messages in eight weeks, then 363 in five. Matt: *"if only bp didnt abandon the
league."* Mara: *"You would have been if BP wasn't disqualified."* Jon: *"BP - no vote and
worst song."* What changed in August — the new season, the new chat, the eye surgery, the
end of the World Cup (*"Fifa finished. Mind on music league"*)?
