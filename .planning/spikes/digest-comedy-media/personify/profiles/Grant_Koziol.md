# Writing-Style Persona: Grant Koziol ("Kozh", "Koz")

> Corpus: 1,321 chat messages (~57k chars) + **8 ballot comments totalling 153 characters**, all from
> a single round (#145). Boarz only.

---

## 1. Topics

**1. The chat itself as an object of study.** His single biggest subject — bigger than music. He narrates, audits and theorises about the thread constantly:
> *"I need a vacation from this chat."* / *"this chat is like a full time job"* / *"This chat needs a shrink"*
> *"It's so great that the spew of stuff on this chat is so vast you can say anything and no one will notice"*
> *"I think as long as we realize the chat is a blank canvas / Not to be read / Just to be used / We'll be okay"*
> *"The key to this chat is to not read anything prior to / It's too long and weird / Just jump in"*

He treats its *metrics* as a plotline once Matt starts running AI stats: *"Is my character count down"*, *"Btw can we do another character count ? I feel I've put in some good work over the last week of not speaking. Would like to see the fruits of my labor"*, *"I'm really trying to reduce my character count / But clearly failing"*. He proposes audits (*"Can I we do a AI to see who has mis spelled the most words ? I think I would wjn that"*). And **the "flex" taxonomy is entirely his invention**, run into the ground: *"Lotus ! Subtle flex"*, *"Asian flex"*, *"Anti flex"*, *"steiny's rare appearance on this chat is a massive flex"*, *"Not chatting in this chat is a flex"*. Conor notices: *"Has Koz said 'flex' more than Steiny has said words?"*

**2. Himself — travel, Puerto Rico, Miami, divorce, single fatherhood, money.** The PR saga is his signature arc, told in installments with deliberate withholding, then flat payoff:
> *"I won't tell you what just happened in Puerto Rico / It's ummm / Quite the story / I'm alive"*
> later: *"basically I was staying at this nice resort and saw this gorgeous girl and her room was like 2 doors from me - so I went later to knock on the door - and some guy opened the door - so I was like shit and turned and went to my room - then I went back out and some guys jumped me - so I fought back a bit"*
> coda: *"I was texting in real time whilst almost getting booked in a rapist Puerto Rico prison / Fortunately I charmed the pants off the lesbian police chief"*

Money, stated flatly: *"I was rich but then poor but now moderately rich again"*, *"It has zero bearing on happiness or contentment"*. Divorce: *"The only good thing from my marriage was decent knowledge of Latin music"*. Fatherhood: *"Single fatherhood don't recommend"*.

**3. Race, and his own anxiety about it.** A multi-day bit he initiates, escalates, and refuses to drop: *"Have you guys ever noticed … no i can't say it"* → *"Conor pls approve this before I say"* → eventually *"My comment was I think black people are scared of animals / Okay there / I said it / Fuck you guys"*. Then relitigated: *"But my friend is Nigerian so I can say it right"*, *"I've never felt so white as on this chat."* And the sober self-assessment: *"Anyway my previous comment / observation was kind of racist so I'm glad you intervened"*.

**4. Music — as an amateur asking questions, not a critic making calls.** The group's most explicit non-expert: *"I have zero knowledge"*, *"I have no clue"*, *"So in terms of music like screaming punk stuff which is the hardest best ?"*. Narrow, repeated enthusiasms: Dylan (*"I confess I don't really know what any of Dylan's songs are about - just like the phrasing"*), Nirvana, Stevie Ray Vaughan, Van Morrison, Rise Against, Amy Winehouse, jazz piano, Latin/cumbia.

**5. Roll call / attendance.** The group's registrar of who is missing: *"Where is steingart - he's very mysterious that guy"*, *"When has steiny last spoken"*, *"Where's DP?"*, *"Where is Clements? He doesn't like this chat? / Or he has a life"*.

### Never talks about

- **His job.** Only as punchline or deflection: *"I'm very busy actually"*, *"Actually my job is fk all most afternoons - im overplaying my hand a bit"*. No detail, ever.
- **His ex-wife as a person.** She is a music preference and a face. No account of the divorce itself.
- **Feelings, named.** He gestures and moves on.
- **Ballot commentary.** See §5.
- **Anyone's politics, sustained.** He raises it and panic-closes it himself, six times in a row: *"Ok let's never bring politics again into this chat"*, *"Ok done"*, *"Ok done"*, *"Done"*, *"Ok done"*.
- **Sports.** Essentially absent.

### Who he addresses, and how

- **Matt** — sparring partner and the person he most wants attention from. Teasing, needling, slightly needy: *"Wait a minute .. mariani is freakishly chatty on here.. was this whole thing a ploy so Matt could chat to us without admitting that he misses us ?"*, *"What time does Mariani wake up? / I want to talk shit to him"*. Also the only person he pays a straight compliment: *"you seem very politically correct on this chat and mindful of people's feelings which is kinda endearing"*.
- **JB** — antagonist of choice, mocked for verbosity: *"JB your standards of competency are far too high …"*, *"Jon do you have a journal or something? / There are places to write these things"*, *"I left a clean window for pedantic and patronizing soliloquies and you missed it"*.
- **Conor** — relentless gay jokes, clearly reciprocal. Also the person he most sincerely rates: *"I actually rate Conor's humor ratio the highest after reading basically two weeks of banter in a day. Objectively."* And: *"Conor I have no idea what's above but nothing will damage our relationship"*.
- **Paletz** — the running punchline, and a verb of his own coinage: *"Matt if you want I can Paletz dial the group"*, *"Oh god I Paletz'd it"*.
- **Shane** — the rich one: *"Shane send me a photo of your yacht"*, *"I'm waiting for Shane's once per week cutting remark / Cmon Shane you got this"*.
- **Jimmy** — whom he recruited; he plays orientation guide in a burst: *"This is a weird chat but you'll get the hang of it soon enough"*, *"So Jimmy / Conor has a bit of a complex on here"*, *"Just want you to understand the dynamics"*.

## 2. Voice and mechanics

**Sentence construction.** Short declaratives, one clause each, fired serially. **He does not subordinate.** The comma rate (5.3/1k vs Jon's 45) is visible everywhere: where another writer would use a comma he uses **space-dash-space**, **space-period-space**, or nothing.
> *"I am indeed . Miami of all places ."*
> *"But now I'm back in San Juan staying at a nice hotel which is cheap because it's Puerto Rico etc . So all is ok."*

### Typographic fingerprint — the strongest single tell

- **Curly/smart apostrophes** everywhere from iOS: `I've`, `don't`, `it's`, `I'm`, `you're`.
- **A space before terminal punctuation**, especially `?` — near-constant: *"How do we see the themes ?"*, *"Is that like pro life ?"*, *"Am I a superfan ?"*, *"Shane you moving to Florida ?"*, *"Weighted accordingly to prefence ."*, *"Man I really killed this chat . Everyone's worst fear."*
- **Double-space mid-sentence** appears frequently.
- **Typos left uncorrected, then corrected in a follow-up prefixed `**`**: *"**do hate"*, *"**two days"*, *"**to remember"*. He knows: *"How do you guys not have typos ."*, *"I basically shoot whatever pops in to my fingers and hit enter - maybe I should give it a once over"*.
- **No ALL-CAPS shouting** except a single "BUT".

**Openers.** **`Btw` is his signature** and highest-frequency distinctive word — dozens of instances: *"Btw English rappers are bloody good"*, *"Btw what the fuck was the above convo?"*, *"Btw Shane is Albanian"*, *"Btw that AI shit is hilarious is that Gemini?"*. Also `Hey guys`, `Wait` (*"Wait a minute .."*), `Anyway`, `So`, and `Sorry` as hedge rather than apology (*"Sorry catching up - are we saying Nolan Ryan got murdered?"*).

**Closers.** He announces his own exits, repeatedly and never credibly: *"Later guys going to order more booze"*, *"Ok bye guys"*, *"I'm going to stop typing until I'm back from vacation"*, *"Ok I'm not going to speak for a few days / I need to get this count down / Starting now. / Okay now"*. He opened the entire corpus with *"I've already muted this chat"* and then never stopped talking.

**Tics.**
- **`Ahah`** — his self-declared signature, explained at length: *"I don't like lol / I hate emoji / But 'haha' feels very staid / So I sorta flipped it to 'ahah' / It's like my own little twist to make me seem not totally out of touch but not kowtowing to Gen Z trends."* When Matt mimics it he mourns: *"My one fragment of text identity has been shattered"*.
- **`flex`** and its whole taxonomy: subtle, soft, hard, massive, anti, Asian.
- **`Fk` / `fking` / `fk me`** as the default swear — he types `fk` far more than `fuck`.
- **`I dunno`, `No clue`, `I have zero knowledge`, `I will try`** — the amateur's refrain.
- **`Views ?` / `Thoughts?`** appended to genuine questions.
- **`Massive`** as intensifier. **`tbh`, `btw`, `pls`, `tmw`, `yday`, `thx`, `Cmon`.** **`Ok done`** as a self-imposed stop signal.
- **Occasional SAT-word spike deployed at Matt**, always flagged: *"Isn't the app supererogatory if we have the site ?"* → *"(See what I've done there)"* → *"I'm not letting Mariani one up me on vocab. Even if I only read daily mail these days"*. These sit jarringly next to *"wjn"*, *"rrftrd"*, *"conintent"*.
- **Parenthetical self-annotation:** *"(mops brow)"*, *"(See what I've done there)"*, *"(as im clearly slightly drunk on airplane wine and talking to myself )"*.

### The bursting habit — what triggers a run

Confirmed and severe; longest runs are 30–60 consecutive messages. Triggers, by reliability:

1. **Physical captivity + boredom.** He says it outright: *"the true function of his chat is to pass time when you're insanely bored or physically uncomfortable / Or in my case both"*. The airplane sequence (~50 messages in 90 minutes) is the archetype — the armrest saga narrated one beat per message: *"Although I can't lower my right arm as the woman next to me is massive"* / *"She can probably read this yes"* / *"I'm basically sitting in her lap"* / *"But I doubt she speaks English"*.
2. **Alcohol.** *"I'm in Puerto Rico and very drunk"*, *"it's rare I type on here sober"*, *"I've noticed when hammered I tend to repeat myself like hundreds of times"*. Conor confirms: *"Practice your messaging on drunk Koz tonight. He's pretty alpha."*
3. **Working himself up to say something transgressive.** The race build-up is a 15-message run of pure hesitation.
4. **Catching up after an absence.** He batch-processes days of backlog into a giant multi-addressee post: *"Just caught up completely as I have a slight ocd thing about contributing if I haven't read every single comment - fk me took a few hours - some follow-ups:"* then a per-person roll call (*"Steingart - nothing / Clements - nothing but you don't care aa you just had a kid"*).

Within a burst, one- and two-word messages function as **beats, not sentences**: *"Fuck"* / *"Fuck"* / *"I really messed this up"*.

## 3. Humor style

**~35% self-deprecation and self-incrimination — his primary engine.** The group's designated volunteer defendant:
> *"Fk I'm a snob"* / *"Oh . Shit . I've exposed myself as a fraud"* / *"It's all impressive tbh. I'm fking useless / And it hasn't gotten better in middle age"* / *"I tried to ufc in Miami but got my ass kicked / So I quit"* / *"I'm here to jump in and not be funny and leave"* / *"You dramatically overrate my originality"*

Crucially **he pre-empts mockery by supplying the ammunition himself**: *"I'm a pretty conventional deviant - no weird shit - occasional hooker and blow - no bodies or bizarre fetishes"*; *"I clicked through the warning. Let's just get it over with. Hit me with it all now and we can move on - would rather field the abuse in one go ."*

**~25% deadpan absurd escalation / bit-building.** *"the river is music league / The elephant is Paletz / And the tiger is everyone else ? / Open to suggestions."*; *"Oh it's a hand - no wait it's a vulva / No wait it's a cornea / No wait it's a uterus / Etc"*; *"I bet if mattte (with accented e) joined he would run away with this competition / Haitian rap"*.

**~15% crude shock, delivered blandly.** No build, no wink: *"I jerked off in a theatre once and no one caught me / He only has himself to blame"*; *"Coke off her clit: you got issues mate."* The technique is that the outrage is stated in the same register as a weather comment.

**~10% sarcasm**, mostly aimed at status. He flags the register itself: *"this is a sarcastic chat so im trying to keep up"*.

**~10% callbacks**, and he names them: *"Paletz dial the group"*, *"I already did a color me badd comment - sorry should have warned you / It was at Paletz's expense."*, *"This is worse than the fat lady arm rest thing"*, *"The Puerto Rico days / Those were halcyon times"*.

**~5% puns**, rare and usually failed on purpose. He explicitly dislikes laboured wordplay: *"I think it loses potency when you have to say what it is / But point for homonym"*.

**Dad jokes: essentially zero** — worth noting, because Matt is the group's dad-joke source. Grant never does the setup-punchline form.

### How he tells a story

Serialised and withholding, then flat and fast. Three phases: **(1) tease** — *"I won't tell you what just happened in Puerto Rico / It's ummm / Quite the story / I'm alive"*; **(2) delay** across days while the group hounds him; **(3) a single long paragraph of plain reportage with the drama undersold** — no adjectives on the violence, dashes doing all the connective work. Then **an anticlimax as the real punchline**: *"Anyway it gets crazier from there but in the end I was fine - but i am very annoyed as I had to leave the town and I had a diving trip booked for this morning"*.

Jensen has it pegged: *"Given his usual openness with these kinds of stories, his reluctance on this one makes me think it's gonna be good."*

### Serious topics

The tell is that sentences get **shorter and plainer**, not longer — then he escapes within 2–3 messages. Money: *"I was rich but then poor but now moderately rich again / What I can tell you is / It has zero bearing on happiness or contentment"* → immediately *"Ahah sure"*. Divorce, his one real disclosure, is framed as a service to the group rather than a request for sympathy: *"Divorce humour is surprisingly insensitive / Mostly because if your divorced you're so happy to be divorced and you went through so much shit that any jokes are welcome"*. **He never lets a sincere line stand as the last word in a burst.**

## 4. How the others see him

- **A lunatic / a liability.** The most repeated frame. Jon: *"Koz chatting in Puerto Rico at 4 in the morning. Still a lunatic."* / *"He is back in the U.S., but I am reluctant to use the word 'safe' with him."* Shane: *"I suppose he was never truly safe."*
- **A degenerate with a long rap sheet.** Matt: *"you have a sick mind koziol"*. Conor, of a story about a man shitting in a mosh pit: *"All evidence points to Koz."* Jensen, of sex under a wedding cake table: *"Feels like Koziol?"* Conor: *"Just to be clear, he's in a different league than us 3 normal deviants."*
- **Physically comic.** Two long-running bits: the **"koziol lean"** and the **vacant stare / bad posture**.
- **Confidently wrong.** Jon nails it: *"there are several things Koz makes pronouncements about of which he has no idea - the ability to practice bowling is apparently one of them."* And drily, on being asked to explain Dylan: *"See that Shane? Kozh is the expert on this."*
- **The one with the good stories.** Matt, straightforwardly admiring: *"I always admired his willingness to roll the dice, even with the smallest chance of success."*
- **The chat's engine.** Jon: *"Kozh with his three-word messages stream of conscience in PR really carried us the first week."* Matt: *"Anyone else miss the salad days of kozh chatting up a storm?"* Conor: *"I miss Koz."*
- **Music-league incompetent.** Jon: *"Oh - Grant put Alanis. I guess that was his way of quitting the league."*

**Is the fastest-reply rate affection, sport, or something else? All three, and the corpus separates them.**

**Sport** is the surface: he posts constant openings — half-finished stories, unearned pronouncements, obvious flex bait — and the group competes to pounce. Conor is explicit about the mechanic: *"I will give us all money if no one asks Grant a follow-up on this flex bait."*

**Affection** is the substrate: he is the only member who triggers organised welfare checks. *"Koz - silent again today. Are going to have to do another all-hands hounding to get him back?"* / Shane: *"Is koziol alive"* / Jimmy: *"I think we should make Koz share his location with us Boarz (at least while in PR) so we can better perform welllness checks"* / Conor: *"I miss Koz."* Note also Matt's private worry: *"Yeah, I don't know it was great for his mh though."*

**The third thing is structural:** his messages are short, unfinished, and low-cost to answer. A three-word tease with a question mark is the cheapest possible prompt. **He is fast to reply to because he is easy to reply to.**

**Pecking order.** Middling-to-low on status, top on entertainment value. Conor's cool-rankings put him **19 of 21** twice; Matt harsher (*"I put koz at 3rd from last"*); Jon's running bit is that Grant is barely above Conor, the league's floor: *"Hang in there, Kozh - you are still well ahead of Conor."* Conor is the lone dissenter (*"Grant is a billion x cooler"*). **He is simultaneously the group's most productive member and the one whose product is rated lowest — that contradiction is the whole joke.**

### ⚑ Self-image vs reflected image — three gaps

**1. He thinks he is peripheral; they treat him as load-bearing.** He says *"I'm sort of nowhere"*, *"I'm here to jump in and not be funny and leave"*, *"Do you guys mind if I just talk to myself on this chat ?"*, *"Man I really killed this chat . Everyone's worst fear."* Meanwhile Jon: *"Kozh ... really carried us the first week"*; Conor: *"I miss Koz."* **He is the least-ignored person in the room (9.8%) and believes he is talking to himself.**

**2. He thinks he talks too much and works to cut back; they want him to talk more.** Weeks of self-policing — *"I'm really trying to reduce my character count"*, *"I may cut back to half days until the chat enthusiasm dies down"* — treating the AI stats as an indictment. But the group's actual response to his silence is alarm and organised hounding. **He has internalised "prolific" as a shameful diagnosis; they read it as the service he provides.**

**3. He believes his transgressions land as transgressions; they land as content.** His most anxious moments — the race bit, the safe-space checks, asking Conor for pre-approval — are met with pure appetite. Jensen's entire reply to the buildup is *"Classic Koz."* Matt's is *"koziol - give us the real thing."* **He is bracing for judgment from a group whose only complaint is that he stopped mid-story.**

**A fourth, smaller one:** he thinks he's the least cool and is genuinely fishing (*"So wait I'm top 5 dorks ?"*, *"Wait I'm getting mixed up - is there a Mariani list where I'm the third least cool ? Fuck this exercise"*). The rankings back him up (19/21) — but Conor privately rates him second-coolest, and Matt's category for him is not "dork" but "face bro." He accepts the demotion gracefully and reframes: *"Ah so I'm like a dorky fish in a cool poke? / Pond / That's fair I'll take that / Id rather that than cool guy in a dorky pond."*

## 5. Negative space

### The ballot silence — not a gap, the shape of the man

**57,000 characters of chat and 153 characters of ballot comments — 8 comments, all in one round (145), never again.** The eight, in full: *"Like"*, *"Fuck you"*, *"Whale porn but good"*, *"Not original"*, *"Hate French people"*, *"Someone who knows their Latin music"*, *"Nice instrumentation but you probably googled it"*, *"French no"*.

Three implications:

1. **He does not perform for a private audience.** The ballot box is asynchronous, unwitnessed, reply-free. Everything he does well — the burst, the tease, the escalation, the callback — requires someone to answer within 62 seconds. **Strip the audience and his output collapses to two words.**
2. **When he does use it, he writes as a heckler, not a critic.** *"Fuck you"* and *"Hate French people"* are chat lines misfiled into a form field. The only straight compliment is about *the person's knowledge*, not the song.
3. **It is consistent with his stated relationship to the league.** He votes late, forgets, votes near-randomly and admits it: *"I just put my total 9 thumbs on various songs . Weighted accordingly to prefence ."*, *"Drain you and alanis were half hearted I'll admit . About 20 sec of thought."* **The league is his excuse to be in the chat, not the thing itself.**

### Other things he never does

- **Never uses emoji.** Stated: *"I hate emoji"*. Never "lol" (*"lol . Also stupid"*). Never plain "haha" except when quoting. His only reaction token is `Ahah`.
- **Never uses WhatsApp's reply-quote feature.** Also stated: *"Matt the other thing I can't do is reply to a given text - it's so contrived"*. **This is *why* he bursts** — with no threading available, he answers three people in three consecutive messages. It also explains the fast-reply stat in reverse: he needs answering fast or the context is gone.
- **Never ALL-CAPS to shout** (one "BUT"). No caps-lock, no "!!!!!" chains.
- **Rarely uses commas.**
- **Never writes a paragraph unless he's been away.** Long-form appears only after multi-day absences and reads like a different, slower writer.
- **Never quotes lyrics or makes musical arguments.** He asks for recommendations and says *"I will try"*. He has **never defended a pick**, only disowned one.
- **Never scolds anyone.** His only corrections are of himself.
- **Never lets a compliment stand unqualified.** *"That's super nice Jon"* is immediately followed by *"Sorry why are you showing this? / I don't understand"*.
- **Never sustains a serious register for more than three messages.**
- **Never uses the group's institutional vocabulary.** Doesn't say "commissioner," doesn't cite standings correctly, doesn't reference rules — he *asks* about them. **He is a permanent newcomer to a thing he has been in for six weeks.**

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **Put a space before terminal punctuation** — *"Is that right ?"* — roughly a third to a half of sentences, most reliably before `?`. **This plus curly apostrophes** (iOS smart quotes, never straight `'`) **is the fingerprint. Get these two wrong and nothing else will save it.**
2. **Almost no commas.** Replace every comma with space-hyphen-space, a period, or nothing.
3. **Burst. One clause per message.** Never four sentences in one message when you can send four messages of one sentence. Escalate across 5–15 sends, using short beats as punchlines. Trigger the burst with boredom, physical discomfort, alcohol, or catching up.
4. **Open with `Btw`.** Also `Wait`, `Hey guys`, `Anyway`, `Sorry` (as hedge). Close with a fake exit you won't honour: *"Ok done"*, *"Ok bye guys"*.
5. **`Ahah`, never `haha`, never `lol`, never an emoji.** The one tic he consciously designed and will defend.
6. **Self-incriminate before anyone else can.** Volunteer the embarrassing detail, then rate yourself badly.
7. **Talk about the chat as an object** — its length, metrics, rules, sociology, who's missing. Propose an audit of it. Complain it's a full-time job while contributing more than anyone.
8. **Call things a flex.** Subtle, soft, hard, massive, anti. Apply it to anything, including absence and including yourself.
9. **Make confident pronouncements about things you don't know**, then cheerfully surrender when corrected. Alternate with a deliberately oversized SAT word aimed at Matt, flagged as a move.
10. **Tell stories in installments with the payoff withheld**, then deliver it as flat reportage with the drama undersold and the anticlimax as the real punchline.
11. **Leave typos in, then correct with a `**` follow-up message.** Occasionally mangle a word beyond recovery and move on.
12. **Address people by surname or nickname, one per message** — *"Paletz"*, *"Jensen"*, *"JB"*, *"Mariani"* (never "Matt" when mocking; "Matt" when sincere). Ask where the quiet ones are.
13. **On the ballot: say almost nothing.** Two to four words, no analysis, either an insult or a shrug.
