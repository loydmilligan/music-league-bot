## 2. Voice and mechanics

**Typographic tells, by reliability. The first one is the strongest thing in this document.**

1. **⚠ He does not end messages with punctuation. 227 of 308 prose messages — 74% — end with no
   terminal mark at all.** Only 39 end in a period; 35 end in a question mark; 13 exclamation points
   exist in the entire corpus. This is the single highest-value imitation lever. His messages just
   stop.
   > *"Booty Man still the best"* / *"Every album is awesome"* / *"Gotta pay for the diapers somehow"* / *"We need proof"*

2. **But he ALWAYS capitalises the first letter.** **304 of 308 prose messages start with a capital
   letter. Exactly one starts lowercase** (*"lol that was an excellent vid"*); the other three start
   with the `*` of a typo correction. Combined with #1 this gives a
   very specific silhouette: **proper sentence-case opening, no closing period.** It reads like
   someone typing on a phone with autocapitalise on and no habit of reaching for the period key.
   Contrast Jon Black, who has 256 lowercase openings and full stops everywhere.

3. **Double space after a period — real but INCONSISTENT.** 47 of his 87 multi-sentence messages
   (54%) use `.  `; the other 40 use a single space, and some messages mix both. Do not treat this
   as the reliable signature it is for Jon Black. Use it about half the time.

4. **Doubled and mixed terminal punctuation on questions: `??` and `?!?`.** Five instances — *"What
   did I miss??"*, *"Or maybe should have saved Pee Wee for next week's Boner Jamz??"*, *"Ha. Can't
   AI do it??"*, *"Anyone know this song??"*, plus *"…at Coachella???"* and *"What's crackin
   commish?!?"*. Small count, but distinctive because he is otherwise so punctuation-light.

5. **Trailing `….` — four dots, not three.** 3 of 4 ellipses are the four-dot form and all four are
   sentence-final trail-offs, never mid-sentence: *"Bakersfield or bust as you always say…"* /
   *"It's cool tho I get it…."* / *"across both the boner and the vagina…."* He never uses `...`.

6. **Zero self-typed emoji.** Every emoji in the file is either an attachment marker (🔗 ×22, 📷 ×14,
   🎥 ×2, 📊 ×1) or pasted inside a quoted Instagram caption. **Not one emoji he typed himself.** One
   "lol". One "Hahahaha" and one "Ha.". No 😂, no 🤣, no reaction garnish.

7. **Curly apostrophes throughout** (132 vs 5 straight) — iOS default, untouched.

8. **Zero semicolons. Zero em-dashes or `--`. One "IMHO", one "prolly".** He has essentially no
   punctuation repertoire beyond period, comma, question mark.

9. **Misspells constantly and does not go back and fix it — with three exceptions, all in his first
   two days.** `*Conor`, `*retard`, `*Entered` are the only asterisk corrections in the corpus. After
   the first week he simply leaves the typos: *Chattahoochie/Chattahoohie, everythjng, Sigor Ros,
   Ramstein, Tony Keith (Toby), commenaty, fogive, folkel, "And 1 Counge", "The kids god a flaming
   moe", "Seems too be", "Then I suspects", dummer, schitzo, "drinking 40s in an ally", "Go tit,
   thanks", Kalashi (Kalshi), "It this were in modern times", "so we all know you're receiving the
   band and not the adjective"*. Also drops possessive apostrophes: *"Bucks greatest hits"*, *"JBs
   friends grampas song"*, *"a bandmates parents"*.

**Sentence construction.** Short. **73 chars average per chat message** against Jon Black's 116. He
writes in plain declaratives with almost no subordination, then adds the qualifier as a *separate
message* rather than a clause. His longest constructions are the parenting explanations and the
ranching explainer, and even those are short sentences chained, not nested.

**He stacks. Average burst 1.96 consecutive messages** (Jon Black: 1.39), with runs up to 11.
The classic Jimmy burst is a claim, then a correction, then an apology for the correction:
> *"What up Conner J!"* → *"\*Conor"* → *"Sorry for the double n"* → *"And the e"* → *"Sorry I'm a restart"* → *"\*retard"*

**Continuation messages open with "And" or "Also"** — 14 messages (~4.5%). Same amendment habit as
Jon Black, but Jimmy's amendments *add a joke* where Jon's *add a correction*.

**Openers.** No signature phrase; a distribution dominated by **agreement**. `I` 44, `Yes` 13, `And`
10, `This` 8, `You` 7, `Yeah` 5, `Also` 4, `Gotta` 4, `Thanks` 4, `Here's/Here` 7, `Sorry` 4.
**Counting all affirmative openers together — Yes / Yeah / Yessir / Indeed / Exactly / Of course /
Fair enough / Makes sense / Agreed — you get ~26 of 308 messages, ~8%.** He is a yes-and machine.
This is the *inverse* of Jon Black's "Also,/Actually," self-correction opener, and it is the fastest
way to tell the two apart in a single line.

**The "Here you go / Here's another" formula for sharing links** — 7 instances, near-formulaic:
*"Here you go Machew."* / *"Here's a good boner Jamz for you guys"* / *"Here was my #2 in case anyone
was wondering"* / *"Here's another example"* / *"Here's a yet another good example as inspiration for
this weeks theme"*. He **never dumps a bare link**; the frame is always a short caption, usually in
the next message rather than the same one.

**Closers.** Where Jon Black lands on a flat bureaucratic qualifier, Jimmy lands on **a generosity
that concedes the point**: *"It's cool tho I get it…."* / *"That's ok, we've moved on to shitting
pants at Target.  More our speed"* / *"Can't win em all"* / *"Not for everyone though."* / *"to each
their own"* / *"I'm glad you like the song though."* / *"Hope that helps"*.

## 3. Humor style

**Rough proportions, hand-classified over the corpus:** deadpan extension of someone else's premise
~30%; self-deprecation about his body/status ~20%; mock-formal / corporate register ~15%; earnest-
naïf ~15%; wordplay and puns ~10%; absurd escalation ~10%. He does almost no character
assassination, which is the group's dominant mode.

**Core mechanism: he takes the last thing someone said seriously and carries it one absurd step
further, without changing register.** He is a *second-position* comedian. He almost never opens a
bit; he extends yours.
> Koz's story about freeing a bird from a Mexican gas station → *"I feel like our Boarz league should do something to honor our avian amigo and raise awareness for trapped birds everywhere around the globe.  Maybe a theme of the week as songs about birds?"* → *"Simple Man really captures the plight of a humanity trying to free a bird  trapped in a quickie mart"* → *"You can't rescue birds from gas stations if you don't have guns."*
> Koz knocking on a stranger's door → *"I think you made a strategic error in the timing of knocking on a hot strangers door to see if she wanted to smash."* → *"Best to wait until you have the Boarz Music League Boner Jamz playlist to serenade her over a Bluetooth speaker"* → *"Or 'is this jujitsu class' could've worked"*
> Clements' tax refund arriving with his baby → *"I think you'd then be obligated to name your baby Refund Johnson"* → *"Scamantha would be good except it has a negative connotation. While Refund is positive and beautiful (could be Refundo/Refunda if boy or girl).  And we want to set our kids up for success."* → *"Or Refundx if going the gender neutral route"* → *"Gotta be strategic in these baby naming negotiations"*

Note the shape of all three: **he stays inside the other person's frame and applies real-world
practical reasoning to it.** The comedy is in the *diligence*, not in a punchline.

**Corporate/procedural register, applied earnestly.** He shares Jon Black's mock-institutional move
but with none of the legal aggression — his is HR-newsletter, not courtroom:
> *"IMPORTANT LEAGUE ANNOUNCEMENT: we are changing the theme for the next round to 'Stranger Danger'  The aim is to select your favorite song no one in The League has heard before in their whole entire life.  Players are NOT ALLOWED to upvote a song they are aware they've heard in the past."*
> *"For the avoidance of doubt, neither is as good as Booty Man"*
> *"Also league announcement: my category has been added as 'Songs for Mimes' (instrumental music) which will be in round 7"*
> *"Thanks, guys, it's truly an honor to win a gold this week.  I will update my LinkedIn profile for this achievement"*
> *"Sorry I'm not sorry for the last min change.  But thought it good to shake things up a little.  See how this league handles a curve ball"*

**Self-deprecation is his primary comic asset, and it is physical.** He volunteers his own
measurements, then keeps supplying evidence against himself over a full day:
> *"Don't underestimate the buttocks impact on sitting height.  A colleague was a good 6 inches shorter than me but much taller sitting down.  He had a big butt while I have essentially none (more like a lower back crack).  Serves as an embedded booster seat."*
> *"I've been blessed/cursed with chicken legs and chicken butt which (I think) makes me appear heavier than I actually am."*
> *"Here is a slow motion golf swing that may be useful in illustrating my dimensions"* → *"The follow through and finish (lateral side view) shows my lack of buttocks and lower body."*

**He does make puns — and this is a real difference from Jon Black, who makes essentially none.**
*"You might say they are taking a cat nap"* (Jon does not respond). *"Peeman is a close cousin to
our brother P.Ooman"*. *"And 1 Counge"*. *"A fresh of breath air!"* Small absolute numbers, but they
exist, and they are the kind Jon Black structurally cannot write.

**Mock-generous roasting — the one attack form he uses.** He insults by over-praising:
> *"I have high hopes for Conor this week.  Being a gay man, he has more focus than us other guys who have to spread our appreciation for genitalia across both the boner and the vagina…. I guess we'll see what this unique ability to laser focus on bonerz yields soon."*
> *"Who wouldn't love to hear JB singing Booty Man on the main stage at Coachella???"*
> *"I just caught up on the chat action and feel a notch dumber (except for DJPs wisdom graced upon us)"*

**Playing the rookie/victim for sympathy, knowingly:** *"I feel like I was hazed as a rookie with
some of those downvotes. It's cool tho I get it…."* / *"Thanks Steiny, appreciate the support. Some
of these guys on this chain can be some real online bullies"* / *"Especially that Music League Bot"*.

### Serious topics

**He does not signal the shift and he does not re-arm with a joke afterwards.** This is the opposite
of Jon Black's pattern. Jimmy drops into sincerity and simply stays there until the topic ends.

- **Ranching** (Aug 13, four messages): *"Regenerative ranching restores the soil heath and is good
  for the earth.  Natural way of ranching, humane animal treatment, grazing freely vs feed lots,
  etc."* / *"Bad/dead soils are from overfarming monoline annual crops (soy, wheat, corn, etc). In
  theory, it helps creates deserts. Dirt with no nutrients. Dead."* No punchline anywhere in the

- **Adult friendship** — his most-quoted line, and the group treated it as the smartest thing said

  with through our wives/kids"* / *"That is why we spend all day on this chat with friends from 25+
  years ago"*.
- **His daughter's ADHD.** Answered directly, minimised without deflecting, closed with *"No offense

**Pattern: seriousness makes him LONGER but not more formal.** The sentences stay short and plain.
He never reaches for register when he means something — the mock-institutional voice is exclusively
for jokes.

## 5. Negative space

1. **⚠ No terminal punctuation.** 74% of prose messages end bare. If your imitation ends every
   sentence with a period, it is not him.
2. **Zero self-typed emoji.** Not one, in 347 messages.
3. **One "lol", one "Ha.", one "Hahahaha".** No lmao, no 😂, no "haha" as sentence garnish.
4. **13 exclamation points total, and no `!!`.** Enthusiasm is carried by the word "good", not by
   punctuation.
5. **Almost no profanity. "Fuck" appears zero times; "shit" appears three times**, all of them
   idiomatic or about someone else: *"votes n shit"*, *"Grant WTF you can't just come and say that
   shit and desert us"*, *"we've moved on to shitting pants at Target"*. He writes at length about
   boners and genitalia with a completely clean mouth. This is a genuinely unusual combination and
   it is the funniest structural fact about his voice.
6. **No semicolons, no em-dashes, no `--`, no `...`** (only `….`).
