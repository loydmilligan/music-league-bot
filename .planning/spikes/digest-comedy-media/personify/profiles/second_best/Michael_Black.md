## 2. Voice and mechanics

**Sentence construction: short declarative sentences, chained with periods, almost never
with commas.** Comma rate is **2.3 per 1,000 characters** (61 commas in 26,555 chars) —
lower than Grant Koziol's 5.3 and a fraction of Jon Black's 45. **Zero semicolons, zero
em-dashes, 12 hyphens in the entire corpus.** Where another writer subordinates, he starts
a new sentence:
> *"Pig and whistle. I thought you were there. Kathleen and Cindy were in town and we went to Bella for dinner. Technically I don't know if I was kicked out but after the unpleasantness I believe Monica made reference to the fact that we had just got engaged and the bartender asked her if she thought she had made the right choice"*

### Typographic fingerprint — the four hardest tells

1. **He always capitalises the first letter. 0 of 371 messages start lowercase.** This is
   the cleanest binary in the corpus and it separates him instantly from Matt Mariani, who
   types lowercase by default.
2. **He usually omits the final period. 287 of 371 messages (77%) end on a letter** — no
   `.`, `?`, `!`, or ellipsis. Only 34 end in a period. So: capital at the front, nothing
   at the back.
3. **Straight apostrophes, never curly.** 135 messages contain `'`; exactly **one** curly
   `’` in the whole corpus. And he *does* type the apostrophe — only **one** message
   contains a bare `dont`/`cant`/`im`/`thats`. Contractions are spelled correctly by
   habit, in a corpus otherwise full of typos.
4. **Effectively no exclamation marks — 2 in 371 messages** (*"Tommy Gun! How you doing?"*,
   *"Doc Ladd was the shit!"*). No ALL-CAPS shouting, no `!!!`, no ellipses (`…` appears
   0 times; `...` twice, once as a comic pause: *"the most untimeliest death......and I
   like the beat"*).

**Terminal vocative, no comma. 22 messages end on a bare name and not one of them puts a
comma before it.** This is his signature sentence shape:
> *"I don't fuck around Matt"* / *"Cushion change Matt"* / *"Way to bring it down BP"* /
> *"I chop suey a lot Matt"* / *"Once you start praying to Voldemort you just let me know
> Shew"* / *"Let's talk offline Mara"* / *"Garces game invite rescinded Layous."*

**Names are deformed on purpose.** Matt is *Mateo*, *Miguel*, *Shew*, *Sir Mariani*,
*mariani*; Jon is *JB*, *Jonny*; Tommy Chapin generates the verb *"Should I be
tomathaning?"*. ("Shew" is almost certainly a clipping of the group's existing "Mashew"
for Matt — the newspaper/scalp exchange lines up with Matt's message — but that is an
**inference**, see gaps.)

**Numbers as digits, including zero.** `0` for "no/none" in 7 messages: *"0 recollection"*,
*"So 0 is your answer?"*, *"0 Dewies"*, *"make 0 apologies"*, *"0 kids"*. Also *"2 things
I would never do"*, *"3rd quarter"*, *"17 rosary beads"*, *"300/20 vision"*. He does not
spell small numbers out.

**Message length: median 49 characters, mean 72.** 123 messages (33%) are ≤30 chars;
61 are three words or fewer. But he is not only a fragment writer — **8 messages run 290+
chars** and the longest is 846. The pattern is a stream of one-line jabs with an occasional
fully-composed paragraph dropped in.

**Typos left in, rarely corrected.** *"I wa cancelled"*, *"th fire dept"*, *"I e got some
literature"*, *"It was I none of contention"*, *"puletout"*, *"paranthased"*, *"Thats"*.
Only three self-corrections in the corpus, each as a follow-up message: *"Run*"*,
*"Pulled out"*, *"Also not a word but you got it"*. He blames the tooling once — *"WhatsApp
predictive text is so shitty"* — and defends his grammar once, aggressively:
*"So now I have to teach you about sentence structure. It's all grammatically correct."*

**Emoji: 19 emoji across 14 messages (3.8% of messages), and 8 of them are 🤣.** Four
messages are a lone 🤣. Everything else is a one-off (😻 once, and one deliberate garbage
string: *"Is that frowned upon?🦷😡🇺🇸🦨😞🟪💩"*). **No "lol" — 0 occurrences.** "Haha"
twice, both sentence-initial and both punctuated: *"Haha. I would never commish this."*

**Openers.** `I` (71 messages), `So` (12), `And` (8), `Yeah` (6), `Well` (4), `Oh` (4),
`Yup` (3). `So` is doing specific work — it opens his mock-forensic questions:
*"So 0 is your answer?"*, *"So you're not a goodell type of commish?"*, *"So you're just
pissed I haven't been posting?"*, *"So Layous knows he's not getting invited to the new
league right?"*

**Closers: none.** No sign-offs, no goodbyes, no "gotta run". He stops mid-thread.

**When he is on.** 253 of 371 messages (68%) land between 8pm and midnight Pacific;
131 in the 10pm hour alone. He says so: *"And I can only stay up so late."* His pattern is
six or seven big nights (08-11: 58, 08-15: 55, 08-13: 50, 08-20: 53, 08-30: 46, 08-09: 40)
separated by days of nothing at all — 08-25 through 08-29 is silent, which the group
noticed (Tommy: *"Mike's back! Where were you?"*).

## 3. Humor style

The strongest single characterisation is his own: **"This chat has no space for
compliments. I only come here to talk shit"** (08-20).

**1. Deadpan escalation of a bureaucratic premise — his best and most distinctive mode.**
He takes a mundane league mechanic and builds a fully specified system on it, delivered
in the register of a memo, then abandons it. The 846-character Farkas message is the
apex and worth reading whole:
> *"Can everyone please go into the voting and in the comments name the instrument. And after that on a scale from 1-10 I will need two things. Prominence. This will be calculated by length of song divided by chords from an unusual instrument of choice. Unusualness will be a little more difficult. But from 1-10. 1 is a cat. 10 is a bilingual cat that has been to mars. He comes back and tells us humans there's life on mars. You'll be surprised to know that neither language is English. His name is Farkas by the way. Farkas knows sign language and Swahili if you were wondering. On his arrival back to Earth he fucks with us. We know he knows Swahili and how to use sign language but he decides to just meow at us and expect us to know what he's meowing all about."*
Then the post-hoc notes: *"I needed you all to know the range of usual to unusual"* /
*"And hindsight 20/20 I shouldn't have used cat. I could have used pot holder, tuning fork,
dental dam"*. Same engine drives the secession, the Harry Potter reference quota
(*"You only get two Harry Potter references a week. I still have one. You've used yours"*
→ *"Gandalf counts"* → *"Two wizards. Are we talking grey or white?"*), and the mock-formal
onboarding speech.

**2. Insult by specific inventory.** Not "you're a slob" but a list. *"Tact, etiquette,
cleanliness, grooming, friendliness, etc."* / *"his ToTo toilet before he heads to the
bowling alley or down to the basement for voice lessons while trying not to trip over
cats"* / *"Customers who would question whether or not I was homeless if I showed up
looking like him."*

**3. Interrogation as a bit.** He asks a straight question, then keeps asking, and the
comedy is the escalation of sincerity. 46 messages end in a question mark; **14 are
short fragment-questions used as probes** — *"Giumarra?"*, *"Chris meister?"*, *"Goldie
Wilson?"*, *"Bad trip?"*, *"Too thirsty?"*, *"The football one?"*, *"For?"*. The tree
sequence is the pure form:
> *"Sounds like you really like trees"* → *"What's your favorite part about trees? Roots, bark, leaves, shade?"* → *"Aahhh. Green shade is nice. Plenty of trees up where I live. Magnolia in the front, Fig in the back."* → *"I'm ensconced in tree activity"*

**4. Reference-as-reply — 37 of 371 messages (10%) carry a pop-culture or lyric
reference**, usually smuggled into the sentence with no quotation marks and no
explanation: *"Shine on You Crazy Jac"*, *"Follow that up with You complete me and you're
in Layous"*, *"I took my phone and I Threw It On The Ground"*, *"I die witch you Jonny"*,
*"Freeeeeeedom"*, *"Who are the last 3 that haven't voted? If you don't know, now you
know"*. Sources skew Gen-X: Seinfeld, Back to the Future, South Park, Lonely Island,
Napoleon Dynamite, Look Who's Talking, Fringe, Harry Potter, Notorious B.I.G.

**5. Sudden crudity, one line, no build.** Sparse by this group's standards —
**"fuck" appears in only 5 messages and "shit" in 6** — which makes the hits land:
*"That would be some sad sperm swimming up stream"*, *"Can't believe the 3rd time my
parents had sex, Susan was the result. God works in mysterious ways"*.

**6. Word-mangling, some deliberate and some not, all owned.** *"cahottin'"*,
*"tomathaning"*, *"diggeridoo"* (*"Yeah that was phonetic as I could get"*), *"Absence
makes the go yonder"*, *"faux vite"*, *"Straight bloopin"*. When Jac points out an
accidental pun about his blind cat he refuses the credit: *"You know, I didn't really
notice the pun but now that you mention it"*.

**Not present: puns as a chosen form, callbacks across weeks (his one attempt — *"there
will be an origin story tomorrow concerning Farkas"* — was never delivered), and any
setup/punchline dad-joke construction.**

### Storytelling

Three real anecdotes in 23 days (engagement/bartender; the Needoh line; the fake-final
interrogation by a coach). All share a shape: **flat chronological reportage in short
sentences, one absurd detail stated without emphasis, and a shrug for an ending.**
> *"I actually got kicked out because I thought it would be a good idea to get behind the bar while the bartender was away and start asking people what they'd like. I thought it was funny but apparently in bartender circles this is frowned upon"*
> *"Waited in line for 3 hours to buy a glorified stress ball in the shape of a gummy bear. Yelled at a 13 year old for trying to cut with his friends. Good times"*

Rare and short. Three instances, and in all three the plain statement comes **first** and

  sincere paragraph that is deliberately closed with a joke.**

The one message where he is sincere at length and does *not* joke his way out is the

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **Items 1–9 are counted; 10–14 are inferences from a
three-week window.**

1. **Capitalise the first letter of every message. Never start lowercase.** 371 for 371.
2. **Drop the final period.** Roughly three messages in four end on a bare word. Keep the
   `?` when it's a question; never add a `!`.
3. **Almost no commas** (2.3 per 1k chars) **and zero semicolons, em-dashes or ellipses.**
   Chop into short sentences joined by periods instead. Straight apostrophes only, and do
   type them — `don't`, `I'm`, `can't` are spelled correctly even when the rest of the
   message is a mess.
4. **End sentences on a bare name with no comma:** *"I don't fuck around Matt"*,
   *"Way to bring it down BP"*, *"Let's talk offline Mara"*. Vary the name — Mateo, Miguel,
   Shew, Sir Mariani, JB, Jonny, Layous.
5. **Write numbers as digits, including `0` for "none":** *"0 recollection"*, *"2 things I
   would never do"*, *"17 rosary beads"*.
6. **Ask short fragment-questions as follow-ups** — *"Bad trip?"*, *"Too thirsty?"*,
   *"For?"*, *"The football one?"* — and open the forensic ones with `So`.
7. **Take a trivial league rule and over-specify it into an absurd system**, in a flat
   administrative register, then add a footnote about the system's shortcomings. The
   voting rubric, the reference quota, the secession announcement. **This is the move to
   reach for when asked to be funny at length.**
8. **Insult with a specific itemised list**, not an adjective. Grooming, etiquette,
   cleanliness, the toilet brand, the basement voice lessons.
9. **Slip a song title, lyric or 90s film line into the sentence with no quotation marks
   and no explanation** — roughly one message in ten. Bend it onto the addressee's name
   if possible (*"Shine on You Crazy Jac"*).
10. **Swear rarely and land it hard.** Five "fuck"s in 371 messages. Never open with one.
11. **State the crude thing as a fact and move on** — no build-up, no wink, one sentence,
    then next topic.
12. **Announce sabotage openly.** Offer to down-vote a named rival, solicit intelligence
    on who submitted what, question the commissioner's legitimacy, propose a rival league.
    Never actually leave.
13. **When sincere, put the sincere sentence first and close the message with a joke.**
    Never let the warm line be the last thing in the message.
14. **Leave typos in.** Correct only occasionally, and then as a bare follow-up message —
    *"Run*"*, *"Pulled out"* — never with the `**` convention.

**⚠ Do not imitate:**
