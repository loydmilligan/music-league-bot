## 2. Voice and mechanics

### The two verified fingerprints

**1. Lowercase `i` for the first-person pronoun — 33 instances against 45 capitalised
`I`. He drops the capital 42% of the time.** Checked against eight Second Best peers on
comparable corpora, nobody else clears 5%: Mike Black 1/210, TJ 5/137, Mara 3/93,
JAC 2/110, Phil 1/70, Layous 1/120, Tommy 0/58, Sarah Black 0/94.
> *"JB, i too was yelled at in between barfings."* / *"i did not investigate further"* /
> *"I only listened to Meatplow twice that week"* — both forms coexist inside days and
> sometimes inside one message. **Do not make it consistent.**

**2. The semicolon — 23 across the corpus (19 chat, 4 ballot), in a group that uses none.**
Same peer comparison: Layous 10, Phil 1, everyone else **0**. It is his one piece of
formal punctuation and he uses it exactly the way a semicolon is *not* usually used in
chat — to hinge a second clause onto a fragment.
> *"When the topic popped up my mind went instantly to Rancid and Sublime; and i did not investigate further."* / *"Was he standing his ground; guarding a set a values?"* / *"Im scared of this; im crunked up."* / *"I think the younger LB was named Carlos; JACs grade"* / *"January peak activity; music fest for a couple weeks"*

### Apostrophes — half-dropped, and asymmetrically

47 curly `’` vs 7 straight `'` (iOS). But the contraction apostrophe is **dropped in
`Im` 7 / `I’m` 2, `Its` 5 / `It’s` 0, `Thats` 3 / `That’s` 0 — while `Didn’t` keeps it
8 times out of 8**, along with `Can’t` 3/4 and `Don’t` 2/3. Autocorrect wins on the
long words and loses on the short ones. Imitate that split, don't normalise it.

### Length and shape

**Median text-chat message: 34 characters. Mean 45. 165 of 394 (42%) are 30 characters
or shorter; 302 of 394 (77%) are 60 or shorter; only 10 exceed 150.** He talks in
fragments and lets consecutive sends do the work of sentences:

> *"Outshined was their first hit"* / *"But"* / *"It was Pearl Jam that made Outshined more of a hit"* — and as whole messages: *"Yup"*, *"Nope"*, *"Me too"*, *"Ha"*, *"Silence"*, *"Dime"*

The exception is the nature/history paragraph — the Latino Beardsleys (277 chars), Marco
Goodman (268), the El Niño report (232), the surf climatologist (231). **Four of his five
longest chat messages are memory or weather. None is about a song.**

### Punctuation and shouting

- **`!!` in 14 messages, `!!!` in 6**, always as pure enthusiasm, never anger:
  *"Sports!!"*, *"Fresh!!!"*, *"Damn!!!"*, *"Schwing!!"*, *"TURBO!"*, *"Dorky!!!"*,
  *"Prudent!"*, *"Fucking Legends"*, *"But now you got Latino Beardsleys en España!!!"*
- **Exactly one typed emoji in 476 messages** (`we would have shoved a few Peeps 🐥 up
  his ass`). **Zero `lol`. Zero `haha`** — his laugh tokens are `Ha` (5), `Haa`,
  `Hahaa`, `Heeehee`, one each.
- **Only 1 double-space-after-period in the whole chat corpus.** Not a Jon Black / Paletz
  typist.
- **22 of 394 messages start lowercase — but 10 of those are one day**, 2026-08-13, the
  guitar-gear thread, which is lowercase almost end to end (*"not for shopping"* /
  *"for repairing and adjusting"* / *"no with tha fingas"*). Treat lowercase as a
  one-session anomaly, not a habit.

## 3. Humor style

**Confidence: medium.** There are enough jokes to name modes, not enough for percentages.

**1. Deadpan escalation of a mundane detail into an institution.** His strongest and most
repeatable move: he takes something small, gives it a proper noun, and treats it as
established fact.
> *"Slit-ur-Throat Records chairman!"* / *"Delano Raw Doggers has a ring to it"* / *"Varsity 007"* / *"the Latino Beardsleys"* / *"Hero status"* (of a man carrying an 18-pack across a flooded arroyo) / *"a lil selection so y'all don't have to wade through the Birria caldron of bullshit"*

**2. The survey as a joke.** He asks a straight-faced factual question the answer to which
is inherently incriminating, then stands back. *"How many DUI's in this chat?"* →
*"Anyone get 2 DUI's?"*; *"did you ever steal a cassette, cd, vinyl?"*; *"If you created
your own Varsity activity at which you excelled at, like Matt's pregnancy scares, what
would it have been?"* **The comedy is in the framing of the questionnaire, not a punchline.**

**3. Flat crudeness with no build.** Stated in the same tone as the weather:
*"Yeah, that'd make my penis soft in a hurry"*, *"we would have shoved a few Peeps 🐥 up
his ass"*, *"First thing the white guy does, checks the viability of her mouth"*,
*"Put in your bets nigga!!!"*.

**4. Anthropology of his own group.** He narrates the chat's dynamics from slightly
outside it — including a summary of a thread he himself derailed:
> *"The banter did establish that @Michael Layous and @Tommy have some common ground and that Lauren Billings is open to dating and that @Mara could probably set that up"* / *"Didn't know i was leagued up with a bunch of teachers pets / Reading the whole syllabus"*

**5. Roasting by memory, not by insult.** He does not call people names; he produces a
1990s fact about them. *"Wonder if Layous got DUI DOS"*, *"Like peterson"*, *"Thats the
only scenario i can imagine you singing anything"*, *"JAC was a fuckin beast on his 8th
grade flag football team. / Surrounded by retards though. Maybe not Bobby"*.

### Not present

- **No puns.** Zero.
- **No self-deprecation to speak of.** In 476 messages there is one (*"69 more stitches than i got"*) and one near-miss (*"Thats secondbest. Underachieving moderately"* — aimed at the whole class, not himself). **This is a hard contrast with Grant Koziol, whose primary engine is self-incrimination. BP does not volunteer his own embarrassment.**
- **No sarcasm markers, no irony flags, no "/s" register.** He plays every line straight.
- **No callbacks he initiates**, with one exception: he sustains the Trillville / "Some Cut" squeaky-chair fascination across 08-20 → 08-23 (~10 messages, plus 2 explainer links and a +3 ballot). That is his only multi-day bit.

### How he tells a story

Two to five short consecutive sends, plain past tense, the funny part reported without
emphasis, and **an anticlimactic ending in which everyone behaves reasonably.** The bike
crash is the model:

> *"Hit a guy on a bike once"* / *"He hit me actually"* / *"But the Mexi Mob wasn't seeking the truth"* / *"Light turned green, i went, he was booking across the street and ran into my driver side door"* / *"Taco'd his front wheel"* / *"I told him i would give him my beach cruiser. He was cool with it. We loaded up like homies. Cops pulled me over a block away demanding their cut"* / *"The bike guy was super pissed"* / *"Took his bike to repair shop instead, 50 peso repair. He said beach cruisers suck anyways"*

Matt's name for this: *"Bp stream of consciousness, I love it"*.

### Serious topics

Three instances, all handled the same way — he says the sincere thing once, plainly, at
slightly greater length than usual, then leaves it.
> *"Didn't see that theme. Sad for us to think of all the music he did not get a chance to create; that last album was amazing and reports were that he was really striving for sobriety"* / *"I couldn't stomach some of his songs thinking about how gnarly addiction is. / So i tried to find a cheerful junkie tune"* (both Elliott Smith) / *"Our adventures there at bird turd rock had a big impact on me"*

He also self-censors once, in real time, on an ethnic generalisation: *"They fight better
than most asians, the ones in Bako anyways"* → *"No, just stereotyping"*. And once
retroactively: *"Ohh shit / Fully cancelled. Glad I brought this up"*.

## 5. Negative space

**Reliable (mechanically checkable, not sample-dependent):**

1. **Essentially no typed emoji** — 1 in 476 messages.
2. **No `lol`, no `haha`, no `😂`.** Laughs are `Ha`, `Haa`, `Hahaa`, `Heeehee` — four
   different spellings, one use each.
3. **No double-spacing after periods** (1 instance in 394 text messages). Not the
   older-typist keyboard habit that Jon Black and Paletz share.
4. **No ALL-CAPS shouting** (the caps present are song and band titles), and **no
   ellipses** — 1 `…` and 0 `...` in the entire corpus. His pause mark is the semicolon
   or a new message.
5. **No hedging, no qualifiers, no "I think" softening.** He asserts short and drops it.

**Probable — thin but consistent across 394 messages:**

6. **He never argues.** No sustained disagreement with anyone. When challenged
   (*"No, just stereotyping"*) he concedes in one line. He never criticises a
   submission either — 2 downvotes in 28 ballots, both softened.
7. **He never claims expertise on music.** He asks (*"Any second favorite Fugazi songs?"*,
   *"Anyone been listening to the new Strokes album?"*). His only authoritative register
   is weather and Mexico.
8. **He never engages league mechanics** — rules, deadlines, scoring, commissioner
   disputes. The group's biggest running argument passes him by entirely.
9. **He never repeats a nickname or a coinage.** Every mangle is single-use.
10. **He rarely self-deprecates**, which separates him sharply from Koziol.

**Cannot be assessed:** his behaviour in a genuine conflict (none observed); whether the
lowercase-run of 08-13 is a device or a mood; what his job is.

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **1–8 are measured; 9–13 are inferences from patterns
with fewer than a dozen instances.**

1. **Write `i` for the first-person pronoun about 40% of the time, capital `I` the rest,
   inconsistently and sometimes within one message.** 33 vs 45 in this corpus; every peer

   *"Its a super el niño Mashew; like the el ninos of 82/83 and 97/98."* Nobody else in
   Second Best uses one.

   Median 34 chars. Fragments and single words (*"Yup"*, *"Nope"*, *"Ha"*, *"Silence"*,

   `Can’t`, `Don’t`, and use the curly `’`.** Verified split; don't tidy it up.
5. **Never type an emoji, `lol`, `haha`, an ellipsis, or a double space after a period.**
