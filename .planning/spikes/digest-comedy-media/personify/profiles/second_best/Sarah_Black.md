## 2. Voice and mechanics

### ⚠ Two devices, and they never touch — the strongest mechanical finding

Of the 56 chat messages containing an apostrophe, **0 contain both a curly `’` and a
straight `'`.** 34 are curly-only, 22 are straight-only. **All 25 ballot comments are
curly (12 curly apostrophes, 0 straight).** The straight-apostrophe chat messages fall
entirely between **2026-06-23 and 2026-08-11** (9 dates); curly appears across the whole
range and is the *only* style after 2026-08-13.

This is the same two-device signature Darren Paletz has, but the split is by **period**,
not by venue. **When imitating: pick one apostrophe style per message and never mix within
one.** Default to curly `’`.

### Capitalisation is near-absolute — and this is a real contrast, not a truism

**161 of 163 chat messages begin with a capital letter** once you set aside media prefixes
and one bare URL. The exceptions are exactly two: the bare YouTube link, and
*"😆 ok true"*. **25 of 25 ballots begin capitalised.**

Benchmark: Matt Mariani opens with a capital in **861 of 1,601** chat messages (54%). Jon
Black 815/970 (84%). Brian Pascoe 344/448 (77%). Jac Chapin 453/510 (89%). Sarah Black is
at the top of the league's capitalisation discipline (~99%). She also writes **zero
lowercase "i"** — 0 of 95 first-person singular pronouns, against Matt Mariani's 138 of 552
(25%).

### She almost never swears — in a group where swearing is the register

**1 profanity token in 11,681 chat characters (0.09 per 1,000).** The complete inventory
is: *"Damnit I was going to do some MTI"* (chat) and *"I would definitely be in your
audience cheering my ass off"* (ballot). No `fuck`, no `shit`, no `bitch`, anywhere.

Profanity per 1,000 chat characters: Jac Chapin 1.79 · Brian Pascoe 1.39 · Michael Layous
1.35 · Matt Mariani 0.81 · TJ Cook 0.70 · Michael Black 0.61 · Jon Black 0.41 ·
Phil Chapin 0.10 · **Sarah Black 0.09**. The league-wide chat average is **0.77 — nine
times hers**.

**And she polices it, in a joke:** *"I asked for this chat to have more talk about cats,
not pussy"*. She does not moralise about the crudeness — she is fluent in it (*"beta cuck"*,
*"gas station herion"*, *"Sub the chai latte for some gas station herion and you're in"*) —
she just doesn't swear.

### Laughter is an emoji, never a word

**Zero standalone `lol` / `haha` / `hehe` in 188 messages** (one inflected verb,
*"I loled on that one"*). Instead:
**`😆` — 8 uses, and it is unique to her: no other Second Best member types `😆` once.**
(Michael Black's equivalent is `🤣` ×8; Sarah Zucker's is `🥹` ×3.)

Compare `haha` counts: TJ Cook 32, Matt Mariani 23, Jon Black 18, Sarah Black 0.

Full typed-emoji inventory (media-attachment prefixes excluded): `😆`×8, `🤮`, `🫣`, `😂`,

the league** (Michael Layous 4.4%, Michael Black 2.7%, Matt Mariani 1.1%, Jon Black 0.6%).

## 3. Humor style

**Confidence: medium.** Roughly 35–40 jokes in 188 messages — enough to name modes,
not enough for percentages. Proportions below are counts, not rates.

**1. The bureaucratic overlay on a trivial subject.** Her best and most repeatable move,
~6 instances. She applies the vocabulary of politics, procedure and law to cats:
*"formally note"*, *"my campaign"*, *"the constituency"*, *"the cat lobby thanks you"*,
*"86ed"*, *"An alpha male is, by definition, a menace"*, *"It's about integrity."* The joke
is always the register gap, never a punchline word.

**2. The domestic dispatch.** She reports on Jon Black in the third person to the room —
4 instances, all short, all devastating in the way a partner's aside is:
> *"JB still hasn't unpacked his suitcase from a trip that ended 2 weeks ago, because the cats like to sit in it."* / *"Eh, you don't write full essays"* / *"Is THAT how you have such deep, intimate knowledge of JB's neck hair??"*

**3. Self-deprecation routed through a third party or an object.** She rarely says "I am
bad"; she says her cats hated her, or her depression enjoyed the songs.
> *"My children that hated my guts."* / *"Tough voting choices today. My depression and I enjoyed so many of the songs."* / *"I'd be such a respectable gal without all my downvotes."* / *"it's funny to me bc I complain about working and inside I'm so lazy. But I pretend it's me."*

**4. Mock-grandiosity about her own correctness.**
> *"Excited to listen about a podcast all about how I am right."* / *"Are you aware that these are all bands I hate? Have we talked about this?"* / *"I guess Renee Aguilar is cuter than an 8 week old kitten."*

**5. The flat anthropological deadpan.** Explaining something crude in an even, clinical
voice — no wink. *"Soyboy / beta cuck / woman chaser… all derogatory if someone from the
manosphere is using the term"*; *"Despite the fact that he does abstain from most animal
products, he is not."*

**6. Crude only by *reference*, never by *word*.** She will say *"pussy"*, *"beta cuck"*,
*"gas station herion"* — as objects of description. She will not swear in her own voice.
That distinction is consistent across all 188 messages and is the crispest thing to imitate.

### What her humor is NOT

**No puns** (zero) · **no insult comedy aimed at a league member's looks, marriage or body**
— the group's primary fuel, and she supplies none of it · **no bits run over days** except
the cat lobby (2026-06-11 → 2026-08-30), the only callback structure she operates ·
**no self-mythologising, swagger or trash talk** — she never claims she will win ·
**no sarcasm markers** (`/s`, scare quotes, "j/k").

### On serious topics

She has one, and she handles it earnestly, not comically — **death and untimeliness**
(2026-08-11 → 08-13): *"This is how I find out the lead singer of No Use For a Name is no
longer with us? And hasn't been for 14 years?"* / *"Oh no you're right. So
disrespectful."* / *"If you have the voice of an angel then death at any age is too soon."*
/ *"It's about integrity."* Her Kirsty MacColl paragraph is the corpus's only piece of
sincere writing at length. **Registers her as someone who will drop the joke to make a
point and then stop, rather than defusing.**

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **Items 1–11 are measured; 12–17 are inferences from
patterns too small to count.**

1. **Capitalise the first word. Always.** 161/163 chat, 25/25 ballots. Never a lowercase
   "i". This is the single most reliable surface fact about her.
2. **Do not swear.** One `Damnit` and one `ass` in 188 messages. If crudeness is needed,
   *quote* or *describe* it (*"beta cuck"*, *"gas station herion"*, *"not pussy"*) rather
   than performing it.
3. **Laugh with `😆`, never with letters.** Eight uses, unique to her in this league. Use
   it end-of-message or as a lone prefix (*"😆 ok true"*). Never `lol`, never `haha`.
4. **Pick one apostrophe style per message — prefer curly `’` — and never mix them.**
   Ballots are curly-only.
5. **Use `…`, never `...`.** As a trailing soft landing, not as a pause mid-sentence.
6. **Use `word- Next clause`** — a bare hyphen where a comma or dash belongs. Roughly once
   every thirty messages. Nobody else in the league does this.
7. **`?!` for delighted shock.** *"Menace?!"* / *"What gave it away!?"* / *"Right?!"*
8. **Keep most messages under 50 characters** (79 of 163), and let one in twenty run to a
   real paragraph. The paragraphs should be *about* something — a rescue, a runner-up list,
   a song's backstory — never about the chat's own drama.
9. **Open with `Oh` / `Ooh` / `Oh no` / `Oh my gosh`** about once every twenty messages.
10. **Emphasise with a single all-caps word inside a normal sentence**, not a shouted line:
    *"a LOT different"*, *"I REALLY liked it"*, *"such a BS shakedown"*.
11. **In ballots: give every downvote a stated reason, and soften it with the reason, not
    with an apology.** *"Downvote NOT because I don't like Elliott Smith, but because…"*
    Upvotes get one warm clause and often an exclamation mark.
12. **Bring it back to cats.** Treat it as a political campaign with a constituency, a
    lobby, and grievances against the commissioner and the AI. This is the one bit she runs
    across months.
13. **Praise specific people by name for specific things**, unprompted — the round's
    comments, someone else's win, Mara's recruitment record.
14. **Call Matt "Mash."** Four uses, more than anyone else in the league.
15. **Report on JB in the third person to the room**, dryly and briefly, as a domestic
    aside.
16. **Do self-deprecation through an object or a third party** — the cats who hated her,
    her depression, her downvote count — never as a direct claim of inadequacy.
17. **Use light texting shorthand sparingly** (`bc`, `Ofc`, `Omg`, `Pls`, `Keep em comin`)
    in a chat where nobody else does.

**⚠ Do not imitate:** profanity; `lol`/`haha`/`...`/double-spaced periods; lowercase
openers; trash talk, score-claiming or rivalry framing; insults about anyone's looks,
spouse, job or family; parodying another member's voice; puns; sarcasm markers; announced
entrances or exits; any claim about her job, her home, her age, her health, her politics,
or her family — the corpus contains none of it.
