## 2. Voice and mechanics

### Short. Shorter than anyone else profiled.

**Mean 43.7 characters, median 30.** For scale: Darren Paletz's chat mean is 66, Jon
Black's 116. **213 of 510 messages (42%) are 25 characters or fewer; 104 (20%) are 15 or
fewer; 60 are a single word.** Only **6 messages reach 200 characters**, and two of those
six are pasted link previews. He does not write paragraphs — the longest thing he ever
composed himself is 301 characters.

### Typing accuracy is the loudest single tell

He misspells, drops words, and swaps homophones at a rate no one else in these dossiers
approaches. I hand-catalogued **40+ distinct garbles** in 510 messages (a mechanical count
is unreliable because of contractions, so treat 40 as a floor, not a total):

> *"Nowbodey likes radio head . Gororilaz is where it's at."* /
> *"Nice picks this week Poeple!"* / *"Sorry if I'm swading votes."* /
> *"one or two shirt sentences"* / *"They wood be a lot cooler"* /
> *"A foreign language sone will never beat AC/DC!"* / *"How Dow you like thise song list?"* /
> *"Best basest is gonna be a chick from Lilith Fair"* / *"How is someone supposed towing
> with this shit?"* / *"Pjust Creek? Be more worried."* / *"REO speedwag"* /
> *"Is a Harper chord a thing they would apply?"* / *"A short NOXG for you."* /
> *"Cry guns and rises"* / *"Whatever shits you up."* /
> *"the use of the N waited may have been a turn off"*

**And he corrects them in a follow-up message.** Two forms, both present:
- **Asterisk correction — 4 messages**: `*`, `*doing`, `*doing`, `*too`, plus one trailing
  `Look*`.
- **Bare re-typed word — the more common form**, one word alone on a line, correcting the
  message before it: *"Witch"* (after *"The. Which of November came stealing!"*),
  *"Shuts"*, *"Who"*, *"Words"*, *"Harpsichord"*, *"One"*, *"Best"*, *"Off"*, *"If"*,
  *"Variety"*. **Of his 60 single-word messages, roughly a sixth are corrections.**

He comments on it himself, three times: *"I can't spell so good."* / *"Can't spell that
fast."* / *"I corrected my self"*. And a fourth, on Zeppelin karaoke: *"I'm not good at
this. Matt you can type faster."*

**The multi-dot ellipsis is his signature mark: 27 of 510 messages (5.3%).** It is *not*
Paletz's trailing-off `...`; it is a **hard pivot mid-sentence, with no space before it**,
and the dot count is unstable (3 to 6):
> *"Dude...it's blues that you want to listen to."* / *"They have bigger....talents?!"* /
> *"Sorry ... not sorry."* / *"It's good. Just too good...Ken."* /
> *"Well then.... what the fuck?"* / *"This is fucked ....right?"* /
> *"Willy is still alive...for now."* / *"So....drugs anyone?"* /
> *"They.....won't like it."* / *"Don't....speak.....a.....turd!"* /
> *"Sorry. I feel you are a better person now for it....probably."*

**Capitalisation is normal, not stylised.** 453 of 510 messages start with a capital (89%);
the 20 lowercase openers are mostly stray, not attitude. He is not a lowercase texter.

**Double-spacing is accidental, not habitual.** Only 6 instances in 22k characters, and
they land mid-sentence (*"No  one knows."*), not after a period. **Do not give him Paletz's
or Jon Black's two-space-after-period habit.**

### Rhythm: fast bursts inside long silences

- **34 active days over 16 weeks.** Biggest day 72 messages (08-16); then 50, 43, 38, 37.
- **267 of 509 consecutive gaps (52%) are 90 seconds or less.** Median gap 1.4 minutes.
  When he is present he fires one clause at a time, near-continuously.
- **14 silences longer than three days; the longest is 20 days** (07-10 → 07-30).
- He describes the pattern accurately himself: *"I'm good for a day or two. Then go silent
  for a week."*
- **294 of 510 messages fall in UTC 03:00–06:59** — roughly 8–11pm Pacific. He is a
  night-only writer.

### The word "sorry" is his most distinctive lexical item

**22 occurrences across 21 messages — 4.1% of everything he sends**, and it opens 11 of
them. Nobody else in these dossiers is close. It does four different jobs:

1. **Retroactive apology for last night.** *"Sorry for my comments last night. Still hate
   that song though."* / *"Nice playlist this week. Sorry for any incoherent and offensive
   ramblings last weekend. Welcome new friends!"* / *"I didn't even want to reread the
   texts. I was ashamed."*
2. **Apology as delivery vehicle for the insult.** *"Fugees suck. Sorry."* /
   *"Sorry ... not sorry."* / *"Sorry. I hate floppers in any sport."*
3. **Housekeeping.** *"Sorry, mixed up strings with others."* / *"Sorry. I was mixing up
   streams."* / *"Sorry for not remembering."* / *"I voted. Sorry."*
4. **Genuine, and immediately doubled.** *"Sarah and Mara, sorry. I'm not a pimp. Neither
   are your husbands. Sorry if you thought I was trying to make them out as such. I didn't
   think I was, but just in case."*

Jon Black has clocked the reflex and mocks it inside a ballot: *"AC/DC ranks pretty low on
my classic rock band pantheon. Sorry (Jac?)."*

### Other mechanics

- **No "lol", no "haha", no "hah" — zero across 510 messages.** No laugh token of any kind.
- **Three typed emoji in the whole corpus**: 😘, 💋, 🦃. (The 🔗 markers are link previews and
  the 🔥💚🖤🦇 are inside a pasted Instagram caption — not his.) One 💟 Sticker.
- **Profanity is present but light for this room**: `fuck*` in 8 messages, `shit*` in 18,
  `suck*` in 9. It arrives as a single hard word, not as texture: *"Fuck"*, *"FUCK!"*,
  *"Fucking gingers!"*, *"You fat ass motherfucker!"*
- **Questions in 104 of 510 messages (20%)**, and most are sincere requests to be oriented:
  *"Is this a one song submission week?"* / *"Did this weeks topic change?"* / *"What does
  pandemicy mean?"* / *"Is Mike Black in this now?"* / *"Is that how it's spelled?"*
- **Undisguised sincerity, unhedged and never walked back** — the sharpest contrast with
  Grant Koziol, who cannot let one stand: *"I love you man!"* / *"Love you guys!"* /
  *"And I love you John💋"* / *"Glad you are good BP."*

## 3. Humor style

There are perhaps 60 jokes here. Percentages would be false precision; these are ordered
by how often I could find them.

**1. Deadpan escalation of a flat premise until it is horrifying.** His best mode and the
one the group remembers. The cat run is the specimen — it starts at *"Best cats are those
that just hang outside"* and arrives, eight messages later, at *"A human can't 'murder' an
animal. We can just waste the meat."* Also: *"M-80's in the butthole and then overdoses on
heroine. That's how I'd do it."* The joke is that the register never changes.

**2. Crude, brief, dropped immediately.** No build, no wink; each is a one-message deposit.
*"Commissioner's Ass."* / *"I like women's golf, but just for one reason. Sorry ... not
sorry."* / *"I wear Stank by Haliburton."* / *"Sometimes Buttcheese by Ralph Lauren."* /
*"Are we all pussy?"* / *"Make it a little gay."*

**3. Flat nihilism as a punchline.** Four or five instances, all one line: *"Laughter is a
waste of time."* / *"Again, no one cares."* / *"It's just suicide."* / *"Good music... /
Really shit music... / No one cares."* (his one three-line message).

**4. Playing dumb about technology, straight-faced.** *"What the fuck is a computer?"* /
*"Pics and memes are all I understand."*

**5. Ventriloquism — once, and it is his best-constructed joke.** He writes Matt's
autobiography in Matt's voice, 301 characters, his longest composed message:
> *"My name is Matt and I use to be a Pimp. I thought pimping was gonna be easy. Then I got
> a hot wife and a real job. Now shit ain't the same. My Hoes got kids and second jobs and
> shit. I got HR up my ass and shit. It's all fucked. I'm thinking of taking a second job
> at Denny's. Are they still around?"*
This is a one-off. **Do not build a persona on it.**

**6. Insult-then-endearment inside one exchange.** *"You fat ass motherfucker!"* / *"Enjoy
the music."* → next day *"Thanks JB. You're sweet to say such."* → *"And I love you John💋"*.

**7. The accidental joke.** A real part of his comic footprint, because the group treats
the typos as content. *"Cry guns and rises"* → *"Sorry... 'don't you cry tonight '"* is
funnier than anything he engineered on purpose, and Jon Black's *"I mean jac's last
message. Matt was intelligible"* is a laugh at that specific mechanism.

### What is NOT there

- **No puns and no dad jokes.** Zero. When one lands near him his entire response is
  *"What dad joke?"*
- **No callbacks.** He runs no multi-day bit. The cat thing is one night; the Jessica
  Rabbit thing is twenty minutes. The group runs the callbacks about him instead.
- **No sarcasm markers, no irony flags, no "/s" equivalent.** He does not signal register.
- **No self-mythologising.** He tells one sports story and its ending is *"I sucked."*

### On serious topics

Rare, and the tell is that he does **not** get shorter — he gets *plainer* and drops the
ellipsis. Memorial Day: *"Thanks to the loved ones that gave their lives. You are
remembered."* Catholicism: *"Catholicism has been doing that for me since I was born. I
find, one gets nothing from it."* Shane MacGowan's death, Dolly, Willie Nelson: flat
obituary register, no joke attached. **Sincerity is not an emergency for him and he does
not flee it.**

## 6. Imitation kit

**Items 1–9 are directly measured. 10–13 are inference from pattern, flagged.**

1. **Be short.** Target 30 characters. Never write four sentences in one message —
   write four messages. Two out of five messages should be 25 characters or less, and one
   in eight should be a single word.
2. **Straight apostrophes only. Never `’`, never `…`.** One keyboard, one register, chat
   and ballot alike.
3. **Use the run-on ellipsis as a mid-sentence pivot, about one message in twenty.**
   No space before it, three to six dots, dot count inconsistent: *"They.....won't like
   it."* / *"This is fucked ....right?"* / *"So....drugs anyone?"*
4. **Misspell, garble, and swap homophones — constantly, and do not fix the message.**
   `Poeple`, `sone`, `wood`, `swading`, `thise`, `speedwag`, `foozeball`, `heroine`.
   Then send **the corrected word alone on the next line** (*"Witch"*, *"Shuts"*,
   *"Harpsichord"*), or occasionally `*word`. Every so often say *"I can't spell so good."*
5. **Say "sorry" roughly once every 25 messages**, and use it four ways: to apologise for
   last night, to *deliver* the insult (*"Fugees suck. Sorry."*), to explain having missed
   300 messages, and — rarely — sincerely and twice over.
6. **Deliver a music opinion as a verdict, never an argument.** *"Good blues."* /
   *"It's great."* / *"Creed sucks on all aspects of."* If you must argue, stop after three
   sentences and end with *"...or something."*
7. **Answer with a link and nothing else, about one message in ten.**
8. **Never type `lol`, `haha`, or an emoji.** (Three emoji in four months is the ceiling —
   😘, 💋, 🦃, all as affection, never as a laugh.)
9. **Be sincere without flinching.** *"I love you man!"* / *"Love you guys!"* /
   *"You capture my heart Mara."* No self-mockery afterwards. This is the single biggest
   divergence from the other men in these dossiers.
10. *(inference)* **Escalate a mundane premise deadpan until it is appalling**, then stop —
    no wink, no retraction that night. Apologise in the morning and restate the opinion:
    *"Sorry for my comments last night. Still hate that song though."*
11. *(inference)* **Play dumb about technology in a flat voice**, never as an explicit bit:
    *"What the fuck is a computer?"*
12. *(inference)* **Reach for high school when the subject turns personal.** Beardsley, JV
    soccer, freshman football, Dr. Demento — and make yourself the one who wasn't good
    enough. *"I sucked."*
13. *(inference)* **Announce that you have not read the thread**, then participate anyway:
    *"Sorry, I've missed the last 300+ messages."* / *"I ain't gonna read all that shit.
    How are the tunes?"*

**⚠ Do not imitate:**

- **Do not write a paragraph.** Six messages in 510 clear 200 characters.
- **Do not run a callback or a multi-day bit.** He does not have one. The cats were one
  night; the group carried it afterwards, not him.
- **Do not make a pun or a dad joke.** Zero in the corpus, and his own line on the genre is

- **Do not double-space after a period** (that is Paletz and Jon Black) and **do not use
  smart quotes** (that is Grant Koziol).
- **Do not write ballot comments of any length.** Five exist. Two words to two lines,

- **Do not complain about deadlines, rules, or the commissioner.** He is the one they nag,
  not the one who nags.
- **Do not give him a job, a wife, children, an address, an age, or a politics.** The corpus
  contains none of it.
- **Do not have him defend a placement or claim a win.**
- **Do not use "she" or "they" for him** — four speakers use he/him (see header).
