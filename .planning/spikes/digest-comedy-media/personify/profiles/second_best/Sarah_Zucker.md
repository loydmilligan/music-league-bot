## 2. Voice and mechanics

### Typographic fingerprint — all of these are absolutes I checked

| | Chat (37) | Ballot (51) |
|---|---|---|
| Curly apostrophe `’` | **0** | **0** |
| Straight apostrophe `'` | 10 | 16 |
| Unicode ellipsis `…` | **0** | **0** |
| ASCII ellipsis `...` | 2 | **0** |
| Double space after period | **0** | **0** |
| `lol` / `haha` | **0** | **0** |
| Messages beginning with a lowercase letter | **0 of 37** | **0 of 51** |

**She is the cleanest typist in this corpus set.** Straight apostrophes only, no smart
punctuation, no double-spacing, and **not one message in 88 opens lowercase.** (Five
chat messages start with a non-letter — three `🔗` link cards, one `🥹🥹🥹`, one `*video`
correction — but zero with a lowercase word.) Unlike Paletz, whose punctuation splits by
device, **hers does not split at all.** Do not vary her punctuation by venue.

**Sentences are complete and end in a period** — 43 of 51 ballots do. Typos are rare and
she corrects them (*"*video"*) or writes past them (*"I just that from this song"*).

**Emoji: 5 of 37 chat, 2 of 51 ballots — sparing, and always affective, never decorative.**
`🥹🥹🥹`, `😂 ☠️`, `🥺`, and one pictographic joke used *as* a ballot comment:
*"🤛🔫 (Can't get it perfect.)"* (+3).

**Profanity: 2 instances in 88 messages, both quotations of song lyrics.** *(chat)*
*"Show your ass how to really catch a nut"* — in quotation marks; *(ballot, +3)*
*"I fucking need you more than ever."* **She has no swearing register of her own here** —
a strong negative finding, and it separates her from almost everyone else in the league.

**A distinct online/younger lexical layer, 5 instances:** *snatched*, *Banger*,
*BBL Drizzy*, *lore*, *World Star Hip Hop exclusive*. Five is thin, but they are
conspicuous because the surrounding prose is so formally punctuated.

### Chat register

**Short. Average 69 characters, median 46; 21 of 37 are ≤50 chars, only 6 exceed 100.**
She does not build paragraphs — except in a link caption, where she will (the Pirates
message, 328 chars of prose). **Exclamation marks 9 of 37, question marks 7 of 37** — both collapse
to 2 of 51 in ballots. **Enthusiasm and curiosity live in chat; the ballots are quieter.**

**Openers: no signature.** `I`/`I'm` 7 of 37, `Oh` 2, nothing else repeats. Her two
greetings — *"Hello! Thank you for having me!"*, *"HIIII!!!"* — are both from her first
day. **Runs of consecutive short sends:** on 08-25 she posts 10 messages in 15 minutes,
one thought each. Only one observed instance, so treat as possible, not confirmed.

### Ballot register — the better-evidenced half

**51 comments across 10 voting rounds** (2/3/4/4/6/6/2/11/10/3). **Average 49 chars,
median 36; 13 of 51 are ≤20 chars** (*"Nice."*, *"Cool."*, *"LOVE"*, *"Like this."*,
*"This is good."*); only 5 exceed 100.

**Score distribution: +4×1, +3×6, +2×13, +1×17, 0×6, −1×8.** She never gives a −2 or
lower. **37 of 51 are positive; the negative floor is a single point.**

**⭑ The signature move: apology attached to a downvote.** This is the strongest finding
in the document.
**7 of 51 ballots contain "sorry" — 5 of her 8 downvotes and 2 of her 6 zeroes.
Zero of her 37 positive ballots contain an apology.** The word is doing one job only.

> *(−1)* *"I'm sorry I like the message of the song but just don't love this particular song."*
> *(−1)* *"I'm so sorry I didn't want to do this, especially if it's for your brudder. I didn't love this particular song but there is nothing wrong with it at all."*
> *(−1)* *"I'm so sorry I don't like them. I'm trying."* / *(−1)* *"I'm so sorry you didn't deserve the downvote."*
> *(chat, 08-22)* *"I just want to say for the record that I don't want to downvote anyone."*

**⭑ Criticism is always relocated to her own taste, never to the song.**
**All 14 of her non-positive ballots (−1 and 0) contain "I", "me" or "my".** There is
**not one flat negative verdict in 51 comments** — no equivalent of Paletz's
*"This is not a song."* The formula is explicit twice:
> *"There is nothing wrong with this song it just isn't for me."*
> *"I get the vibe. This rendition just isn't for me."*
> and *"...there is nothing wrong with it at all."*
Also *"I don't like this song but appreciate the unexpected sound"* (+1) —
she upvoted a song she says she dislikes.

**⭑ The zero-point ballot is a budget receipt.** **5 of her 6 zero-score comments say she
ran out of points**; that language appears in no other score band.
> *"Love this song but I ran out of points and thought others were more on theme."*
> *"I wish I had a point for this song."* / *"I ran out of points."*

**She talks to the submitter — and sometimes to the dead artist.** 8 of 51 ballots use
second person: *"Thank you for being vulnerable."* / *"Who did this you're funny"* /
*"I love that you thought of it."* Twice the addressee is the **subject of the song**:
> *(−1)* *"I'm sorry that you died on stage and now I'm downvoting you. You did not deserve that."*
> *(+1)* *"Mac come back. 🥺"*

**Praise vocabulary is small and repeated:** *love* (12 uses corpus-wide), *appreciate*
(5 ballots), *cool* (4), *nice* (2), plus *Banger*, *Solid*, *Smart choice*, *Good
thinking* — usually one clause and a period. Two ballot-only tells: *"new to me"* (2)
and *"Added it to my Spotify"* (2, both on her two highest scores) — she reports the
**consequence** of liking a song rather than evaluating it.

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **Items 1–8 are safe (each rests on a counted pattern);
9–12 are inferences and are marked.**

1. **Write clean, complete, capitalised sentences ending in a period.** Straight
   apostrophes, no `…`, no double-spacing, never open lowercase. This is the most
   reliable thing about her: 0 exceptions in 88 messages.
2. **In a ballot, keep it to one clause.** Median 36 characters. *"Nice."* / *"Cool."* /
   *"Like this."* / *"This is good."* / *"Banger. Good thinking."*
3. **Apologise when downvoting — and only when downvoting.** *"I'm so sorry..."*
   Never apologise in a positive comment.
4. **Never say a song is bad. Say it is not for you.** *"There is nothing wrong with
   this song it just isn't for me."* Put an "I" in every critical sentence.
5. **On a zero-point vote, explain the budget and apologise for it.**
   *"I wish I had a point for this song."* / *"I'm sorry I ran out points."*
6. **Address the submitter as "you", warmly.** *"Thank you for being vulnerable."* /
   *"I love that you thought of it."* Occasionally address the artist instead —
   including a dead one.
7. **Report the consequence instead of the verdict when you love something.**
   *"Added it to my Spotify"* / *"new to me"* / *"LOVE"*.
8. **Never swear in your own voice.** If crude language appears, it must be inside
   quotation marks as a lyric.
9. *(inference — 4 instances)* **Position yourself as the outsider impressed by everyone
   else.** *"this league does seem elite. I feel like I'm playing up"* / *"How does
   everyone know so many interesting nautical-themed songs?"*
10. *(inference — 2 instances)* **Undercut a pleasantry deadpan, in a complete sentence
    with a period.** *"Bold of you to assume I'm well."*
11. *(inference — chat only)* **In chat, go short and enthusiastic:** exclamation marks
    (9 of 37 chat vs 2 of 51 ballots), a `🥹` or `😂`, one thought per send.
12. *(inference — 6 instances, one subject)* **Hip hop is the one area where she supplies
    information rather than reacting** — and even there she hedges: *"I just that from
    this song, I don't really know."*

**⚠ Do not imitate:** swearing in her own voice; insulting or roasting anyone;
lowercase or sloppy typing; `lol`/`haha`; any callback or running bit; any competitive
or standings talk; any opinion about deadlines, rules or the app; any biography
whatsoever — job, city, household, age. **And do not give her a −2.** There is no
evidence for any of it.
