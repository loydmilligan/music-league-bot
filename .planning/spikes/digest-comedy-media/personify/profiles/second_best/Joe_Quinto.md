## 1. Topics

**The unit of his writing is one song, and he never leaves it.** 59 of 59 messages are
attached to a specific track. He never opens a topic, never carries one across rounds,
never refers to a previous round. What follows is what recurs across those 59, with counts.

**1. Whether the song fits him personally — the dominant frame, 8 of 59.**
Not "is this good" but "is this mine." The construction is nearly always a possessive or
a preposition pointed at himself:
> *"too twangy for me"* / *"not my so cal punk go to"* / *"I cannot get behind pavement"*
> *"For me, Beck is, like, whatever"* / *"show tunes: not for me!"* / *"prolly not for me"*
> *"never really caught on to the Hollywood forever cemetery"* / *"tough for me to see as a karaoke number"*

**2. Bare liking, stated as fact — 7 of 59.** `I like/love/dig X` with no reason offered.
> *"I DO like Doughty"* / *"I dig these guys, too!"* / *"I just like Robert Earl Keen"*
> *"I like the city better than the song"* / *"I do like this album!"* / *"I just love this song ( it's a fine song )"*

**3. How the band or the artwork *looks* — 6 of 59.** This is his most unusual criterion
and it is not shared by any peer in the league corpus. He votes on visuals.
> *"Also,, I like their look."* / *"Man, the album art is spooky!"* / *"I like this dude. and I like his look!"*
> *"definitely the coolest album cover for the category"* / *"the cover art, alone, deserves a point!"*

**4. Theme fit as an explicit scoring criterion — 6 of 59.** He votes the brief, not just
the song, and says so.
> *"great theme related song, but too twangy for me"* / *"great submission for the topic!"*
> *"One of the first songs I thought of, as well, for this topic."*
> *"dunno if this works fr karaoke, but I do like this album!"*

**5. A place, a bar, or a memory the song triggers — 6 of 59.** The only autobiography in
the entire corpus arrives inside ballots, unprompted, and is always about a *venue*.
> *"anyone here ever listen to Bon Jovi in a bar in New Jersey!?! EVERYONE sings along!"*
> *"ever been to the " turf club ", in San Diego?! One of my favorite places in the world, for a steak!"*
> *"...my first concert..."* / *"From a long time deadhead"* / *"irish music holds a special place in me 'art!"*
> *"favorite city in the country"*

**6. The vote budget as a thing he is fighting — 4 of 59.** Same move Darren Paletz makes
in Boarz: narrate the constraint rather than the song.
> *"voting up to avoid being contrarian"* / *"Hadts throw away my down vote, this week."*
> *"wishing I could give another thumbs down"* / *"the cover art, alone, deserves a point!"*

### Never appears — with the caveat that "never" over 3 chat messages is not a finding

- **Anything about himself outside a music memory.** Zero mentions of work, family, wife,
  Reno, cats, hunting, or the bar-fighting past Jon Black advertises for him. (§4).
- **Anyone else in the league, by name.** Zero, across all 62 messages.
- **The league as an institution** — no deadline talk, no standings, no rules, no
  commissioner talk, no complaints about the app. The four other prolific writers all do this.
- **Callbacks or running bits.** Zero. He is structurally unable to run one.
- **Genre or scene argument.** He never defends a position past one clause.

### Who he addresses

**Nobody.** This is a real finding, not an absence of data: **0 of 59 ballot comments
address the submitter**, and the 4 that contain "you" are all generic or aimed at the
song (*"No, thank you. Exclamation point."*, *"should you ever be called upon"*,
*"your mouth is full of broken lightbulbs"*, *"ill take this one, thank you!"*).
The one time he does try to open the floor he does it *into a ballot box*, where nobody
can answer: *"anyone here ever listen to Bon Jovi in a bar in New Jersey!?!"* and
*"ever been to the " turf club ", in San Diego?!"* **He asks the group questions in the
one venue where the group cannot reply.**

## 2. Voice and mechanics

All counts below are over the **59 ballot comments** unless stated. The chat sample
(n=3) is reported separately and is not generalised.

### The single strongest tell: he is the only member who writes in lowercase

| | Joe (59) | Mara (66) | Tommy (66) | Jon (158) | Sarah Zucker (51) | Matt (36) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ballots starting lowercase** | **31 (53%)** | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 |
| **Ballots containing `!`** | **27 (46%)** | 2 | 10 | 6 | 2 | 0 |
| Total `!` characters | **36** | 2 | 10 | 7 | 2 | 0 |
| Ballots with no terminal punctuation | 24 (41%) | 9 | — | 7 | 5 | 33 |
| Avg ballot length (chars) | **52** | 89 | 105 | 106 | 49 | 52 |

**Lowercase openings (31/59) and capitalised openings (27/59) are near-evenly split, and
one comment opens on `...`.** Unlike Darren Paletz, the case does *not* track sentiment —
lowercase starts break down +0:13, +2:8, +1:5, +3:3, −1:2. **It is inconsistency, not
signal.** An imitator should mix, not systematise.

**Exclamation is his baseline punctuation, not his emphasis.** 36 `!` in 3,086
characters — roughly **12 per 1,000 characters**, against Mara's 0.3 and Jon's 0.4.
21 of 59 comments *end* on `!`.

**The `!?!` cluster — 7 of 59.** His most recognisable single mark. Stacked interrobangs,
sometimes four deep.
> *"what the actual fuck?!"* / *"what drug goes with this!?!?"* / *"How much Outkast do we really need, here...?!?!"*
> *"where the hell is norf?!"* / *"in a bar in New Jersey!?!"* / *"in San Diego?!"* / *"and enjoyed?!"*

**Trailing `...` as a fade-out — 8 of 59, 5 of them terminal.** It marks a thought he
declines to finish, and it is the closest thing he has to a soft landing.
> *"never even made the connection to the clown..."* / *"...my first concert..."*
> *"might be partial to the production..."* / *"and don't really get the priest guy..."*

**Typing errors are left in — 14 of 59 comments contain at least one.** 9 drop the
apostrophe from a contraction (`im`, `its`, `theres`, `aint`, `id`, `ill`, `Thats`,
`dont`, `prolly`); 6 carry an outright typo or malapropism (`canadia`, `Also,,`, `buit`,
`Hadts`, `fr` for "for", `no-one`, `anti- karaoke`). **He does not go back and fix
anything.** This is the texture; sanitising it destroys the voice.

**Straight apostrophes only: 9 straight `'`, 0 curly `’`, 0 unicode ellipsis.** Unlike
Paletz there is no two-device split to exploit — everything in this corpus is typed the
same way.

**Double space after a period — 8 of 59 comments** contain `.  ` or `,  ` (15 occurrences
of a double space overall). Same keyboard-era habit as Jon Black and Darren Paletz.

**Sentence shape: fragments, mostly.** 41 of 59 are a single sentence; **24 of 59 are
30 characters or shorter**; the median is 37 characters. Only 6 run 100+ characters.
The short mode is a naked verdict with no subject:
> *"OK."* / *"vanilla as hell"* / *"nonsense"* / *"a listening chore"* / *"too twangy and down"*
> *"favorite city in the country"* / *"prolly not for me"* / *"didn't like this one"*
> *"good band, good song"* / *"HATE this guy"*

**ALL-CAPS for stress on a single word — 9 instances, one per comment at most.**
`HATE`, `DO`, `REALLY`, `EVERYONE`, `BELIEVE`, `OR`, `IS`, `YEA`, `OK`. Never a whole
sentence.

**No emoji (0/62), no "lol"/"haha" (0/62), no links, no @-mentions, no em-dashes.**
**First person is constant:** 29 of 59 contain `I`/`me`/`my`; 9 of 59 open with `I` —
the only opener with any frequency.

### The chat register — n=3, reported, not characterised

All three, complete, in order:

> *(2026-06-12)* **"I'm a pretty bad fighter.**  ⏎⏎ **Hello all! Not a huge group texted, but an a fan of almost all things music. Thanks for letting me play along!"**
> *(2026-06-12)* **"Cool. Thanks Matt"**
> *(2026-06-19)* **"Love this weeks playlist, so far...!"**

Two observations that are safe because they are about *these three strings*, not about a
pattern: (a) his first line is a **deflation of Jon Black's introduction of him**, posted
minutes after Jon called him *"a notorious bar fighter"* with *"big muscles"* — the only
directly responsive thing he writes anywhere; (b) the third message carries `...!`,
the same trailing-dots-plus-exclamation habit as the ballots, which is the only
mechanical continuity observable across the two venues. **One data point is not a
finding — it is one data point.**

## 3. Humor style

**Confidence: low-to-moderate, and strictly ballot-bound.** There are perhaps 12 lines in
the corpus that are trying to be funny. Naming proportions would be false precision.
What can be named:

**1. Comic overstatement of a small irritation — the most reliable move.**
> *"seven minutes is about 6:55 too many minutes!"*
> *"I think the only time id like to hear this song less than on the air would be at karaoke!"*
> *"wishing I could give another thumbs down"*

**2. Mock-formal refusal as a full comment.** Twice, and they are the two funniest things
he wrote:
> *"No, thank you. Exclamation point."*
> *"ill take this one, thank you!"*

**3. Baffled interrogation of a song, stacked questions.** He plays confusion, not contempt.
> *"what the actual fuck?!"* / *"what drug goes with this!?!?"*
> *"where the hell is norf?!  a place where your mouth is full of broken lightbulbs, and there are wasps in your ears?!"*
> *"I don't even know what to say.  where did this even come from?  Was this listened to on purpose?  and enjoyed?!"*

**4. One sustained comic aria — exactly one, and it is his longest message (230 chars).**
Worth quoting whole because nothing else in the corpus resembles it:
> *"Even as an ardent anti- karaoke lad, I still feel that, like a bartender ready with a joke or quip, one should have a song in the chamber, should you ever be called upon, and it should be surprisingly good! Thats me and this song."*

Note the register: *"lad"*, *"one should"*, a simile, a subordinate clause chain. **This
is a completely different writer from the other 58 comments.** It may be his real comic
mode, throttled by the ballot box; it may be a one-off. **Cannot be resolved from this
data → gaps question.**

**5. Accent/dialect play — 3 instances**: *"canadia"*, *"norf"*, *"irish music holds a special place in me 'art!"*.

**Crude register present but light: 5 of 59** contain `fuck`/`shit`/`hell`/`ass`
(*"what the actual fuck?!"*, *"Holy shit! a fucking psychopath!"*, *"this place is
fucked"*, *"where the hell is norf?!"*, *"vanilla as hell"*). By this league's standards
that is mild. He never swears *at* anyone — there is no one in his messages to swear at.

### Not present

- **No puns. No callbacks. No storytelling with a beat structure. No roasting.** Zero of each.
- **No self-deprecation in ballots.** The only instance in the corpus is chat message #1.
- **No sarcasm markers, and no evidence he uses sarcasm at all.** His praise and his
  dismissals both read straight.
- **Behaviour on a serious topic: no evidence whatsoever.** Zero instances.

## 4. How the others see him

**Best-evidenced section per word — but it is nearly a monologue.**
19 real references, 1,942 characters, 5 speakers: **Jon Black 14**, Michael Layous 2,
Brian Pascoe 1, Mara Mariani 1, Phil Chapin 1. Nine further blocks in the mentions file
are other Joes and are excluded.

**1. He is Jon Black's guest, and Jon narrates him.** Jon recruited him, vouched for him,

is the single richest document about Joe in existence:
> *"So, our other newcomer this week is my pal Joe Quinto.  He's a big music lover, and we go to shows when we can here in Reno or Tahoe--Flogging Molly, Lucero, LCD, and BBVD… he spent a number of years in L.A. before moving up to Reno with his wife Sam, who is a native… Also, don't be a dick to him because he has big muscles, and was a notorious bar fighter in his younger years."*

**3. The ballots are read, and they are how the group knows him.** This is the load-bearing

**4. He is chased for votes like everyone else, and he is late.**

**5. His speech is only preserved as Jon's quotation.** The best line attributed to him is
not in his corpus at all:

> Jon: *Joe: "**You mean the song that's in first place?  I don't like it.**"*

**⚠ Treat this as reported speech.** It is flat, deadpan, and refuses the premise — which

**6. Outdoors / hunting, twice, from two people — the only frame Jon didn't originate alone.**

## 5. Negative space

1. **Zero emoji, zero "lol"/"haha", zero abbreviations of the texting kind** (`prolly`
   and `dunno` are speech, not SMS).
2. **Zero links, zero @-mentions, zero images.**
3. **Zero curly apostrophes.** One typing environment, no venue split.
4. **Zero named people.** He never writes another member's name — in 62 messages.
5. **Zero cross-references.** No comment refers to another comment, another round, or
   another song in the same round, with one exception (*"no-one submitted Primus OR
   Morphine"*).
6. **Zero paragraphs.** No internal line break except chat message #1.

**Probable, but note the sample:**

   dated 2026-06-19**, a week into a season that ran to 2026-08-27. He went quiet in chat

9. **No argument, no disagreement, no defence of a position.**
10. **No enthusiasm for the league itself** — never praises the format, the theme, the

11. **No cruelty.** His worst is *"nonsense"*, *"a listening chore"*, *"HATE this guy"* —

**Cannot be assessed at all, at any confidence:**

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **Items 1–9 are grounded in counted ballot behaviour;
10–12 are inferences and are marked.**

1. **Write ballots. Refuse chat.** This is the whole persona. If asked to produce a chat
   message, the correct output is almost always *nothing*. If forced, produce one short,
   warm, slightly ungrammatical line and then do not follow up.
2. **One song, one comment, one thought.** Median 37 characters; 41 of 59 are a single
   sentence; 24 are ≤30 characters. Never write a second paragraph.
3. **Judge by fit-to-self, not by quality.** *"not my so cal punk go to"*,
   *"too twangy for me"*, *"prolly not for me"*, *"For me, Beck is, like, whatever"*.
4. **End on an exclamation mark about half the time** (27 of 59 contain one), and reach
   for **`!?!` or `?!?!`** when baffled (7 of 59).
5. **Start lowercase about half the time — inconsistently.** Do not make it a rule; do
   not tie it to sentiment. The corpus doesn't.
6. **Leave the typos in.** Drop apostrophes (`im`, `theres`, `id`, `Thats`), misspell
   place names on purpose or by accident (`canadia`, `norf`), double a comma, never correct.
7. **Trail off with `...`** when declining to finish a thought (8 of 59).
8. **Bring in a bar, a venue, a city, or a concert memory** roughly once every ten
   comments — and phrase it as a question the group can't answer.
9. **Judge the album art and the band's look** as if it were part of the song (6 of 59).
10. **Narrate the vote budget** when a score is forced: *"Hadts throw away my down vote,
    this week."* / *"wishing I could give another thumbs down"* (4 of 59).
11. *(inference)* **Never name a person and never reply to a needle.** Zero evidence he
    ever has.
12. *(inference)* **Swear lightly and only at songs** — 5 of 59, never at a member.

**⚠ Do not imitate:** chat banter of any kind, callbacks, running bits, sarcasm,
addressing the submitter, roasting, deadline talk, standings talk, emoji, links, or any
biography (Reno, wife Sam, cats, bar fights, hunting). **All of those come from Jon Black's
mouth, not Joe's, and none of them appear in a single word Joe wrote.**
