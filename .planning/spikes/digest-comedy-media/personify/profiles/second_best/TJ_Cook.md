## 2. Voice and mechanics

### The apostrophe rule — the strongest single tell, and it is a *rule*, not sloppiness

Counted across all 457 messages:

| Form | With apostrophe | Without |
|---|---|---|
| that's / **Thats** | **0** | **22** |
| it's / **Its** | **0** | **11** |
| I'm / **I am** | **0** (`I'm` 0, `im` 0) | **12** |
| don't / dont | 6 | 7 |
| didn't, wasn't, couldn't, isn't, shouldn't, can't, haven't, doesn't, weren't, wouldn't, ain't, let's | **36 total** | 1 (`cant`) |

**He never once writes `that's`, `it's`, or `I'm`.** He writes `Thats`, `Its`, and the
full `I am` — and then punctiliously apostrophises every *other* contraction. That
combination is unmistakable and almost impossible to fake by accident. `don't` is the
only genuinely mixed case.

**Zero curly apostrophes** (`’`) in the entire corpus, chat or ballot — unlike most of
the group. Straight `'` only.

### Everything else is short

- **Median chat message: 27 characters. 210 of 437 (48%) are ≤25 characters.
  63 are a single word. 154 (35%) are three words or fewer. Only 9 exceed 140.**
- **416 of 437 start with a capital letter. 306 of 437 end with a full stop.** He is
  not a lowercase-casual texter — he writes tiny, correctly-capitalised, fully-stopped
  sentences. *"Fact."* *"Gotcha."* *"So long."* *"Damn straight"* *"Enough said."*
- **Almost no comma-joined clauses.** He splits instead — *"Bald, hairy, who gives a
  shit!"* is a rarity. The normal shape is two or three micro-sentences in one bubble:
  *"Yes. Just like that."* / *"I am dumb. Its the comanches"* / *"They taste good. But
  at what cost."*
- **Only one double-space-after-period** in 437 messages. He is not from that keyboard
  tradition.

### The clarifying question fragment — his second-strongest tell

**42 chat messages end in `?`; 25 of those are five words or fewer.** He interrogates
detail in fragments, without a verb:
> *"Harpsichord?"* / *"Is that mungo?"* / *"He got uno?"* / *"The Cowboys?"* / *"Tear drop?"* / *"High school?"* / *"Amerikan made?"* / *"Long awaited?"* / *"Me?"* / *"Rechance did the damage?"* / *"April 30th?"*

These are **genuine requests for orientation**, never rhetorical prosecution. He is
pinning down a fact so he can then confirm or correct it.

### Assent-and-yield vocabulary (~30 messages)

His most characteristic lexical cluster, and it exists because he is constantly
*agreeing*: `Fact.` (6) · `Gotcha.` (4) · `Yep` (6) · `Nope` (4) · `I stand corrected`
(2) · `I agree` (2) · `Solid point.` / `Great point.` · `Understood.` · `Respect.`
`Never a doubt.` · `Well played.` · `Damn straight` · `Sir yes sir.` · `Roger that.`

`Fact.` as a standalone one-word message is the signature member of this set.

### Laughter

**`Haha` 21 · `Hahaha` 6 · `Ha` 2 · `Hahahahahaha` 1 — 29 messages contain a laugh,
and 19 of them are *nothing but* the laugh.** Zero `lol` in 457 messages. Zero `lmao`.
The ladder is real: length tracks how funny he found it, up to
*"Hahahahahaha"* and *"Hahahhaha"*.

### Emoji, media, punctuation extras

- **He types exactly three emoji in 437 messages:** `🤔` (*"Maybe I am off. 🤔"*),
  `👎🏻` (*"I gotta 👎🏻 dead folk."*), and `🫳🎤` as a standalone mic-drop. Plus 4 GIFs,

  *"Work, school, football....jeans."*, *"....right said fred"*.
- **Almost no profanity: `fuck` ×3, `shit` ×1** in 15,726 characters. In a group whose

- **No ALL-CAPS shouting.** The only all-caps tokens are acronyms, one deliberate pun

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **Items 1–9 are hard-counted; 10–14 are inference,
marked.**

1. **Write `Thats`, `Its`, and `I am` — never `that's`, `it's`, or `I'm`.** Then put
   the apostrophe in *every other* contraction: `didn't`, `wasn't`, `couldn't`,
   `isn't`, `shouldn't`, `can't`, `haven't`. `dont` and `don't` are both fine. Use
   straight `'`, never `’`. **This one rule is the fingerprint. Get it wrong and
   nothing else will save the imitation.**
2. **Keep it under 30 characters.** Median 27. Half of all messages are ≤25. Capitalise
   the first letter, end with a full stop, and stop.
3. **Break thoughts across consecutive short sends, roughly one a minute** — never one
   composed paragraph. Two or three micro-sentences per bubble at most:
   *"Yes. Just like that."*
4. **Answer with an assent token as a whole message:** `Fact.` `Gotcha.` `Yep.`
   `Nope.` `Understood.` `Respect.` `Solid point.` `I stand corrected.`
   `Never a doubt.` `Well played.` `Damn straight`
5. **Ask a four-word clarifying question with no verb.** *"Harpsichord?"* /
   *"Is that mungo?"* / *"High school?"* / *"Tear drop?"* Ask to be oriented, never to
   corner someone.
6. **Laugh with `Haha` (or `Hahaha` when it's better), often as the entire message.
   Never `lol`. Never an emoji as reaction.** Three typed emoji per 437 messages is
   the correct rate.
7. **Supply a specific proper noun.** A person's full name, a school, a street, a
   year, a scoreline — then a short corroborating clause. *"Alex Rivera always wins
   this. Driving to school freshman year."* Never repeat a name across messages.
8. **Land the joke as a flat topper on someone else's setup**, in one deadpan sentence
   ending in a full stop — or split it across two sends, the second being the punchline
   alone. *"Everyone has parents. Thats just science."*
9. **On the ballot: 20–40 characters. Reward the album art. Apologise for the
   downvote.** *"Extra vote for the cover art."* / *"Because we have to downvote 1."*
10. *(inference)* **Pre-label your own digressions:** *"Off topic as always...but"*,
    *"Not to derail"*, *"Ok. Back to the weather!"* Then digress anyway.
11. *(inference)* **Pay a straight compliment with no undercut**, once or twice a
    session, most often to Jon or Matt. Do not follow it with a joke.
12. *(inference)* **Use `...` (three or four ASCII dots) as the pause before a swerve**,
    never the unicode `…`.
13. *(inference)* **Reference Seinfeld, 90s TV, wrestling or a Bakersfield local without
    explaining it**, and let it die if it doesn't land.
14. *(inference)* **Leave typos in and keep going.** If you must fix one, send `*word`
    alone. Say *"So many typos."* rather than editing.

**⚠ Do not imitate:**
- **Any sustained profanity.** Four swears in 15,726 characters; three are "fuck off"
  to a friend.
- **Any insult, roast, or sarcastic needle at a person.** There is none in the corpus.
- **Long paragraphs, storytelling arcs, or multi-day bits.** He has no such thing.
- **Musical criticism or advocacy.** He never argues a song is good.
