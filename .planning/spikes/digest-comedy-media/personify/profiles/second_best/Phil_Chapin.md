## 2. Voice and mechanics

### Short, and short in the same way Jac is

**Mean 45.3 characters, median 28.** For scale: Jac 43.7/30, TJ 36.0/27, Matt 54.2/37,
Jon Black 75.5/60, Tommy Chapin 87.0/72. **109 of 230 messages (47%) are 25 characters or
fewer; 21 (9%) are a single word; only 4 clear 200 characters.** He does not write
paragraphs — he writes a paragraph *across* four or five sends.

### The typographic fingerprint — and it is the exact inverse of his brother's

| | Phil (230 chat) | Jac (510 chat) | Tommy (88 chat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curly apostrophe `’` | **80** | **0** | 0 |
| Straight apostrophe `'` | **3** | **143** | 70 |
| Unicode `…` (msgs) | **16** | **0** | 0 |
| ASCII `...` (msgs) | **0** | **27** | 0 |
| Curly double quotes `“ ”` | 3 msgs | 0 | 0 |

**This is the single most reliable discriminator in the corpus.** Phil is iOS smart
punctuation, top to bottom, in chat *and* ballot (8 curly / 0 straight apostrophes in the
14 ballot comments). Jac is straight ASCII, top to bottom. **Two brothers, opposite
keyboards.** The only other unicode-`…` user in the league is Michael Layous, and he runs
it at 115/387 = 30% of messages with stacked `….…` — four times Phil's rate.

**Phil's `…` is a soft trail-off, not Jac's hard mid-sentence pivot** — it sits at the end
of a clause or the end of the message and *softens* it:
> *"Hey, no Bright Eyes…"* / *"Nice… nice…"* / *"PC is my outer name. Hello there…"* /
> *"Three squirts on the chest and never the clothes… it's what draws them in"* /
> *"As do you, Michael Layous… a man of musk"*
Twice it becomes `….` (ellipsis plus a period): *"PC is ok with me…. As are my initials"*.

### Other mechanics, counted

- **Ends without terminal punctuation: 178 of 230 (77%).** Only 25 (11%) end in a period.
  Jac ends 58% in a period, Jon 69%, TJ 70%. **Do not put a full stop on the end of a
  Phil message unless it is a considered one.**
- **Starts with a capital: 213 of 230 (93%). Only 3 messages (1%) start lowercase.** He is
  emphatically *not* a lowercase texter — that is Matt Mariani (41%).
- **Double space after a sentence: 24 instances; 21 of 230 messages (9%) contain a
  double space.** Real but light. Jac has 6 in 22k characters (1%), Jon 20%, Tommy 69%.
  It shows up in his ballots too (3 instances in 14 comments).
- **`?` in only 13 of 230 messages (6%)** — the lowest rate of the eleven measured (Jac 20%,
  Tommy 25%, Mara 23%, TJ 11%). He asks few questions, but two of the thirteen are his
  longest messages in the corpus. **The questions are rare and long; everything else is
  short and declarative.**
- **`!` in 14 of 230 (6%).** Ballots run hotter: 4 of 14 comments carry one.
- **ALL-CAPS shouting: zero.** His six all-caps tokens are `JAC` (×3), `GHS`, `SNL`,
  `GUNSHIP` — names and acronyms, never emphasis.
- **Emoji: one** (🤓, in *"Maybe for Book League…🤓"*). But **two text emoticons**,
  `:)` and `;)` — and he is one of only two people in the league who types them at all
  (Michael Layous, 12; everyone else zero). *"Thank you! Glad it got some votes :)"* /
  *"Already submitted ;)"*.
- **Links: 9 of 230 (4%)**, mostly YouTube/`share.google` — nothing like Jac's 9% link-as-
  speech habit.
- **`lol`: 2 (one bare). `Haha`/`Hah`: 2.** Four laugh tokens total in 230 messages.

### Sentence-initial conjunction — his paragraph glue

**13 of 230 messages (5.7%) begin with `And` or `But`** — 9 `And`, 4 `But`. That `And`
rate (4%) is the highest of the eleven measured; next is Jac at 2%, everyone else ≤2%.
**Honest caveat: 6 of the 13 fall in one five-minute run on 08-23**, but they occur on 8
separate dates. The habit is real: he thinks in one long sentence and breaks it across
sends with a conjunction, so the messages read as a chain rather than a list.
> *"And the shade is nice."* / *"And I like that I dont feel like I'm in a desert"* /
> *"And there's a lot of squerr rolls. And chipmunks. And homeless stooling all around
> them"* / *"And post we shall"* / *"And it was beautiful"* / *"And mean girls."*

> stooling"* / *"Siggestions don't need credits"* / *"Less there's blame"* / *"you're met
> less than a few"* / *"BP and JB get mixed in such sorties eye"* / *"the title of the
> thee"* / *"should t be submitted"* / *"Boat songs are wanted. Ahh idea wanted. As is cool

**Corrections: 5 events in 230 messages, in two forms**, both as a following message —

### Rhythm — a night writer who arrives all at once

- **155 of 230 chat messages (67%) land between 04:00 and 08:59 UTC** — roughly 9pm–2am
  Pacific. He states the cause: *"Always. I work nights"*.
- **29 active days across 15 weeks.** The distribution is brutally uneven: **80 messages on
  08-23**, then 30 (08-13), 22 (08-30), 19 (07-29), 13, 12, 10 — and 22 days in single
  digits. There is a **17-day silence** between 06-30 and 07-17.
- **120 of 229 consecutive gaps (52%) are 90 seconds or less; median gap 1.4 minutes.**
  Identical shape to Jac (52%). When a Chapin is in the room, he is in it continuously.

### The ballot register is the same register

Unlike Darren Paletz, Phil has **no device split**: same curly apostrophes, same
double-spacing, same missing terminal punctuation (8 of 14 comments end unpunctuated),
same first-person voice (11 of 14 use "I"/"my"/"me"). At a **51.5-character mean** he sits
exactly where Matt Mariani does, far below Jon Black (106) and his brother Tommy (108),
and far above Layous (24). **Fourteen comments in four rounds is a middling record, not
Jac's near-silence (5) and not Tommy's 66.**

## 3. Humor style

### The engine: politeness deployed at the wrong altitude

His most repeatable joke is to answer crude or chaotic material in a courtly, mock-formal
register, straight-faced, and then stop. Instances (a hand-list, not a script count — I can
count the words but not the tone):
> *"I beg your pardon"* · *"And post we shall"* · *"Women and children first"* ·
> *"Blessed be"* · *"Sailor talk . Right"* · *"Snaps to whomever put in Fats Domino"* ·
> *"Hail Voldemort"*
The joke is the gap between the diction and the subject — this arrives in the middle of a
cologne-and-body-odour bit and a sea-shanty argument.

### Deadpan advice for a horrifying premise

Second most common. It always takes the *shape* of being helpful:
> *"Give the meth population some help between two stepping songs"*
> *"Buttcheese helps when you want an extra Gordita crunch in the drive thru I hear"*
> *"Three squirts on the chest and never the clothes… it's what draws them in"*
> *"Two leaves ain't dead."* / *"Celebrate them."*

### The earnest question as a delivery vehicle

His signature construction, and unique to him in this league: a genuine, formatted,
paragraph-long request for rules clarification with the joke buried in it and delivered in
the same anxious tone as the real question — *"Just worried about overpaying for a casual
music league…. Less intense than my wordl league… Also do poppers count"*. **The bit is
that he never breaks the frame.** He is still asking a real question at the end.

### He joins bits; he does not start them

Worth stating flatly, because it is the biggest single difference from Grant Koziol and
Jon Black. Checked against the peer corpora:
- **"squinch"** — his ballot *"Imagine the squinching!"* looks like a coinage; it is not.
  Mara, Jon and Matt were all using it in the league's first round, months earlier.
  (Matt later fires it back at *him*: ⊕ *"@⁨~Philip⁩ Am I wrong or do we have a couple
  squinchy/bourbon-offery submissions this round"*.)
- **"Stank" / "Buttcheese"** as cologne names — Jac's bit (*"I wear Stank by Haliburton"*).
  Phil's contributions are *"Stank don't quit!"* and the Gordita line, both amplifications.
- **The Jonas Brothers frame, the googly eyes, the "Displaced"/"Unhoused" pair** — all
  extensions of someone else's opening.
**He has no callback of his own and runs no multi-day bit.** Nothing he starts survives the
night, and he does not try to restart it.

### Self-deprecation: short, immediate, never developed

*"Oh sorry I'm dumb"* / *"I lost"* / *"Yeesh I forgot"* / *"Ermigosh. Sorry Matt."* /
*"You're right, my bad"* / *"Apologies all around."*
**`Sorry` appears in 7 of 230 messages (3%)** and opens 5 of them — the second
highest rate measured after Jac (4%). Tommy Chapin: 0%. Jon Black: 0.4%. **The apology
reflex is shared by two of the three brothers and absent in the third.**

### Where sincerity sits — the inverse of Grant Koziol

Koziol never lets a sincere line stand as the last word; he burns it within three messages.
**Phil puts the disclaimer in front and then lets the sincerity run.** The Sacramento
sequence is the specimen: *"Super gay and over rated…"* is the pre-emptive flinch, and
everything after it is unhedged and unretracted — the delta breeze, the shade, the couch
offer. Same shape on the Muppets: *"It might be the least loved of muppet movies but it
meant something to me"*, and nothing follows it. Same on the group: *"Love yoh bro, no
homo"* — the hedge is *inside* the sentence and the affection is not withdrawn.

## 5. Negative space

**Structural, and safe even at n=230:**

1. **Zero `fuck`. Zero `shit`.** Not once in 230 messages / 10,414 characters. **Only 4
   messages (1.7%) contain any crude word at all**, and the four are *"the Audrey is a
   little bitch"* (a plant), *"Super gay and over rated…"* (trees, immediately reversed),
   *"Stank don't quit!"* and the *"Buttcheese"* line — the last two being echoes of Jac's
   cologne bit. **Comparative: Jac 6% of messages, Layous 7%, Tommy 7%, Michael Black 4%,
   Matt 4%, Jon 2%. In a room where Jon Black writes "Eat shit" and "You're also a POS" and
   Jac writes "You fat ass motherfucker!", Phil is the cleanest male voice in the league by
   a factor of three.**
2. **He never insults a person.** Hand-checked across all 230 messages: there is no
   second-person insult, no mockery of a submission, no complaint about another player, no
   commissioner grievance, no deadline nagging. His only recorded negatives are aimed at a
   coffee chain (*"Ya and the wait is dumb"*), a plant, and himself.
3. **Zero @-mentions in 230 messages.** He is tagged constantly and never tags back.
4. **No ALL-CAPS emphasis, ever.**
5. **No paragraphs.** 4 messages ≥200 characters, and two of them are rules questions.
6. **No callbacks, no running bits, no coinages.** Every recurring phrase in his mouth
   belongs to someone else first.
7. **No pun and no dad joke in the corpus** — the same hole Jac has.
8. **No `haha` habit, no emoji habit.** Four laugh tokens and one emoji in four months.
   Instead: two 1990s text emoticons, `:)` and `;)`.
9. **He never argues.** He states a preference (*"I counter with showtunes"*), concedes
   fast (*"Oh sorry I'm dumb"*, *"You're right, my bad"*, *"I see it now"*), and does not
   return to it. There is no sustained disagreement anywhere in the corpus.
10. **He never defends a placement, never claims a win**, and mentions his own results
    twice — once as surprise (*"I did not expect to place high let alone win"*) and once as
    regret (*"I should've gone with my first intention"*).
11. **He gives an unqualified compliment and does not take it back.** *"Good work to all"*,
    *"This playlist is fun"*, *"I'm very happy with this playlist"*, *"You're always
    welcome"*, *"Love yoh bro"*. This is the Jac-and-Phil signature and the sharpest split
    from Grant Koziol, who cannot let one stand.

**Probable, but the register is narrow:**

12. **No daytime presence.** 67% of messages in a five-hour late-night window.
13. **He does not initiate a thread; he joins one.** Of his 29 active days, I can find no
    day where his first message of the session opens a new subject rather than answering
    one — except the plant advice on 08-13, which Matt solicited.

**Cannot be assessed from this corpus:** how he behaves when genuinely attacked (never
happens); whether he votes in rounds where he leaves no comment; anything about his register
in a one-to-one conversation.

## 6. Imitation kit

**Items 1–11 are directly measured. 12–15 are inference from pattern, flagged.**

1. **Smart punctuation only. Curly `’`, never `'`. Unicode `…`, never `...`.**
   80 curly to 3 straight; 16 `…` messages to 0 `...` messages, in chat *and* ballot.
   **Get this wrong and you have written his brother Jac, who is the exact inverse.**
   This is the fingerprint.
2. **Be short and do not punctuate the end.** Target 28 characters. Nearly half your
   messages under 25 characters; roughly one in ten a single word; **three in four ending
   with no full stop at all.** Capitalise the first letter (93%) — he is not a lowercase
   texter.
3. **Never swear.** Zero `fuck`, zero `shit`. If a crude word is unavoidable, it must be
   somebody else's bit that you are amplifying, and it must be mild: *"Stank don't quit!"*
