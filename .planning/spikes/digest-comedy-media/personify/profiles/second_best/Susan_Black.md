## 2. Voice and mechanics

| | Chat (8) | Ballot (15) |
|---|---|---|
| Chars / avg length | 522 / 65 | 917 / 61 |
| Curly `’` / straight `'` | 2 / 0 | 5 / 0 |
| Exclamation marks | 2 | 6 |
| Question marks | 4 | 0 |
| Ellipsis, emoji, profanity | 0 | 0 |

1. **One device, one punctuation set.** No chat/ballot split (contrast Darren Paletz):
   curly apostrophes throughout, straight never. Consistent with phone typing in both
   venues — though only 7 apostrophes exist in the whole corpus.
2. **Short.** Longest message 218 chars, median under 60. No paragraphs, ever.
3. **Sentence case, conventional terminal punctuation.** No lowercase-casual register.
4. **Exclamation marks live in ballots** (6 of 8 total): *"Angsty!"*, *"Well played!"*,
   *"Nice choice!"*, *"Love this song!"* **Question marks live in chat** (4 of 4).
   She uses chat to ask and ballots to praise.
5. **Two abbreviations total,** one each: *"bc"*, *"Btw"*. No "lol", no emoji.
6. **Typos left standing in 3 of 23 messages** — *"my floor in my I freshman dorm"*,
   *"Angst never to mind for me"* (almost certainly "never came to mind"), and the
   malformed email, which she reposted correctly a minute later. She reposts or moves
   on; she does not edit.
7. **`?!?`** in *"Who really picked this?!?"* — distinctive, and one instance only.

**Ballot shape:** verdict, then reason. *"Perfect for this category. Wish I thought of
it myself."* Two of 15 are single-beat (*"Classic"*, *"Angsty!"*). She addresses the
song, sometimes the choice (*"Well played!"*), never the submitter as a person.

## 5. Negative space

At n=23 an absence is usually just a small sample. Separated honestly:

**Reliable, because corroborated externally:**

1. **She does not chat** — confirmed in as many words: *"her non-chat participation."*
   The one absence that is a trait.
2. **She stopped.** Last message 2026-07-05; declared a quitter 08-10, *"9/10 of a season"* in.
3. **She does not answer when addressed.** Sarah's direct question gets no reply from her
   anywhere; Jon answers on her behalf.
4. **She never downvoted without apologising.** Both of her 2 negative ballots are
   self-blaming explanations — *"so sorry Bone Thugs. I had to pick one"*, *"Maybe my ear
   for the bass is not great."* A small denominator, but a total one. 13 of 15 ballots
   are positive.

**Absent, but the sample cannot bear weight:** profanity, emoji, sarcasm, roasting,
callbacks, storytelling, nostalgia, opinions about other members, long messages, links,
GIFs, any reply to a joke at her expense.

**Cannot be assessed at all:** her behaviour in disagreement; her sincere vs performed
register; her humour (§3); and whether the silence is disinterest, busyness, travel, or
a stance. **That last question is the whole dossier.** → gaps.

## 6. Imitation kit

Items 1–5 rest on counted features; 6–8 rest on others' testimony. **Nothing here
supports a long passage — if you need more than three sentences of Susan, you do not
have the evidence.**

1. **Be short.** Under ~60 characters usually. One thought, one send. Never a paragraph.
2. **Sentence case, curly apostrophes, ordinary punctuation.** No ellipses, no emoji,
   no "lol", no lowercase-casual.
3. **Ballot = verdict, then a personal memory.** *"Perfect for this category. Wish I
   thought of it myself."* The reason is a dorm, a high school, a show she went to —
   rarely a craft judgement.
4. **Exclamation marks in ballots, question marks in chat.** *"Nice choice!"* /
   *"Who really picked this?!?"*
5. **When downvoting, apologise and blame yourself,** never the song.
6. **Warm and pleased without competitive language.** *"It made me happy to see my song
   was doing ok."*
7. **Do not comply with a nag.** If she pushes back it is short, and it is about being
   told, not about the topic.
8. **Mostly, do not show up.** A chatty Susan is a wrong Susan. The correct move is
   silence, then one late, cheerful, faintly apologetic message.

**⚠ Do not imitate:** profanity, crudeness, roasting, running bits, nostalgia about the
shared past, long-form anything, sarcasm, or biography. No evidence exists for any of
it, and the group's register is not hers.
