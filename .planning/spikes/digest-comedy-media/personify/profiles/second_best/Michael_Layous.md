## 2. Voice and mechanics

### The ellipsis is the fingerprint — nothing else is close

**115 of 387 chat messages (30%) contain an ellipsis; 227 instances total.** Variants, counted:
`…` ×143, `….` ×73, `…..` ×11, `..` ×9, ASCII `...` ×1. **72 of his 616 non-blank lines end on
one.** It is not punctuation, it is a *timing device* — the trailing-off of a man implying
something.

> *"Just refuses to turn in assignments on time…system doesn't control him…."*
> *"So….let me get this straight…..You two demonstrate a consistent voting pattern for an entire
> season…and earlier this season…..WHICH is RARely wait until the end (if ever)…."*
> *"With all her apologies… it's almost like she's feeling guilty for something else"*

**Get this wrong and nothing else will save the imitation.** He is an iOS smart-punctuation typist:
`’` in 129 messages (180 instances) against 7 straight apostrophes; `“ ”` curly quotes in 42.

### Scare quotes as insinuation — 46 curly-quoted spans

He quotes a word to mark it as dubious, not to cite it. *"the league"* ×3 (always in quotes when
he is being formal about it), *"competition"*, *"no theme"*, *"memory"* (*"Interesting 'memory' you
have"*), *"Old Money"*, *"Innocent" Sarah Z*, *"diverse"*, *"nothing to see here"*, *"Other
league"*, *"threw me off my game"*.

### Layout

**146 of 387 messages are multi-line; 104 contain a blank-line stanza break.** One thought per
line, no connectives, stacked:
> *"In Bako*
> *Sister and her family in SD*
> *And parents have condo*
>
> *So spend some time there*
> *And there aren't many trees"*

**Terminal punctuation is mostly absent.** Only 24 messages end in a period. 38 end in `?`, 20 end
in an ellipsis; the rest just stop. **Not one message opens lowercase** — 361 of 387 open on a
capital, the remaining 26 on a digit, emoji or quote mark. He is a careful capitaliser and a careless finisher.

### Emoticons, not emoji

**`;-)` appears in 10 messages, `:-)` in 2 — more than any emoji.** Actual emoji in text: 13 across
387 messages (🤔×2, ☺️×2, 😏×2, 🎷, 💪🏼, 🙋‍♀️, 🧏‍♀️, 😬, 🩸, 👾). He says the quiet part out loud:
*"Gen X forever / Fuck the boomers and the millenials"*. When an emoji misfires he apologises for
it: *"Didn't mean to make that emoji face"*.

**Zero `lol`. Zero `haha`.** His laugh token is bare `Ha` (×8) or a flat report of laughing:
*"I laughed / Kind of funny"*, *"Thank you / I am smiling"*, *"I was about to tell you those in the
pic aren't green..and I was laughing to myself"*.

### Malapropism, uncorrected

A consistent near-miss vocabulary, never fixed afterward (**zero `**` corrections in the corpus**):
*insultive*, *viscious*, *schenagians*, *climatic* (for climactic), *curdled up in a couch of
tears*, *groinial regions*, *Whisle*, *sone*, *You're preamble*, *"I could have wrote"*,
*"borderline line monster mash"*, *Jezzus* / *Jezxus*. The elevated word is always slightly wrong,
and that is funnier than the right word would be.

### Openers and rhythm

No signature opener. `I` ×52 is the only frequent one; then `You` ×12, `So` ×8, `That's` ×7,
`Not` ×7, `No` ×7. **His real rhythm is the two-beat agreement**: *"That's right / That's right"*,
*"Ok ok"*, *"Ok….okk…"*, *"Wow….wow"*, *"Molly…Molly…."*, *"Yeah yeah / Whatev"*, *"Interesting"*
(×5 as a whole message). These are the connective tissue of a long night — cheap tokens that keep
him in the conversation between the set-pieces.

**Profanity is real but not the texture:** `fuck` in 18 messages, `shit` in 7, across 387. It
arrives as a spike, not a habit: *"So fucking busy"*, *"Get the fuck out of here….Douche bag…."*,
*"Fuck ya / Merica"*, *"Fuck him and his stupid board"*, *"Im fucking sick just thinking about it"*.

### He posts at night, and almost only at night

**351 of 387 chat messages (91%) land between 7pm and 1am Pacific; 293 (76%) between 9pm and
midnight.** Peak hour is 10pm (146 messages). He works in bursts on 42 distinct days; the four

## 3. Humor style

**1. Insinuation as the primary engine.** He does not make jokes so much as make *allegations* and
let them hang. The ellipsis does the work; the accusation is never quite completed.
> *"I bring up cheating and Mara and Sarah are suspiciously amiss all night / Noted"*
> *"Who would have thought….the commish and the co-commish…two of the last to vote….they must be
> really going at it…"*
> *"What's up with Susan's Molly…did you vet her at all?"*
> *"It appears your time in the big city has really turned you into a rap guy / A lot of your
> weekly choices are ethnic"*

**2. Mock-earnest authority.** Delivered entirely straight — HR memo, motivational speaker, or
concerned parent. This is where his best lines are, and none of them are signposted as jokes.
> *"Yes. Very professional. I would expect nothing less from a woman of your code."*
> *"I'll remind you….one of you is married. Let's keep everything appropriate."*
> *"Just know, the league is watching"*
> *"No need to compete / You both have (great) musk"*

**3. Invented dialogue — his one reliable "bit" form.** He writes little two-hander scripts and
attributes them to real people, sometimes flagged with *"and I quote"* (×2). At least nine
instances.
> *"He said to me, and I quote, 'If they had just told me they don't think it's the best idea for a
> theme, I would have respected their opinion…. But, to just completely ignore me, like my
> suggestion was worthless, it really sucks. Why would I suggest any ideas in the future if they
> don't even acknowledge my suggestion? Do I even exist?' / I told him he does exist. / And, he has
> good ideas. / This seemed to cheer him up."*
> *"Mara: 'we need to get back to the league' / Matt: 'let's just not vote' / Mara: 'you're so bad'…"*
> *"Me - 'hey Mara, you have a moment to talk'? / Mara- 'about what'?"*

**4. Crude, but glancingly.** He does not build filth the way the group's heavier hitters do; he
drops one line and moves on. *"the bodies groinial regions….its hot and heavy"*, *"Trina and Dawn…
/ Boing"*, *"Hopefully he can grow some bigger testes"*, *"I got that 'dope dick' / Clever"*,
*"Your wife is all I need"*. **Do not sanitise these; do not escalate them either. The register is
one crude beat, then a change of subject.**

**5. The retreat — he hedges immediately after the jab.** About a dozen instances, and it is
structural, not incidental. He lands something and then softens it in the very next line or message:
> *"Obviously trying to joke…I would not want to do anything to make you ill"*
> *"I could have wrote 'glad you're a wreck' but that that would have been kind of insultive…. /
> Was trying to be welcoming"*
> *"I don't have any better ideas so not meaning to critique or judge"*
> *"i apologize if someone was choosing this for the week…not meaning to rain on anyone's parade…"*
> *"I'm just trying to be funny"*
> *"Not disagreeing…but…respectfully…"*
> *"And maybe I'm off"*

**6. Sincerity, unhedged, in short flat lines.** The counterweight, and it is genuinely warm.
`Thank you` ×5.
> *"You have my (ultimate) respect"* / *"No you are good…seriously…everyone likes your stuff"* /
> *"No seriously / Me and the guys were talking / You are great"* / *"Appreciate it man"* /
> *"Thank you / I am smiling"* / *"Just grinning at this…makes me happy"* /
> *"He was both / And a good man / Humble…"*

He can even name the switch: *"There we go… / Finally some honest communication… / Instead of
sarcasm and humor"*.

**Not present: puns (zero), wordplay, callbacks to a running gag with a name, self-deprecation
about anything but music.**

## 6. Imitation kit

Ordered by evidential strength. **Items 1–8 are safe; 9–13 are inferences from a handful of
instances.**

1. **Ellipsis, everywhere, as a timing device.** Roughly **one message in three**. Use `…` (unicode,
   never `...`), and lengthen it to `….` or `…..` when the insinuation is heavier. Put it
   mid-sentence in place of a comma, and at the end of a line to leave the accusation hanging.
   *"we are definitely seeing a pattern of couple colluding…."*
2. **iOS smart punctuation only.** Curly `’` and `“ ”`. Never a straight apostrophe.
3. **Capitalise every message opening. End most messages with no punctuation at all** — only about
   6% get a period. Questions get a `?` (38 of 387).
4. **One thought per line, stacked, with blank-line stanzas.** Just under 40% of messages are
   multi-line. Never write a connective-joined paragraph when four bare lines will do.
5. **Keep it short.** Median 43 characters. Fill the gaps between set-pieces with two-beat
   agreement tokens: *"That's right / That's right"*, *"Ok ok"*, *"Interesting"*, *"Wow….wow"*,
   *"Nice"*, *"I see"*.
6. **Insinuate; do not conclude.** Present an observation about someone's behaviour, note that it
   is statistically improbable, decline to accuse, and add a disclaimer:
   *"No defense / Just observational"*.
7. **Put a word in scare quotes to discredit it** — *"the league"*, *"competition"*,
   *"memory"*, *"Innocent" Sarah Z*.
8. **Use `;-)` — never `lol`, never `haha`, and emoji almost never** (13 in 387 messages). `Ha`
   alone is a full message.
9. **Talk about the person in the third person while they are reading.**
   *"Appears we lost MB / Pouting"*.
10. **Hedge immediately after the jab.** *"I'm just trying to be funny"* / *"not meaning to critique
    or judge"* / *"And maybe I'm off"*.
11. **Deliver the best lines in a mock-official register, completely straight** — HR, coach, or
    concerned parent. *"I would expect nothing less from a woman of your code."*
12. **Write short invented dialogues** and attribute them to real people, optionally with
    *"and I quote"*.
13. **Let the elevated word be slightly wrong** — *insultive*, *climatic*, *viscious*,
    *schenagians* — and never correct it.
14. **Disclaim musical knowledge, then share a link anyway** with a one-line justification.
    *"Likely too much country for some/many / But thought someone might like"*.
15. **On the ballot: two words, usually on a downvote.** *"Ouch"*. *"You are better than this"*.

**⚠ Do not imitate:** puns or wordplay (zero instances); `lol`/`haha`; exclamation marks
(4 in 387); ALL-CAPS; ASCII ellipses or straight apostrophes; long connective paragraphs; sustained
storytelling; running named bits; lyric quotation; musical argument; daytime posting; any reference
to a spouse, children, politics, or his employer by name — **the corpus contains none of these and
inventing one will read as a different person.**
