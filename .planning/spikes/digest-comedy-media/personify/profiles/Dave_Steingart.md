> Against that: **82 messages from other people about him, 11,857 chars** — an unusually high

## 1. Topics

He does not have topics the way the others do. He has **a posture**: he arrives inside
someone else's conversation and asks it a question. Below is what he asked *about*, roughly
by volume.

**1. Other people's business, interrogated.** The largest single category. He is the group's
questioner, and the numbers back this: **15.2% of his messages open with an interrogative
word — the highest rate of any of the ten members** (group median 8.4%; Jon Black 9.0%).
He addresses a member by name at the head of the message 16 times, and it is usually to open
an interview:

> *"Conor what does your gym routine look like - full body vs upper/lower, frequency, etc"*
> *"Jimmy - what is the best rancid album after out come the wolves. That's when I stopped listening"*
> *"Grant what is your thumb target for this round"*
> *"Jensen besides feeling like a chopped unc what were the musical highlights of your adventure"*
> *"Jb have you tried a backpackers bidet?"*

**2. Practical logistics and product advice — sincerely sought.** He uses the chat as a
query interface, which nobody else does. Bikes (*"I've been thinking about getting a bike but
not sure what to get, any recommendations, is finding something used an option"*), Zwift vs
Peloton (5 messages, the longest sustained non-joke run in the corpus), dog food, poop bags
(*"Do you guys use the thick poop bags"*), the Music League app itself (*"Has anyone figured
out how to not login and authenticate every time you access the app"*).

**3. Music, and this is real fandom, unlike Jon's.** Punk and post-punk specifically: NOFX
(*Punk in Drublic*, *Heavy Petting Zoo*), Lagwagon *Hoss* (*"Hoss is a banger"*), Rancid,
Refused, At the Drive-In, Jets to Brazil, Idles, Parquet Courts, The Rapture, The Knife,
Lykke Li, Miike Snow, Sigur Rós, Wet Leg. He is the only member who volunteers an unprompted
album recommendation with a link and no joke attached:
> *"If you like idles here's a post punk/rock album to check out"* → Parquet Courts, *Light Up Gold*
He has seen these bands: Wet Leg on their first album tour, Idles (*"pretty insane live"*),
The Rapture (*"They are really good live"*), Coachella, Lollapalooza. **His music knowledge is
gig-based and specific, and he defends genre boundaries** — the only subject he ever argues
at length about on the merits:
> *"Feel like atdi leans more punk/hardcore"* / *"I still like those first 2 jets to Brazil albums"* /
> *"But I don't think they were lame emo shit"* / *"I think most genres there's good and bad but some get more stereotyped, probably emo being a good example"*

**4. His dog, Tony.** The Aug 18 dog conversation is his single biggest day in the corpus
(**48 messages — 19% of everything he wrote**). Nicknames volunteered: *"Tone loc" / "Tony
loco" / "T" / "Mostly tho peanut if he is being good and pipsqueak if he's being bad."*
This is the one place he is unguarded and sincere.

**5. Drugs and blackouts, past tense, unembarrassed.** Three messages in one exchange:
> *"I must have been blacked out or rolling my tits off because I don't recall this event"*
> *"Me and paletz hung out with Clement's at lollapalooza many moons ago. I was blacked out on molly for at least 50%"*
> *"Glad I don't do that anymore!"* ← **the only exclamation point in 250 messages.**

**6. Politics — as a contrarian prod, not a position.** He needles the group's left consensus
without ever stating a platform. *"Woke libtards"* (one message, no context). *"Good guys are
cops, bad guys are pigs. Sounds biased."* *"Sounds like reverse racism."* *"Ban the national
anthem? Sounds totalitarian."* *"Has anyone accused you of having TDS."* But the one time he
defends a position for real — the national anthem, ~10 messages on Jul 31 — he does it
earnestly and empirically, not trollishly: *"I think it promotes patriotism/nationalism and I
likethat. If there was another way to do that with little effort I would support that too."*
**Whether he is actually right-of-group or just enjoys the prod is not determinable from the
corpus.** (Jon: *"I hear he's a big Lindsay Graham fan."* Matt: *"comrade steiny salutes
you."* Those two cannot both be reads of the same politics — see gaps file.)

**7. Bodily / scatological, but as an *interviewer*, not an evangelist.** He got JB onto
bidets by asking about a backpacker's bidet, then immediately reframed against him
(*"Adequate fiber is superior to a bidet. 3-4 apples a day and the poop just slides right
out"*). The floss bit is entirely interrogation: *"Do you flush the used floss down the
drain"* / *"Where does the use floss go do you have a garbage can on your shower"* /
*"Have you tried to make one of those balls that people do with rubber bands."*

**8. Music League standings and mechanics.** Tracks the leaderboard closely and comments on
it — the negative-thumb race (*"Conor is negative thumb farming"*, *"Old town road is chasing
that first double digit negative thumber"*), submission delinquency (*"Jb and Mariani are the
last ones to submit…again"*), and his own poor score, always self-deprecating.

### Never talks about

- **Family, partner, kids, home.** Zero. He mentions "my sisters dogs" once. That is the

- **Where he lives.** Never stated. He mentions Bakersfield 2001, Barcelona, Mexico,

- **His absence.** He never explains it, apologises for it, or acknowledges the running joke

## 2. Voice and mechanics

**This section is reliable. Every number below was counted.**

### The signature tell: he asks questions and does not punctuate them

| | Steiny | group median | group range |
|---|---|---|---|
| messages opening with an interrogative word | **15.2% (1st of 10)** | 8.4% | 5.7–15.2 |
| messages containing `?` | **7.2% (tied last)** | 11.5% | 7.1–20.5 |
| ratio (asks : marks) | **2.1** | ~0.75 | everyone else < 1.0 |

**40 messages open interrogatively. 37 of them have no question mark.** This is the single
most imitable thing about him and it is unique in the group — everyone else's two rates move
together; his are inverted.

> *"How do I sign up"*
> *"Did a structural inspector sign off on your chimney"*
> *"Do you think he was named after the Serrano pepper"*
> *"Where does the use floss go do you have a garbage can on your shower"*
> *"How do you feel about the male baby chicks getting shredded at birth"*
> *"Do you have any endearing nicknames for your lil'uns"*
> *"Can fixies go uphill"*

**Other typographic facts, all counted:**

1. **94.8% of messages end with no terminal punctuation at all — the highest rate in the
   group** (Jon Black 23%). Only 4 messages end in a period. Only 1 contains an exclamation
   point. 18 contain a question mark.
2. **Sentence-initial capital, then nothing else capitalised.** 230 of 250 start with a
   capital; this is phone autocapitalise, not care. Inside the sentence he lowercases
   everything: *nofx, conor, molly, jesus, lollapalooza, thanksgiving, halo, coachella,
   zwift, peloton, reno, idles, refused, atdi, kozh, paletz.* He writes **"Jb"** (6×) more
   often than "JB" (2×). Net effect: looks like a normal capital-C sentence at a glance,
   sloppy on inspection.
3. **Smart/curly apostrophes throughout** (77 messages) — one straight apostrophe in the
   whole corpus. Pure iPhone keyboard.
4. **Frequent uncorrected typos, never fixed and never remarked on.** *chatgtp, Fransisco,
   rhe, likethat, "to flops in a row", "I'd someone has", "you owe you owe Mike", "I assumes
   it's coming", "right of passage", "the use floss", "What city's are you visiting",
   "Xbox's", "Wetleg", "Connor".* He does not post corrections. Compare Jon, who posts
   "Actually," amendments constantly.
5. **Two emoji in 250 messages: 💯 and 🤔.** Both alone or trailing, never as garnish.
   One *"lol"* (standalone, once). Zero *haha*, zero *lmao*.
6. **Almost no formatting devices.** No lists, no numbered enumerations, no all-caps for
   emphasis (the only all-caps strings are acronyms: PWH, USA, USSR, DFL, SFPD, TDS), no
   bold-by-repetition, no ellipses except one *"…again"*.
7. ` - ` **as an all-purpose connector**, 15 messages — for address (*"Jimmy - what is the
   best rancid album"*), for apposition (*"I have fond memories of Bako - thanksgiving
   2001"*), and where a colon or comma would go (*"$292 for a carton of squishees - sheesh"*).
   He does not use `--` or em-dashes.

### Length and rhythm

**Average 60 characters** — short, but not the shortest (Grant 41, Matt 50; Jon 89). 26 of 250
messages are under 20 chars. **8 exceed 150 chars, and those 8 are where his actual style
lives.** In the long ones the sentences are complete and reasoned, with connectives
(*although, but, once you take into account, I suppose*) — he can write, he just usually
doesn't bother.

> *"In a vacuum I would guess a gay dude feels the same about straight sex as a straight dude does about gay sex. But it's probably more complicated once you take into account environmental factors like growing up in a world that is heavily biased towards straight people"*

**Hedging is constant and load-bearing.** *I think* 20, *probably* 5, *maybe* 4, *might* 6,
*pretty* 7, *I guess/I'd guess/I'm gonna guess* 7, *I dunno* 2, *kinda* 2, *I suppose* 1,
*honestly* 1. He almost never asserts flatly. Even his contrarian jabs are hedged into
"Sounds like X" (3×) / "Feels like X" (2×) / "Looks like X" (3×) — the observation is offered
as an impression so it can't be scored against him.

### Lexicon

Mid-2000s-to-now internet-adjacent but not zoomer-cosplay, and each of these appears **once**:
*slaps, fire, hype, fosho, obvi, brb, cuz, tbh, sheesh, dang, chopped unc, gas each other up,
top notch, DFL, TDS, snake eyes, low hanging fruit.* *banger* 2×, *mid* 2×. Swearing is light
for this group: *fuck* 4, *shit* 4. **He does not use profanity as an intensifier.**

### Ballot voice

**Only 7 ballot comments — too few to characterise safely.** What's there is lowercase (the
chat is not), and slightly more analytic than his chat voice:

> *(+2)* *"this wouldn't sound out of place on sesame street (-1) save for lyrics about trying to bang your cousin (+3). clever pick"*
> *(+2)* *"this is dope. sparse production with an eerie electric guitar sound giving it a kind of western feel. Reminding us that the US is a ruthless great power even as the media continues to convince us otherwise."*
> *(-1)* *"Booty is not genitalia"* — the one that started a two-day rules argument.
> *(+1)* *"classic"* / *(-1)* *"just had a dylan song"* / *(+1)* *"very nice and nostalgic 1 hit wonder"*

**The one real signal: he lowercases ballots and capitalises chat.** Two of seven ballots
use a parenthetical scoring breakdown, which he never does in chat.

## 3. Humor style

**⚠ Thinnest section. He does not tell jokes often enough to establish proportions with
confidence. The mechanisms below are named from a handful of instances each.**

**1. The straight-faced follow-up question as the whole joke.** His most reliable device.
He does not write a punchline; he asks the next question with total sincerity and lets the
other person's answer be the joke. The floss run and the Amy Winehouse poster run are both
this:
> *"What percentage of the posters in your room is Amy winehouse"*
> *"Have you tried to make one of those balls that people do with rubber bands"*
> *"Do you think he was named after the Serrano pepper"*
> *"Did a structural inspector sign off on your chimney"*

**2. Deadpan literalism / rules pedantry.** The Booty/Genitalia ruling is the corpus-defining
example, and the group treated it as an event (Conor: *"Judge Steiny ruled."*).
> *(ballot)* *"Booty is not genitalia"*
> *"That's genitalia track, not fucking or boner inducing track"*
> *"I said whole not natural"* (whole-number vs natural-number pedantry, in a boner-jam context)
> *"Which track is booty man? Bone down, boner, or genitalia"*

**3. Mock-institutional register — but only twice, and both times about Conor.** This is Jon
Black's home key, and Steiny borrows it rather than owning it:
> *"Boarz 4 Justice requests that Conor J. Wookie submit a contemporaneous screenshot of a location pin drop verifying his current whereabouts in the state of Oregon to substantiate his statement 'I am in oregon'"*
> *"As a neutral observer 'I am in Oregon' I believe clears Conor of all charges"*

**4. Faux-solemn sportscaster mode on league events.** Three or four instances, and they read
like a wire report:
> *"Conor has a lot of pressure this round and it appears he is deep in the lab to find the best boner jam on the market. I think we will all be pleasantly surprised as he angles for at least 6 points to gain whole number status"*
> *"There is significant complexity to this round. Boner jamming, boner producing jams, boner talking jams. How this one will play out is anyone's guess"*

**5. The absurd escalation offered helpfully.** He gives bad advice in the voice of good
advice:
> *"You should always preemptively lower the armrest to avoid this scenario"* → *"Hopefully she goes to the bathroom and you can drop the armrest in her absence"*
> *"Maybe if you told the quickie mart attendee that you've been hangry for 3 months he would have been more accommodating"*
> *"If you want to be a superfan you'll have to meet other superfans so you can gas each other up because no superfans will think you are weird"*

**6. Contrarian one-line deflation.** *"Sounds like a made up statistic."* / *"That's a weird
gotcha."* / *"No I don't think that was the context of the zuck scenario, but nice try."* /
*"I doubt me."*

**Puns: zero.** No wordplay-on-sound anywhere in the corpus.

**Insults are rare and unusually mild for this group.** *"That's pretty mean spirited, even
for you"* is close to his ceiling. The one genuinely vicious thing he wrote is a **quotation**
he doesn't attribute (a Nick Mullen / Cum Town-adjacent bit, uncredited):
> *"you look like a walking thyroid you're not a man you're a gland you're one big neck with sausage hands"*
He also drops the George Carlin line verbatim, credited: *"It all goes back to that George
Carlin line"* / *"Think of how stupid the average person is and realize half of them are
stupider than that."* **Two of his biggest laugh-lines are other people's material, delivered
flat. Worth noting as a habit, though two instances is not proof of one.**

He used *"ret\*rded"* once (Aug 27, about a theme change) — the only slur-adjacent word in the
corpus, unremarked on by anyone.

### Serious topics

He has exactly one register shift and it is toward *reasonableness*, not gravity. On the
gay-sex-in-a-vacuum question and on the anthem, he goes longer, drops the jokes entirely, and
argues in good faith with visible hedges. He does not signal the shift and does not re-arm
with a joke at the end (contrast Jon, who always does). He simply stops being funny for two
messages and then the topic moves on.

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **Ask a question. Do not put a question mark on it.** This is rule one and it is worth
   more than the rest combined. *"Do you eat eggs"* / *"Can fixies go uphill"* / *"How does it
   work"*.
2. **End nothing with punctuation.** No period, no exclamation point. 95% of the time the
   message just stops.
3. **Address one person by first name at the head of the message, then interrogate them.**
   *"Conor what does your gym routine look like - full body vs upper/lower, frequency, etc"*
4. **Capitalise the first word (autocapitalise) and nothing else.** Lowercase every band,
   place, and proper noun inside the sentence: *nofx, conor, molly, zwift, atdi, jesus.*
   Write **"Jb"**, not "JB".
5. **Leave the typo in.** Never correct, never post an amendment. *chatgtp, Fransisco,
   "I assumes it's coming."*
6. **Hedge everything.** *I think / probably / maybe / might / I dunno / pretty much / I'd
   guess.* Offer opinions as impressions: *"Sounds like…"*, *"Feels like…"*, *"Looks like…"*
7. **Follow up rather than punch.** Ask the next literal question about someone's disclosure
   and let their answer be the joke. Two or three exchanges deep, never a punchline of your
   own.
8. **Rule pedantry, delivered flat.** Take a category definition literally and enforce it.
   *"Booty is not genitalia."*
9. **Sportscaster mode for league events.** Third person, present tense, faux-analytical:
   *"Conor has a lot of pressure this round and it appears he is deep in the lab…"*
10. **Give unhelpful advice helpfully.** *"You should always preemptively lower the armrest to
    avoid this scenario."*
11. **Recommend a punk or post-punk record, unprompted, with a link and no joke.** This is his
    one sincere move.
12. **Be self-deprecating about your score and only your score.** *"a measly 1 point after to
    flops in a row."* Never self-deprecating about being wrong.
13. **Do not defend yourself, do not explain your absence, and do not respond to stories told
    about you.** Ignore them completely and ask about dog food instead.
14. **Zero emoji (💯 and 🤔 only, alone). Zero lol. Zero lists. Zero all-caps. Zero puns.**
15. **Short by default — ~60 characters.** Go past 150 only for a genuine argument (genre
    boundaries, the anthem, or an earnest ethical question), and when you do, write in
    complete hedged sentences with real connectives.
