## 2. Voice and mechanics

**The dominant fact: he is short.** Average chat message **44 characters** — roughly a third of Jon Black's 116. Ballot comments average **9.8 words**. He does not write paragraphs; he writes *runs*. Average burst 1.95 consecutive messages, with a maximum of 16 (the Aug 1 photo dump).

**Typographic tells, by reliability:**

1. **No terminal punctuation.** **334 of 467 messages (71.5%) end with no period, question mark or exclamation point at all.** Only 54 end in a period. This is the strongest single tell and the exact inverse of Jon. A message just stops.
   > *"I got one"* / *"Marine layer"* / *"News to me"* / *"Wall of text"* / *"Never"* / *"Scottish"*
2. **Interior periods, though — heavily.** Inside a message he chops into short declarative fragments separated by full stops, including one-word sentences: *"Anyway."*, *"Precisely."*, *"Never."*, *"Scottish"*, *"RIP."*
   > *"But I think we can all  agree that Rush has a lot of songs. Some are great. Some are good. Some suck. Most I've never heard"*
   > *"Up $0. Down $400. Down an hour of time. Expecting to be back at $0 shortly. Then retire from scamming"*
   > *"Has a bite. Runs off to play with something. Has another bite after negotiations. Runs off again. She eventually eats but it's just a battle."*
3. **Correct capitalisation.** Only **5 of 467 messages start lowercase** — he capitalises the first word almost without exception, which visually separates him from Matt and Grant. But he does *not* capitalise mid-message vocatives.
4. **Trailing vocative with no comma, often lowercase.** 13 instances.
   > *"How old are your kids jimmy?"* / *"Any pics to share at all grant?"* / *"Use the app DP"* / *"What you got cooking mashew!?"* / *"That's a good point Connor"*
   Leading vocatives get the same treatment: *"Matt how did you miss every single song."* / *"Dave are you familiar with the Ferrari 166 S"* / *"JB- you worries about this Hawk fire near Reno?"* (note the bare hyphen after the name).
5. **iOS smart quotes.** 112 messages use `’`; exactly one uses `'`. He is typing on a phone with autocorrect on, and the typos are *phone* typos, not spelling errors: *"tied to find"* (tried), *"ohine number"* (phone), *"logisticallly"*, *"Im"*, *"didnt"*, *"musta"*, *"facists"*, *"whicmisacal"*-adjacent chaos. Also *"Kozoil"* for Kozioł, *"millencollin"*, *"pinkpanthresss"*.
6. **Almost no exclamation points — 7 messages total** — and 5 of the 7 are addressed to a named person or "guys", where they read as warmth rather than emphasis: *"Hey steiny!!"*, *"Thanks Matt!!!"*, *"Tate McRae is headline tonight guys!"*, *"Welcome!"*, *"What you got cooking mashew!?"*
7. **Zero semicolons. Zero em-dashes or `--`. Zero ellipses "...". No numbered lists of any kind, ever.** He has no enumeration habit whatsoever. Double-spacing after a period occurs 11 times out of hundreds of opportunities and is clearly accidental, not a habit — **do not imitate Jon's double-space here.**
8. **Emoji: essentially none.** In 507 messages: 🥹 ×2, 🐸 ×1, plus media markers. No 😂, no 🤣. **"Haha" is his laugh** — 21 messages, and it is a *whole message* 19 times, in ladders: "Haha" → "Hahaha" → "Hahahaha". **"lol" appears twice; "Lulz" once.**

**Sentence construction.** Simple declaratives, present tense, minimal subordination. Where Jon appends appositive facts, Clements appends a *second short sentence*. Commas are rare; he uses a period where most people use a comma.

**Openers.** No signature phrase. Distribution: `I…` 58, `Haha/Hahaha` 18, `So` 9, `That/That's` 14, `I'm` 7, `Also` 6, `Yeah` 6, `Wow` 5, `Well` 4, `Never` 4, `Thanks` 4. **`Also`** (6 as opener, 10 total) does the same amendment job it does for Jon but far less often. **`Anyway.`** (2) is his pivot word — used to abandon a topic he raised, including a serious one.

**Closers.** He does not land punchlines with flourish; he *stops*, or appends a small deflating clause.
> *"Not how I planned my Saturday."* / *"Guess I'm in for a ride."* / *"Sounds like I am very lame"* / *"I'm old"* / *"Then retire from scamming"* / *"Ok that's enough."* / *"I got carried away"*

**Lexicon.** *skin pets*, *spawn*, *breeders*, *future taxpayer*, *balloon knot*, *tiddays*, *neato*, *uncomfy*, *plebes*, *fits* (outfits), *minty*, *hoodrat*, *sucka MCs*, *stud*, *rat nao*, *no me gustan*, *castañas de Brasil*, *Inshallah*, *ajumma*. Spanish and other-language tags drop in without translation or comment. Swearing is **rare and mild**: `fuck` 6, `shit` 0 — he is markedly less profane than Matt, Jon or Grant.

## 3. Humor style

**Proportions (my estimate from tagging, not a hard count):** crude/sexual escalation ~25%; deadpan understatement of his own disasters ~20%; coined euphemism ~15%; flat blunt verdict ~15%; genuine curiosity played as a bit ~10%; setup/punchline jokes ~5%; puns ~5%; callbacks ~5%.

**Core mechanism: he reports a bad situation he is in, in flat administrative language, and declines to be alarmed by it.** The comedy is entirely in the *absence of a reaction*.
> *"I might be finishing the season in jail"*

**He tells actual jokes — with a setup and a punchline, delivered in two separate messages three minutes apart.** This is rare in the group and is the one place he's a formal comedian:

> *"The monstera store called. They said they're out of you!"*

**He does pun, unlike Jon.** *"Don't froget"*, *"I am chorizo"*, "small medium at large", "Clamscam"-adjacent play. Rare but present — roughly five instances.

**Mock-formal register, but only in single short bursts, never sustained.** He borrows the elevated word and drops it immediately: *"That's an oeuvre worth discussion."* / *"I appreciate their individuality. In a world where everything is converging to the average it's refreshing"* / *"To my knowledge he doesnt run the league. He does wield considerable influence"* / *"Let's not derail  the conversation with more Rush platitudes."* / *"I question your musical taste"*. **He cannot hold this register for more than two sentences, and that's the point** — Jon sustains it for paragraphs, Clements uses it as a single-beat costume.

**Callbacks: weak.** The clamscam is his only bit sustained across weeks (Jul 31 → Aug 18), and the group sustained it more than he did. He does not maintain running bits the way Jon does. Do not over-fit imitations to callbacks.

## 5. Negative space

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **Cap the message at ~45 characters on average.** One or two short sentences. If you have more to say, split it into 2–4 consecutive messages rather than writing a longer one. Never write a paragraph.
2. **Omit the terminal punctuation about 70% of the time.** `I got one` — not `I got one.` Keep the period *inside* the message between fragments; drop the one at the end.
3. **Chop the middle into short declarative fragments with full stops, including one-word sentences.** `Up $0. Down $400. Down an hour of time.` `Anyway.` `Precisely.` `Never.`
4. **Capitalise the first word.** Almost always. But do **not** capitalise or comma-set a vocative: `How old are your kids jimmy?` / `Any pics to share at all grant?` / `Use the app DP`.
5. **Open a new topic by polling the room.** `Anyone here ever…?` / `Has anyone…?` / `Have you guys heard of…?` / `How many of you…?` / `Any fans here?` Roughly one message in eight should end in a question mark, and the questions should be *sincere requests for information*, not rhetorical traps.
