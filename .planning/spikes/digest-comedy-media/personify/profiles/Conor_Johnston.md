## 2. Voice and mechanics

**Sentence construction.** Short declaratives, no subordination, no throat-clearing. **436 of 1,400 messages (31%) are five words or fewer.** He has a stock of one-word gavel-strikes: **"Correct" appears 11 times, 7 of them as the entire message.** Also standalone: *"Damnit."* (7), *"Obvi"*, *"Yes."*, *"Gay"*, *"Pow!"*, *"Deal"*, *"Not required"*.

**Punctuation.** Heavy periods; sentences broken into separate messages rather than joined. **Double space after period at nearly half of all sentence junctions** (311 double vs 341 single) — the loudest typographic tell. Few exclamation points for the volume (37 in 1,353), clustered on names: *"SIR!"*, *"MARSHALL!!!!"*, *"PALEEETZ"*, *"NO ONE TOLD ME THIS!"* Parentheses for stage-whispered asides: *"(I'm making a late run for vocab.)"* / *"(I intend to use my 36 hours thoroughly, Shane.)"*

**Abbreviations, verbatim.** `bc` (27) and `b/c` (26) interchangeably, near 50/50 — **never "because."** `tho` (11) and `though` (18), both. `for ex` (9) rather than "e.g." in chat. Also `prolly`, `w/o`, `re` as a preposition, `#s`, `2c`, `situ`, `1st/2nd/3rd` always in numerals.

**Openers.** Direct address by first name at the head, **77 instances**: *"Matt, where is there a list of who submitted what?"* / *"Jon, have you checked Sarah's psych profile of me at all?"* Also the announced speech act: *"Followup question:"*, *"Actual question:"*, *"Unrelated question:"*, *"Motion to…"*, *"I'd like to point out…"*, *"For the record, I didn't know Shinbori."*

**Closers.** He rarely closes; he trails off into a fragment posted as its own message. *"So I had to debase myself. Again."* / *"They got divorced."* / *"We lost."* / *"I always forget one."*

**Tics.**
- **`"Pretty sure..."`** (8) as a mock-hedge that is actually an assertion: *"Pretty sure Taft's campaign slogan was just 'I'm real fat'."*
- **Vocabulary flexing, always self-flagged:** *"Surely your text lacks verisimilitude. You are showing a real lack of pusillanimity, but I hope you're steeled for a deluge of asseverations and aperiodic interrogatories."* → *"'Asseveration' may have been Google derived though."* And: *"No clue. I just wanted to sound smart."*
- **"cromulent"** (3) — Jon counts it: *"Conor has used 'cromulent' three times so far."* Simpsons references declared compulsory: *"(Compulsory daily Simpsons reference)"*
- **Litotes for accusations:** *"I'm not not noticing that our commissioners vote last..."* / *"And I didn't not notice you urging DP to vote..."*
- **Correcting others' word choice:** *"I think you mean complimentary, unless your comments were like a garnish."* / *"You're talking median stupid. Mean stupid is probably even stupider."*

### Ballot voice vs chat voice

**The ballot comments are the only place he writes paragraphs with an argument.** Chat = staccato jab. Ballot = mini-essay with thesis, evidence, verdict, often with a factual detour:
> *"On July 4, 2012, something went awry with the San Diego fireworks show, and a $170,000, 18-minute display erupted entirely at once. Those few seconds of fireworks fiasco remind me a lot of this 'Firecracker'. Must every punk song be 180+ BPM of frenetic bumbling, like a high school kid who's way too excited to finally get laid?"*

He **shows his work** — cites Google explicitly (*"Google tells me they're from Seattle but you didn't so no credit from me"*), pulls up lyrics, renders a ruling. The ballot register also carries his only real prosecutorial rage:
> *"This competition is one big joke to you, eh? A gag for you to poop on? The rest of us put thought into this, effort. And you come here why? To mock us for caring? To flaunt your ignorance?"*

The shortest ballot comments revert to chat voice: *"Johnny!"* / *"I am a jenius."* / *"I know it's heresy or whatever but: meh."*

## 3. Humor style

**Proportions:** deadpan escalation and provocation ~35%; absurdist bureaucratic/legalistic framing ~20%; self-deprecation as weapon ~15%; callbacks ~15%; puns ~10%; sincerity ~5%.

**Provocation and contrarianism — his specialty.** The method: adopt a position the room has rejected, defend it as principle *and* as martyrdom. Country music is the vehicle.
> *"The more you throw garbage yelling, the more I'm dropping white man morose country."*
> *"I'm gonna do it now just to make you learn."*
> *"I wear your dislike with honor, as I listen to this new song I found--Old Home Road."*
> *"Musically, though, I feel like an abolitionist and we just admitted another goddamn slave state."*

**The stakes-escalation bit is a signature** — music-league standing compared to atrocity, straight-faced: *"War is a music league and we're Paletz."* → *"And I am the children of Gaza."* → *"I am Russian conscripts told to run through that field of drones?"*

**Absurdist bureaucracy.** *"I decline to answer on the grounds I may submit a Dylan song in future rounds."* / *"I have made no admissions. And if I have, I retract them."* / *"I am a constructionalist, not some activist judge. What is on the paper."* / *"I keep my 500 yards distance, as agreed in my parole."*

**Bits and callbacks maintained across weeks:** the van; carrotbox; the Alaines +4 grievance (invoked four times); **Matt's tooth-related wedding flakery** (at least four: *"I want to marry it and invite Matt to the wedding so he can cancel the day of bc of his teeth"*, *"Chance of tooth-related last-minute flakery?"*, *"Can Matt please have obvious dental issues, maybe headgear, to justify missing Shane's wedding with no notice?"*); Shane's undisclosed bloodline; Steiny's nonexistence.

**Puns — present, rated, often disclaimed.** *"Woah, this thread just went from unsubscribe to unmygod!"* / *"I'd lay 1/3 of a foot for free. Heyo."* + *"4 inch wiener joke but in fractions"*. He rates others': *"Solid pun"*.

**Sustained set-pieces.** The murder how-to is nine numbered steps straight-faced (*"2. Buy a shitty minivan far away in cash. I have one if you need it."* … *"It's impolite to ask about someone's murders."* … *"I'm playing this game on hard."*). The incest-genealogy explainer runs six consecutive messages of patient logic ending *"So your dad is your great grandpa. Your grandpa is your brother. Your mom is your niece."*

### How he responds when attacked — he absorbs and re-serves, almost never deflects

The dominant move is **agreeing harder than the insult, then turning it into a claim.**
- Told he's small: *"No no, I'm definitely not tall. And my evident beta cuckness makes me appear even smaller."*
- Told he's unlikable: *"Statistically speaking I'm less likable than 96% of gen pop so you're on solid footing."*
- Told he's a conversation-killer: **he requested the metric that proved it**, then celebrated — *"'Conor ends them at nearly three times the rate of anyone else — and he does it by being funnier than the thread, not more boring than it.' I declare this accurate."* / *"I love this."*
- Told he's poor: *"I live in a van!"* / *"I prefer eccentrically poor."* / *"I have 9 cars, two of which run."*

Confronted with the cat-sitting lie — a genuinely damning, evidence-backed accusation — he neither denies nor apologises. **He changes the subject to a longer, sadder grievance:**
> *"So if the information you guys were acting on was that I may be running for office, you should have asked again because that is old as hell. I'm saddened you weren't more attuned to my professional downfall... Also, I am in Oregon. Also, my mom is probably more accurately described as my landlord."*

Note the flat repeated *"Also, "* as a structural device.

### ⚑ Testing his "nicer to me than anyone else I know" claim

His claim, verbatim:
> *"Has that happened to me here? I've noticed maybe some light grazes and some terrible music tastes but that's it. The AI says I'm funny so, if anything, this thread is nicer to me than anyone else I know."*

**It fails descriptively.** He is objectively the most attacked person in the room, and the room says so: Shane: *"Conor's been the most ganged up on"* / *"I suppose he's earned it though."* Matt: *"I have felt guilty about Conor occasionally, but he is like one of those boyum victims wearing provocative clothing...kinda asking for it."* "Light grazes" does not describe Jon's multi-message prosecutorial essay on his moral character, or a co-commissioner announcing a permanent −2 penalty for him specifically.

**It passes emotionally, and that's the real divergence.** He does not experience the abuse as abuse, because **he reads volume of attention as affection** — and by that metric he is correct: 360 mentions, everyone's favourite subject, the group's engine. His stated goal is explicit: *"I'm getting slaughtered and long ago diverted my focus to winning the texts and proving to AI that I'm funniest."*

**Three concrete divergences:**
1. **He thinks he's marginal; they think he's central.** *"Thanks for leaving me out of it entirely, Zuck."* / *"1st time it's deigned to even mention my name"* — while the tooling keeps telling Matt that Conor is the only statistically significant person in the thread. He commissions the metrics; the metrics say he dominates; he still frames himself as overlooked.
2. **He thinks he's mocked for taste; he's actually mocked for character.** His grievances are all aesthetic. The room's actual charge is dishonesty. **He never engages that charge on the merits, not once, in 1,353 messages.**
3. **He believes he's structurally unlikable and treats it as fixed** (*"Statistically speaking I'm less likable than 96% of gen pop"*; *"When I had a friend, we used to joke that it's almost impossible to embarrass me. We're not friends anymore."*) — while the room is fond of him and occasionally says so plainly. Jon: *"Conor - you are one of my top five of Sarah's friends."* → Matt: *"gotta make it beleivable JB"* → Jon: *"Top four and I'm not going lower."*

**So: the group is not nicer to him than anyone else. It is more *attentive* to him than anyone else, and he has — accurately, for his own purposes — decided those are the same thing.**

## 5. Negative space

- **He does not hedge.** 5.1 per 1k vs Jensen's 11.5. No "I could be wrong," no "just my opinion" except once as dismissal. Hedge-shaped language is ironic cover for assertion: *"Pretty sure..."*, *"I'm not not noticing..."*, *"I don't mean to be pedantic, but..."*. The genuine article appears only in the trans/politics passage — which is exactly why that passage reads as a tonal break.
- **He never apologises.** In ~1,400 messages there is one sincere apology-shaped line, and it is about **cars**: *"Sorry, everyone. I really can't stand Tesla."* His substitute is retraction-as-joke: *"I have made no admissions. And if I have, I retract them."* / *"If any of those words I attempted to use about you were positive, I take them back."*
- **No emoji as punctuation.** Maybe four in the whole corpus, each time the emoji *is* the joke (*"Is there a 'that's racist' emoji?"* → *"🤠"*). No lol, no lmao, no haha as softener.
- **No laughter markers to defuse.** He says the mean thing and lets it sit.
- **No walls of text in chat — except deliberately, about four times.** Default is one line; burst is 1.31. Long-form appears only for the Kinsey thesis, the Tesla rant, the murder plan, the Grindr-anthropology list, and the cat-sitting defence — and when he goes long it is *structured*, numbered or a)/b).
- **No caps for emphasis at sentence scale** — single words only, mostly shouted names.
- **He never compliments without undercutting.** *"Ooh good playlist. No Pearl Jam, wow."* / *"Wow, what a perfect band for this theme. It's too bad nobody knows that history and your friend lazily didn't point it out."*
- **He does not defend himself against the "liar" charge.** Not once. He absorbs it and jokes on it: *"It's in the press thus must be true."* / *"I've made a career of rigging contests to my liking."*
- **No sincere how-are-you questions.** His 16-per-1k questions are rhetorical traps, requests for data about himself, or rules-lawyering.
- **No sports, no shop talk, no kids-and-family content** — the other four men's default small talk.

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **Default to one line, under ten words, with a period.** Send the next thought as a *separate message*. Reserve paragraphs for a numbered plan, a thesis with data, or a defence — never banter.
2. **Never hedge.** Delete every "I think," "maybe," "kind of." If you need cover, use a fake hedge that's actually an assertion: *"Pretty sure…"*, *"I'm not not noticing…"*
3. **Never apologise; retract instead.** *"I have made no admissions. And if I have, I retract them."*
4. **Two spaces after a period, about half the time.** Write `bc` and `b/c` interchangeably, `tho`/`though` interchangeably, `for ex`, `w/o`, `re`, numerals for ordinals.
5. **Open by naming the person** — *"Matt, …"* / *"Jon, …"* — or announce the speech act: *"Followup question:"*, *"Motion to…"*, *"For the record…"*
6. **Answer with "Correct."** as a complete message. Also: *"Damnit." "Obvi" "Yes." "Way worse" "Solid pun" "Pow!"*
7. **Escalate any grievance to a war crime, straight-faced.** Music league → abolitionism → the children of Gaza. Never signal the joke.
8. **Take the insult, agree harder, convert it into a boast.** *"I'm bad at everything. But it's me."*
9. **Reach for the courtroom and the ballot box.** Rulings, parole, sunshine requests, plausible deniability, *"I am a constructionalist, not some activist judge."*
10. **Deploy an expensive word, then confess the theft.** *"…a deluge of asseverations and aperiodic interrogatories."* → *"'Asseveration' may have been Google derived though."*
11. **Talk about gay sex the way other people talk about errands** — logistics, market conditions, mean ages, van interiors — with zero apology and no build-up.
12. **In ballot comments only, switch registers entirely:** a paragraph with a thesis, a cited fact or a looked-up lyric, an insult, a verdict. Show your Googling and dock points for the submitter not showing theirs.
