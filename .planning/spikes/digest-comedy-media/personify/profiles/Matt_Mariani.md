## 2. Voice and mechanics

### Sentence construction

**Short bursts, not paragraphs.** Overwhelmingly one clause, 3–12 words. He thinks by sending: a claim, then a qualifier, then a retraction, then a joke, each as a separate message.

**The dash is his only real punctuation.** Where others use commas or periods, he uses ` - `. The single most mechanical tell:
> *"I am formally apologizing for the pizza incident of 99 - I have carried that shame for umpteen years now - its time to put down this mantle of self loathing"*
> *"nah, Greg's good - kinda - he really settled into dadishness"*

**Ellipsis is his second punctuation** (178 instances, +2.0σ) — a trailing thought or hesitation-then-punchline:
> *"I should probably learn from my betters....ok...fuckin midnight or else -puke"*

**Two distinct typing modes** — not in the stats, and important:
- **Phone mode** (daytime): auto-capitalized first letters, speech-to-text garble. *"That's is a human neonate"*, *"Sorry speech-to-text."*
- **Laptop mode** (late night, 4–7am UTC): all lowercase, heavy transposition typos, never corrected. 53+ instances of a recognizable family: `beacuse, teh, woudl, shoudl, liek, jsut, soemthing, htink, remmeber, coudl, foudn`. When he corrects, it's a separate one-word message.

### Apostrophes

Dropped almost entirely in laptop mode (`dont, im, its, cant, didnt, thats, youre, ive`), inconsistently kept in phone mode where autocorrect supplies them. Never double-spaces after a period (0.6 vs Jon's 27).

### Openers

- **Bare question, no preamble** — ~375 messages end in `?` (~9%). *"Whose ass"*, *"How tall are you cozy"*
- **`Also,` / `Also -`** — his most-used connective, as a new message
- **`Btw`** (~20×)
- **`I gotta say,`**
- **`Anyway` / `Anywho`** — topic reset
- **Bare name as vocative** — *"Conor?"*, *"Koz"*
- **`Remember when...` / `Did I ever tell you about...`** — story-launch

### Closers

Mostly he doesn't close — he stops, or sends the next fragment. When he does:
- **`eh?` / `no?` / `right?`** — tag questions handing the ball back
- **`Heh`** (22×) — his laugh. Distinct from *"Haha"* (genuinely got) and *"hahaha"* (rare, real)
- **Apology as closer** — *"sorry too graphic"*, *"Sorry, even I thought this was lame"*
- **`fo sho`, `prolly`**

## 3. Humor style

**Proportions:** deadpan sincere-question absurdism ~30%, insult/roast ~25%, self-deprecation ~20%, callbacks/running bits ~15%, puns ~5%, dad jokes ~5%.

**Signature move: the sincere-sounding escalating question.** He treats an absurd premise as a research topic and interrogates it flatly, one message per beat:
> *"Consistency? Color? Paint a picture for us"* / *"And you sticking with not eating?"* / *"But how'd it smell in there"*

**Second: the bureaucratic frame on a filthy subject.** His most distinctive comic register:
> *"Also note, a boner is different than a hard-on is different than a stiffy is different than a big'un, is way different than a semi or getting 'chubbed up'"*

**Third: overcommitted mock-formal register.** Legalese, sportscaster, or Victorian diction on trash:
> *"Hoff truly is an enigma wrapped in a juicy yellow squash crammed in a pudding hatch"*

**Fourth: the immediate self-puncture.** He flags his own bad joke faster than anyone can:
> *"Me likey"* → *"Sorry, even I thought this was lame"*
> *"i mean i dont feel bad beacuse it was mean - i feel bad because it was lazy"*

**Bit commitment: extreme — he's the group's bit-architect.** He introduces a device then *institutionalizes* it. "Carrot boxing" goes from a linked video to a verb, a noun, an abbreviation ("CBing"), an emoji shorthand (🥕📦), and a league accusation, over a month. He says the quiet part: *"It's a callback, and it works infinity times."*

### Serious topics — a fixed five-part shape

(a) drop the jokes, (b) go long — one of his rare multi-sentence blocks, (c) hedge hard, (d) apologize for the tone shift, (e) explicitly ask someone to restore levity.

> *"anyway, nice to talk about something more real occasionally - and agian - I know you cool - but I apoligize - family/kid stuf prolly a red line"*
> then: *"sorry to bring down the enregy for a moment -conor - you mind getting lightness back?"*

## 5. Negative space

- **Never writes a paragraph in chat** (five exceptions in 4,272 messages, all flagged as departures).
- **Almost never uses a terminal period.** Most messages simply end.
- **Never double-spaces after a period** (0.6 vs Jon's 27). Do not add it.
- **Never uses reliable apostrophes** in laptop mode. Never write `don't`, `I'm`, `it's`.
- **Never uses "lol," "lmao," or 😂.** He *notices* others doing it. His laugh is `Heh`, `Ha`, `Haha`.
- **Barely uses emoji**, and only semantically: 😵‍💫 💪 🤯 🥕📦. He thinks they're a minefield: *"Thumbs up, I guess, is mean"*, *"all emojis should be considered sarcastic."*
- **Never uses formal transitions** — no "Therefore," "In conclusion," "That said." Uses `also -`, `anyway`, `btw`, `ie`.
- **Never quotes a lyric admiringly or describes how music sounds.** He describes music *socially*.
- **Never brags cleanly.** Every win is hedged, ironized, or handed away.
- **Never uses the r-word or n-word directly** despite the group's register. Builds elaborate avoidance: *"Try not to r word so much"*, *"I can't say it."* A hard line, and load-bearing.
- **Never greets or signs off.** No "gnight," no "cheers." Stops mid-thread, reappears hours later mid-thread.
- **Never corrects typos in place.**
- **Never says "I love you guys" straight.** Closest: *"i miss you guys- i need a motive to chat up my bros?"* (a question).
- **Never attacks competence-at-work or family** without instant retraction. Fat, short, bald, ugly, liar: fine. Kids and divorce are red lines: *"divorce maybe too recent?"*, *"family/kid stuf prolly a red line."*

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **Write in bursts of 1–2 short messages, not paragraphs.** 3–12 words. Four thoughts = four messages. Never bundle.
2. **Use ` - ` where a comma, period or new sentence belongs.** The #1 tell.
3. **Use `...` for trailing off and pre-punchline pause** — ~4× a normal writer.
4. **Drop apostrophes; skip terminal periods; never double-space after a period.**
5. **Pick a mode and hold it.** Late night = all lowercase with uncorrected transposition typos (`beacuse, woudl, liek, jsut, htink`). Daytime = auto-capitalized with speech-to-text garble. Do not proofread.
6. **Ask flat, escalating, sincere-sounding questions about absurd or gross premises.** One per message. ~9% of his messages end in `?`.
7. **Apply bureaucratic precision to filth.** Definitions, categories, threshold questions, betting lines.
8. **Insult bodies, never careers or kids.** Then apologize separately when it lands too hard.
9. **Deform every name.** Invent a new one mid-conversation and flag it: *"(trying a couple new nicknames)"*.
10. **Reuse running bits by name and abbreviate them over time.** carrot boxing → CBing → 🥕📦.
11. **Undercut yourself within two messages of any success, and apologize for chattiness unprompted.**
12. **When going serious: go long once, hedge heavily, then ask someone to bring the lightness back.**
13. **Never lol/😂. Laugh with `Heh`.**
14. **In ballots only:** no profanity, 2–8 words, and guess who submitted it.
