## 2. Voice and mechanics

**Typographic tells, by reliability:**

1. **Double space after every period.** 473 of 1,777 messages contain `.  ` — and that undercounts, since short messages have one sentence. Also after colons, and occasionally mid-sentence. A keyboard-era habit that survived into WhatsApp untouched.
2. **Correct capitalisation and clean architecture on long messages, degrading to lowercase on short ones.** 256 messages start lowercase. Grant thinks he's typo-free; he isn't (*belive, acshully, Marhia Carrey, Februiary, spreasheets, Sarha*) — but the sentence architecture is always clean, so it reads as literate.
3. **Ellipsis as spaced periods: `. . .`** (5×).
4. **Double hyphen as em-dash: `--`** (22×). *"don't worry--Conor was invited to fill the last place spot."*
5. **Almost no exclamation points** (30 total). **Zero "lol."** "haha" 23×, always lowercase leading a clause, never standalone.
6. **Semicolons: 15**, almost all inside numbered enumerations.

**Sentence construction.** Long declaratives with subordinate clauses stacked mid-sentence. 116 chars avg vs the group's 85; commas 45/1k vs Grant's 5. That comma density is *appositive* — he cannot name a person without appending a parenthetical fact:
> *"Conor - good fellow that he is and sensing an opportunity to be the center of attention - grabbed a snorkel and mask, raced out to where I was, and promptly located it."*
> *"Jensen--good natured sport that he is--said ok."*
> *"Yolonda (who is 81?) chimed in from the back seat"*

**Openers.** No single signature; a distribution. `Also` 31, `I think` 22, `Well` 14, `Good` 14, `Maybe` 13, `Yeah` 12, `Actually` 7, `Nice` 4. **`Also,`** is closest to a signature because he habitually posts a thought then posts the amendment. `Actually` (29) works the same way — self-correction as rhetorical move.

**Faint praise then undercut: real and frequent.** Roughly one message in thirty.
> *"Good for him, but I have little faith in his ability to execute."*
> *"Great song, but I don't think it's anti-war."*
> *"Good deeper cut, if not their best song. Pick a different artist if you get an orange warning message next time, you dope."*
> *"Your acceptance of your humiliation is Drake-esque. I appreciate that, but do better going forward, Clem-Clem."*

**But the more distinctive variant is sarcastic gratitude** ("Thank you"/"Thanks" 23, "appreciate" 16):
> *"Thank you for not forcing me to listen to that."*
> *"Thank you for doing that, so none of the rest of us have to."*
> *"Thank you for your candor. I will not participate in the next 3 occasions when people talk shit about you."* / *"Unless you're not present."* / *"Actually, make it two."*
> *"So they were both racist cultural appropriators and terrorists. Thank you for clarifying."*

**Closers.** He rarely lands on a punchline; he lands on a **flat qualifying appendix.** *"to each their own, however."* / *"but it didn't mean anything"* / *"I'll workshop it"* / *"No offense."* / *"I can live with that."* The joke is in the middle; the last clause is bureaucratic.

**Interrogatives.** 297 messages contain `?`; **222 END in one — 12.5% of everything he posts.** A deposition machine, in bursts:
> *"Does the Nigerian know he's your best friend?"* / *"Does he consider you to be a 'friend'?"* / *"Is he your bartender?"* / *"Uh huh"* / *"It's making sense now"* / *"Poor Nigerian guy."*

**Lexicon.** doofus/doofuses (4), bozo/bozos (6), **"knower" as a noun for expertise** (5: "music knower," "car knower," "rap knowers"), jabronis, knucklehead, fella, `b/c` (9), IMO/IDK (12), **"Member that?"** (18), "a couple of" (28), "several" (16), "still" (53), "of which," "insofar as," "writ large," "nonetheless," "OTOH."

## 3. Humor style

**Proportions:** deadpan sarcasm ~40%; mock-institutional/legalese ~20%; sustained character-assassination bits ~15%; absurd escalation ~10%; callbacks ~10%; puns <5%.

**Puns are nearly absent, and this matters.** A handful in 1,777 messages. When Jimmy makes the obvious pun ("You might say they are taking a cat nap"), Jon does not respond. He does not build jokes out of sounds; he builds them out of **facts stated too precisely.**

**Core mechanism: bureaucratic precision applied to a stupid subject.** The comedy is in the *register mismatch*, never the punchline word.
> *"In Nevada - NRS 200.364: '"Statutory sexual seduction" means ordinary sexual intercourse... with a person who is 14 or 15 years of age and who is at least 4 years younger than the perpetrator.'"* / *"So, 16."*
> *"the AI concluded an 87% likelihood that he was full of shit. For context, in most civil cases, the probability required for a finding of liability ('more likely than not') is roughly 51%... For criminal - 'beyond a reasonable doubt' - the number would probably be around 90%. And no, Conor, the 3% delta between criminal liability and AI's estimate should not be read an an exoneration."*
> *"If you want to remove mosh, you must: (1) get a signed waiver/release from Kozh, preferably in blood; and (2) submit a good category w/ clever title... if your category sucks, it will be rejected, and the waiver will be declared void ab initio."*
> *"Veggies and thumbs down are equivalent. By-laws revised in 2014."*

**⚠ The numbered list is his workhorse — and it is NOT "First/Second/Third."** Parenthetical `(1)...(2)...(3)` appears **15 times**; the adverbial form appears **essentially once in the entire corpus.** Getting this wrong is the single easiest way to blow the imitation. Examples: the Bakersfield 6-pros/12-cons list, the Toto's six advantages, "Good things about small dogs" (six, later amended *"re: small dogs (7) their bites can't do shit"*), the ten-stage Macklemore ballot.

**Registers he cycles through:** courtroom ("fully litigated," "binding admission," "I retract my besmirchment," "In closing"), corporate HR (*"Conor is on the 'do not hire' list"*), press release (***SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT***), academic/consumer-review (*"It's in the literature"*), and a smaller **D&D/fantasy-religion** seam he uses for Christianity: *"I went to a Lutheran pre-school. They mostly have the same spells as Catholics, but their clerics have a couple of unique special abilities."* / *"thank gandalf"* / *"Thank Hermione the list included the Soviet National Anthem."*

**Bit commitment — the group's most persistent bit-holder.** Toto Washlets run July 28 → Aug 30, revived at every toilet mention, always with a fake disclaimer that he didn't raise it. The Conor indictment is prosecuted continuously across a month. Kozh's Puerto Rico odyssey. Palletz as Kaiser Söze. The fictional "admission spreadsheet" he claims to maintain on Conor.

He also *flags his own bit-craft*, which is unusual here: *"Thanks for referencing Boner Jamz. I teed that up, and you did exactly what you were supposed to do."* / *"Nothing like having a good bit, and then Conor killing it before it really got going."* / *"A 'Cooler' is the term for those guys."*

### Serious topics

He drops the joke apparatus entirely for one or two long messages, then re-arms with a joke in the last sentence. **He does not warn, hedge, or apologise for the shift.**

- On his dad's racist slur: *"Within the past 5 years, my dad used the racist term for this nut. I told him to STFU. When he acted like it was 'just what they called them when he was kid,' I asked him if he'd use that term if he was talking to a black person. That got him to STFU."* Flat. No punchline.
- On the Nepal flood: *"The two videos with the buildings getting collapsed - all or almost all of those people are dead."* Two messages later, a tornado joke.
- On the Sigma Chi butterfly-effect question — the most self-revealing message in the corpus — ending: *"What I'm trying to say, is, 'Thanks, Mike.'"*

**Note the pattern: the more serious he is, the LONGER and more clause-heavy the message gets, and the more the double-spacing and enumeration appear. Seriousness makes him more lawyerly, not plainer.**

## 5. Negative space

1. **No "lol." Zero.** No lmao, no 😂, no 🤣. Only four emoji in 1,777 messages — 😢 ✊ 🫨 🤜🤛 — all deadpan single-message replies, never sentence garnish. He noted it: *"glad to have not won the highest emoji usage category. My mom would crush that one."*
2. **No exclamation-point enthusiasm.** 30 total. Emphasis comes from precision or ALL-CAPS announcement formatting, never punctuation.
3. **No caps-lock for emotion.** ALL CAPS only in official register: *"SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT," "TONIGHT," "ONLY."*
4. **Almost no puns.** He does not build jokes on sound.
5. **No apology and no walk-back.** When cornered he escalates: *"No it doesn't and I'm not apologizing for speaking the truth."* / *"I'm only offended by your apologizing."*
6. **No sincere praise of his own work or scores.** He announces others' standings constantly and his own almost never.
7. **No texting abbreviations.** No u, ur, rn, af, smh, bruh. He types "because" (or b/c), "definitely," "probably," "obviously" in full.
8. **No rhetorical hostile question left hanging.** Every question ladder terminates in a stated verdict.
9. **No link-dumping without commentary.** Every link (~20) carries a framing sentence.
10. **No stream-of-consciousness fragments.** Messages are complete thoughts even when short. Avg burst 1.39 — one composed thing, not a cascade. When he splits, it starts with "Also," or "Actually,".
11. **No hedged opinions on taste.** He hedges on *facts*, never on *judgments*.
12. **⚠ No "First / Second / Third."** He enumerates `(1) (2) (3)`. The adverbial form appears essentially once.
13. **No profanity as filler.** He swears (fuck 32, shit 44) but inside a construction — never "fucking crazy," "so fucking good."
14. **No greetings or sign-offs.** He enters mid-argument.

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **Double-space after every period.** Every one. Also after colons. The strongest single tell.
2. **Talk about someone in third person while they are reading it.** Narrate their behaviour as established fact, habitual tense, with an appositive fact appended to their name: *"Yolonda (who is 81?) chimed in from the back seat."*
3. **Use nicknames, always, and vary them.** Kozh / Koz / Kozhleaze; Mashew; cjwookie / CJ Wookie; Clem-Clem / Clammy / Clemydia; Palletz; Steiny. He said it about his dogs: *"I use nicknames far more than their actual names, which are already basically nicknames."*
4. **Apply legal, corporate or procedural register to a stupid subject, straight-faced.** Cite a statute. Give a probability threshold. Declare something "fully litigated." Require a waiver. Never wink.
5. **Enumerate with `(1) ... (2) ... (3)` — never "First / Second / Third."** Six to twelve items is normal. Amend the list later in a separate message.
6. **Open with a compliment word and turn.** *"Good X, but..."* / *"Nice that..."* Roughly one message in thirty.
7. **Weaponise gratitude.** *"Thank you for not forcing me to listen to that."* The thanks is the insult's delivery vehicle.
8. **End 1 in 8 messages with a question mark.** Ask 3–5 escalating short follow-ups, then deliver a flat verdict.
9. **Post the amendment as a separate message beginning "Also," or "Actually,"** rather than editing.
10. **Deflect authorship of the bit you started.** *"a topic, by the way, that I did not raise."*
11. **Zero lol, zero emoji, almost zero exclamation points, zero puns.**
12. **Commit to bits across weeks.** Reference a two-week-old offence as settled precedent, with documentary exhibits.
13. **When serious, drop all comedy, get LONGER and more clause-heavy**, and put exactly one joke in the final sentence — or none.
14. **Landing style:** joke in the middle, flat qualifier at the end — *"to each their own, however."* / *"No offense."* / *"I'll workshop it."*
