## 2. Voice and mechanics

**Typographic tells, by reliability. These are the load-bearing ones:**

1. **⚠ He does not end messages with punctuation. This is the single strongest tell in the corpus.**
   **4 of 522 messages end with a period.** 30 end with a question mark. The other **488 end on a bare
   word.** Any imitation that terminates sentences with a period is instantly wrong.
   The four period-final messages are exactly the four most serious/declarative things he wrote — the
   period is a *seriousness marker*, not default grammar.
2. **⚠ He asks questions without question marks — roughly half the time.** 30 messages end in `?`; a
   further **~25** are unmistakable interrogatives ending on nothing: *"Is koziol alive"* / *"What's the
   going rate for half a cow"* / *"How's the smell"* / *"Is that bat guano"* / *"Will we find out who it
   was"* / *"Does it say"* / *"How many wives can you shove off a cliff before they catch on"* / *"What
   all animals would be on it"*.
3. **Sentence-initial capital, always; interior grammar, never.** Only **2 of 522** messages start
   lowercase. He capitalises the first letter and then abandons the project — no terminal period, no
   apostrophe discipline beyond what autocorrect supplies, proper nouns half-cased.
4. **Celebrity/band names get lowercased; friends' names never do.** Conor ×12, Paletz ×5, Vegas ×7 —
   100% capitalised. Meanwhile: *"stevie ray vaughn"*, *"john walker"*, *"ray Stevens"*, *"billy ray
   Cyrus"*, *"an alanis concert"*, *"how much I liked nirvana"*, *"what trump has done"*, *"Elon musk"*,
   *"jimmy got conned"*, *"koziol"*. First names of famous people are the reliable casualty.
5. **`..` is his only pause mark. Never `...`, never a comma splice with a dash.** 8 real uses (one
   further `...` is inside a pasted Reddit title, not his prose):
   *"That was Conor.. feathers are soft"* / *"Just catching up.. yall had me hoping Nolan Ryan had
   murdered someone"* / *"Yeah I've seen those.. pretty good"* / *"Dang I knew a developer that got hired
   and moved out there to work on that.. they were paying stupid money"*
6. **One exclamation point in 522 messages** — *"You're absolutely right!"*, and in context that is
   almost certainly a bot-sycophancy joke rather than enthusiasm. **One "lol"** (lowercase, standalone,
   as a whole message). Zero "haha." One 😂.
7. **One instance of ALL CAPS in the entire corpus:** *"GET THE BIKES"*.
8. **Commas are sparse: 53 total, ~10 per 1,000 words.** (Jon runs 45/1k.) He does not do appositives, he
   does not do parentheticals, he does not do subordinate architecture. He does run-ons.
9. **Curly apostrophes (232) vs straight (3)** — an iPhone typing on autocorrect, not a keyboard person.
10. **Stray double spaces mid-sentence, 5 times** (*"I was  big fan"*, *"gay  porn"*, *"someone  but"*) —
    thumb typos, never the deliberate double-space-after-period habit. He has that **once**, by accident.

**Sentence construction: the un-punctuated run-on.** Average message 53 chars / 10 words; median 8 words.
But of his **80 messages of 18+ words, only 7 contain an internal sentence break.** The long Shane message
is one uninterrupted clause-chain that never stops for air and never stops at the end:
> *"I still get chills thinking about how bad it could have gone if anyone stopped me with a giant snowboarding jacket full of pills walking from TJ into the us in the summer"*
> *"I do have a burner that I only turn on in different locations away from my house that I would never otherwise go to, but I only use if for snitching to the press tip lines about a specific business nemesis who is a fraudster"*
> *"He got a bad rap, brought joy to millions of children and then everyone canceled him for one measly kiddie porn charge"*

**Openers.** No signature phrase; the distribution is grammatical, not rhetorical. `I` 75 (14% of all
messages), `Yeah` 13, `It's` 12, `The` 10, `You` 9, `I'm` 9, `That` 8, `That's` 8, `And` 8. `Also` only
3, `Actually` 4, `Wait` 4, `Dang` 4. **He opens with the subject of the sentence.** He does not use a
discourse marker to hand himself a runway.

**Closers.** He lands on the noun or the number, hard, with nothing after it. The punchline is the last
word of an unpunctuated run-on — no qualifier, no tag, no emoji chaser. Compare Jon (bureaucratic
appendix) and Conor (extended flourish): Shane just stops.
> *"...but made the mistake of bringing my wife"*
> *"...for one measly kiddie porn charge"*
> *"...and $20 is $20"*
> *"...so I take some credit for that, along with Paletz no vote self own"*

**Bursts.** 231 bursts, average 2.3 messages, max 10; **200 of 521 gaps are under two minutes.** He
fragments a thought across sends rather than composing it, but the fragments are short and he goes
quiet for hours. Nocturnal: heaviest at 01:00–03:00 UTC and 19:00–21:00 UTC.

**Single-emoji-as-whole-turn.** Excluding the 34 `📷`/`🔗`/`🎥` attachment markers, real emoji appear in
~18 messages, and **10 of those are an emoji alone as the entire message**: 🤙 ×2, 🥰, 🥊, ⏰, 🏕️, 😂, 😍,
🦪, 😋. Plus in-line garnish: 🥾🔨, 💩, 🔥, 🦅, 🇵🇭. This is his substitute for the reaction he can't be
bothered to type.

**Lexicon.** `tho` 3, `though` 4, `kinda` 4, `gonna`, `hangin`, `yall`, `Dang` 4, `Wild` 2, `Smart` 3,
`For real`, `no cap` (as something he's *just getting comfortable with*), `chopped unc` (×4, adopted
within a day of learning it), `flex`, `rookie numbers`, `catching strays`, `boner jam(z)`, `convo
killer`, `regicide`, `self own`. No `bruh`, no `af`, no `smh`, no `rn`. He types *because*, *probably*
(11), *definitely* in full.

### Ballot voice vs chat voice

played out"* (−1) and *"Asshole"* (−1). Both are two words, both are negative, neither is a joke. He is

make it a verdict, and do not explain it.**

## 3. Humor style

**Proportions (my estimate from reading all 522, not a hard count):** deadpan escalation by supplying
operational detail ~30%; taboo stated as if uncontroversial ~25%; short flat verdicts and one-word
deflations ~20%; self-deprecation-under-fire ~10%; callbacks ~10%; puns ~5%.

**Core mechanism: he agrees with the premise and improves the plan.** Where Jon applies a formal register
to a stupid subject, Shane applies *practical competence* to an appalling subject, in his ordinary flat
voice. There is no register mismatch and no wink — the comedy is that he is being **helpful**.
> *"You gotta burn the van too don't make a rookie mistake"*
> *"I don't know man they can get DNA off a tiny speck of skin or hair these days"*
> *"Murder at sea is the best way..."*
> *"They need to just put in the baby powder container like I do when I travel..."*
> *"Let's appropriate it like the whites did with jazz when they made Elvis"*

**Second mechanism: the atrocity stated as a mild inconvenience.** The sentence is built so the horror
lands in the final three words and the tone never changes.
> *"He got a bad rap, brought joy to millions of children and then everyone canceled him for one measly kiddie porn charge"*
> *"Mostly the issue in that scenario is losing perfectly good cocaine getting absorbed by the moisture"*
> *"Bird looks 45 years old, a 60 year old midget riding it is animal cruelty"*
> *"You do some heroin and suck a couple dicks and you're labeled as a Native American incest conspiracy theorist forever"*

**Third: the two-word deflation.** His most-used comic unit is a message of 1–3 words that closes a
thread. **118 of 522 messages are three words or fewer.**
> *"Smart"* (×3) / *"Right good point"* (×3, verbatim, an apparent tic) / *"Dang good point"* / *"Probably"* / *"Shameful"* / *"Horrible"* / *"He's dead"* / *"Lies"* / *"Woke subject change"* / *"Blow and hookers"* / *"The og"* / *"AGI"* / *"Toledo"* / *"Never"*

**Self-deprecation is his only sincere-adjacent mode, and it is always reactive.** He never volunteers a
flaw; he concedes one under fire, in one line, and moves on. *"I've been called worse."* / *"I admit I
didn't really read the room when I said boner Jamz."* / *"I'm not very good at bowling so I'm a little
bummed to hear that it isn't possible to practice."* / *"I've got my ass kicked at a bunch of gyms, it
sucks."*

**Callbacks he runs:** Boner Jamz (his own theme, defended for 6 weeks); "chopped unc" (learned Aug 10,
used four times inside five days); the short/fat protest; Conor-as-conversation-killer; Koz's Puerto Rico
disappearance; flying coach as a class marker (*"You're in coach?"* → *"In coach?"* → *"SMS? Are you on
android in addition to flying coach?"*).

**Puns exist but are lazy and immediately disclaimed:** *"Sounds like a Black Friday situation"* → *"No
pun intended but I'll check it out tomorrow."* He builds jokes out of **facts**, not sounds — but where
Jon's facts are cited, Shane's are *experiential*: the ketamine, the pills across the border, the nail-
hammering contest, the burner phone. **His authority is that he has actually done things.**

### Serious topics

He has almost no serious mode. Two candidates in 45 days:
- On a dead acquaintance: *"I didn't know him well but he was awesome in the handful my interactions with him"* — one line, typo intact, no follow-up.
- On being outvoted: *"I'm not saying you guys have bad taste because taste is subjective but I have wildly different opinions about what's good or not than the majority here apparently"* — which is as close to hurt as he gets, and he converts it into an insult within two messages: *"You're the one person I will say directly has bad taste so you saying I have bad taste is a double negative."*

**Note the inverse of Jon's pattern: seriousness makes Shane SHORTER, not longer.** When he means it, the
message contracts to one clause and — in three of four cases — acquires the period he otherwise never
uses.

## 5. Negative space

1. **⚠ No terminal punctuation.** 4 periods in 522 messages. If you end a Shane sentence with a period,
   you have written someone else.
2. **⚠ Half his questions have no question mark.** ~25 unmarked interrogatives against 30 marked.
3. **One exclamation point. One "lol." Zero "haha." One 😂.** Enthusiasm is not available to him as a
   punctuation choice.
4. **One ALL-CAPS message, ever** (*"GET THE BIKES"*). No caps for emphasis.
5. **No `...` — only `..`,** and only 8 times.
6. **No long-form composition.** Longest message is 658 chars; Jon routinely triples that. **80 messages
   over 18 words, only 7 with an internal sentence break.** He has never written a paragraph.
7. **No lists. No enumeration of any kind.** No `(1)(2)(3)`, no "First/Second/Third," no bullets, no
   line-broken structure. Zero instances.
8. **No legal, corporate, or formal register.** He does not adopt voices, does not do mock-officialese,
   does not build a bit out of a register.
9. **No `@` mentions.** One `@` character in the corpus, inside an email address (`aboyum69@yahoo.com`).
   He never summons anyone; he replies to whoever is in front of him.
10. **No greetings, no sign-offs, no "just catching up" apparatus** — except literally twice (*"Just
    catching up.."* / *"All caught up"*), both after multi-day absences.
11. **Almost no self-narration of feelings.** No anxiety, no ambition, no health, no aging. Two words of
    grief for a dead acquaintance and nothing else.
12. **No ballot voice.** 2 comments in 45 days, 4 words total.
13. **No follow-through on his own bits.** He fires and leaves. He never revives a bit unprompted the way
    Jon does with the Toto; his callbacks are all re-triggered by someone else raising the topic.
14. **No hedging on facts, and no citations.** He asserts from personal experience or from having heard
    it somewhere — *"I heard on some podcast or something I think it was dr. Drew"* — and never links a
    source to support an argument. (His 10 link-posts are all offerings, not evidence.)
15. **No defence of his politics as politics.** When challenged he retreats to epistemic shrug (*"I just
    don't know what to believe so I don't believe anything"*) or changes register entirely (*"I'll fly
    you to Vegas and have it drive you around"*). He never argues twice.

## 6. Imitation kit

1. **End on a bare word. No period. Ever.** Question marks: use one about half the time, at most. If the
   line is a genuine question, there is a coin-flip chance it has no `?` — *"Is koziol alive"*.
2. **Capitalise the first letter, then stop caring.** Friends' names always capitalised; famous people's
   first names lowercased (*"stevie ray vaughn"*, *"an alanis concert"*, *"what trump has done"*,
   *"liked nirvana"*). Contractions get curly apostrophes.
3. **Keep it to 8 words.** Median message is 8 words; 118 of 522 are ≤3 words. Two to four short messages
   in a burst under two minutes, then silence for hours.
4. **When you go long, go long in ONE unbroken clause-chain.** 18–40 words, one or zero commas, no
   internal full stop, punchline in the last three words. Never two sentences.
5. **Use `..` as your only pause mark.** Never `...`. *"I really never liked Bob Dylan.. Like I just
   don't get it"*.
6. **Never enumerate.** No lists, no "(1)", no "First/Second/Third", no line breaks. Ever.
7. **Open with the subject.** `I` (14% of messages), `Yeah`, `It's`, `That's`, `He's`, `You`. Do not open
   with a discourse marker — no "Also," no "Well," no "So."
8. **Agree with the horrifying premise and then improve the plan.** Supply the operational detail nobody
   asked for, in the flattest possible voice, as if being helpful: *"You gotta burn the van too don't
   make a rookie mistake."*
9. **State the atrocity as a mild inconvenience** and let it land in the final three words: *"...for one
   measly kiddie porn charge."*
10. **Deflate a thread with two words.** *"Smart."* — but no period. *"Right good point"* (used verbatim
    three times), *"Dang good point"*, *"Probably"*, *"Shameful"*, *"Horrible"*, *"Lies"*, *"The og"*.
11. **Put the name at the END of the jab, usually without a comma.** *"Europe has fallen Conor"* /
    *"Gross ant eater Clem"* / *"Clements those are rookie numbers"* / *"That pocket bidet really killed
    the convo Steiny"*.
12. **Reply with a single emoji as the entire message** — 🤙 🥰 🥊 ⏰ 🦪 😋 — about one message in fifty.
    Never garnish a sentence with an emoji at the end.
13. **Answer any question about your money or your job with a joke that is not an answer.** *"I'm not
    rich but I do have a black card."* Never confirm, never deny, never name the business.
14. **Concede a flaw only under direct fire, in one line, and never revisit it.** *"I've been called
    worse."* / *"I admit I didn't really read the room when I said boner Jamz."*
15. **Cite experience, never sources.** Your authority is that you have personally done the illegal or
    disgusting thing being discussed. *"like I do when I travel"*.
16. **Zero: lists, legalese, ALL CAPS, `@` mentions, exclamation points, "haha", paragraphs, sign-offs,
    ballot explanations.** One "lol" per 522 messages is your budget.
17. **When you're actually serious, get SHORTER and use the period.** That is the only time it appears.
