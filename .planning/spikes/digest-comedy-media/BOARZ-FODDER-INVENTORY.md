# Boarz II Men — media fodder inventory

Every **chat moment**, **Regulars** entry and **Coinage** entry published in the Boarz
digests this season (R135, R145–R149), ranked as raw material for a media bit.

Coinage and Regulars only start at R147, so R135–R146 are chat-only — 49 items in total.

**Score** is 1–10 for *usability as a bit*, not for how funny the week was. It rewards the
things the courtroom video proved matter:

- a **specific player doing something to another specific player**
- a **contradiction or receipt** — someone's own words used against them
- a **scoreboard payoff** — the joke lands on a number, not on a punchline we write
- **self-contained** — no paragraph of setup needed

⚠ = handle with care before it leaves the group chat. Reasons noted inline.

Formats referenced are from `SCENARIOS.md`.

## Reading the list

**Make these two first.** #1 *Duly Noted* and #2 *Google Told Me and I Ignored It* are both
from **R148**, both are one round deep, and both have the exact shape the courtroom video
proved works: a named player, a written self-own, and a number at the end. Neither needs
the audience to remember anything.

**R148 is the richest week of the season** — five items in the top twenty. R145 is the
deepest but most of its best material is gross or sprawling.

**Coinage punches above its weight.** All three entries (#8, #19, #23) score 7+, because
the dictionary format has already done the compression — there's a term, a coiner, a date,
a usage count and a best-use quote. That's a script with the research finished.

**Regulars is a supercut, not a bit.** The Consul appears three times and the right move is
one montage across all three rounds (#14), not three separate treatments.

**Four items are flagged ⚠.** #36 (the AMA) is the one I'd actually stop on — it's generous,
personal, and Conor's to release. #45 I'd leave in the chat entirely.
