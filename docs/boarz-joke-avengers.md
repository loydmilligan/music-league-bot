# The Boarzvengers

Ten extremely qualified people to save the world, provided the world submits a song before the deadline. These are affectionate fictional characters built from the Boarz player profiles and the league's own running jokes. The **main pick** gets the full treatment; the **backup** is a spare pitch.

## Matt Mariani (Mashew) — Captain Repost

- **Powers and features:** Can edit a message after everyone has already answered it, double-send a command until it becomes policy, and deduct one point from any ska within earshot. His shield is a rotating digest dashboard; its reverse side displays the rule he is currently violating.
- **Origin story:** A commissioner stayed awake through thirteen unanswered chat messages, tried to fix one typo, and was struck by the radioactive *second draft*. He emerged able to run an entire league while insisting the ballot he just entered is technically allowed.
- **Outfit:** Frayed skate-punk jacket, utility belt of spare deadlines, a captain's shield made from a loading spinner, and a helmet with an aggressively receding hairline painted on it.
- **Why this pick:** The dossier casts Matt as commissioner, prolific editor, rule writer and occasional rule breaker; his ska deduction is already a superpower in practice.
- **Backup:** **The Discovery Channel** — beams a `+1 for discovery` at obscure songs, then talks over their first verse.

## Dave Steingart (Steiny) — Doctor Fine Print

- **Powers and features:** Fires eligibility rulings from two calibrated clipboards. Can split any score into its component merits, and can halt a battle by demanding a legally defensible definition of “booty.”
- **Origin story:** After a ballot arrived with the math shown, the standards committee tried to make him its chairman. He audited the committee, found the category wording defective, and walked out wearing its seal of authority.
- **Outfit:** Lab coat over a dance-punk tee, steel-toe shoes, reading glasses with tiny redline marks, and a measuring tape for the ongoing height census.
- **Why this pick:** His player material shows the rare chat appearance, meticulous ballot arithmetic, theme-eligibility rulings and stature investigation. A procedural superhero practically wrote himself.
- **Backup:** **The Napster Ghost** — materializes once per millennium to drop an early-2000s banger, then disappears before roll call.

## Clements Johnson (Clem) — The Clam Signal

- **Powers and features:** Senses fires, unsafe dancing and signs that somebody needs checking on from several time zones away. His clam-shaped beacon summons help at dawn; each use adds exactly one unnecessary N to Conor's name.
- **Origin story:** While the rest of the team argued over a playlist, Clem noticed an actual emergency. His concern touched a mysterious frog in an overalls pocket, which granted him superhuman empathy and a truly unreasonable 4 a.m. response time.
- **Outfit:** Fireproof overalls with a frog-sized pouch, reflective dad sneakers, headlamp, and a cape fashioned from a baby blanket.
- **Why this pick:** The dossier calls him the group's early-warning system and dawn patrol. The frog and “Clammy” are established Boarz bits, so the costume belongs to him.
- **Backup:** **The Shower Flosser** — pulls a strand of emergency floss from any running shower to lasso a villain.

## Conor Johnston (CJ Wookie) — The Consul of Correct

- **Powers and features:** Ends any argument with a single “Correct.” Stores the rest of the verdict in a devastating ballot essay. Can enter 104 pieces of media into evidence and redact his own most incriminating dispatches.
- **Origin story:** A cosmic tribunal gave him unlimited speaking time. He used one word in the group chat, saved the remaining minutes, and spent them all in the comments section of a song he disliked.
- **Outfit:** Judicial robe cut into a Wookie-sized field jacket, evidence satchel full of memes, redaction tape across one glove, and a stamp reading `CORRECT`.
- **Why this pick:** The profile's split between terse chat rulings and lengthy ballot comments is the central joke; his unusually large media trail and deleted messages complete the courtroom persona.
- **Backup:** **The Distributor** — hurls exhibits faster than the team can open them.

## Shane Farkas (Uncle Shane) — The One-Word Wonder

- **Powers and features:** Can reduce a supervillain's three-hour monologue to “Still played out.” Submits battle plans about 80 hours early, communicates in single emoji, and reserves his only exclamation point for a genuine apocalypse.
- **Origin story:** The Avengers invited him to a strategy meeting. Shane sent one verdict, a 🤙, and a fully completed mission plan four days before the meeting. They had no choice but to hand him the gavel.
- **Outfit:** Uncle-style red cap, tactical cardigan, tiny gavel on a wrist cord, and a speech bubble badge with room for one word.
- **Why this pick:** His dossier features just two written ballot comments, an economical chat style, emoji signals and league-leading early submissions. His silence deserves top billing.
- **Backup:** **The Early Bird** — wins every time-sensitive mission before dispatch remembers to send it.

## Dave Jensen (Jensen) — The Ballot Sleeper

- **Powers and features:** Looks like the team's most reasonable member until voting opens. Then every bland song receives a laser-guided one-liner. His memory field makes enemies “rember” precisely what they did wrong.
- **Origin story:** A quiet citizen clicked “Submit ballot” and discovered that all the opinions he had politely withheld had become projectiles. The transformation recurs every round, with no known antidote.
- **Outfit:** Respectable everyday clothes over a hardcore-punk suit, concealed red pen, and a reversible mask: calm smile on one side, ballot comment box on the other.
- **Why this pick:** His profile explicitly contrasts mild chat behavior with ruthless written reviews. The recurring “rember” spelling gives the power its wonderfully petty mechanism.
- **Backup:** **Ha.** — can end a villain's entire speech with one dry syllable.

## Jon Black (JB) — The Canonizer

- **Powers and features:** Gives every teammate a new name, then issues binding rulings on whether their theme, lyrics and instruments count. Can summon sworn testimony from any party between 2004 and 2011 and stretch a minor objection into a full hearing.
- **Origin story:** When four songs claimed to be about boats without sufficient boat content, JB opened the Book of Canon. The book chose him as its keeper and immediately requested a source for the claim.
- **Outfit:** Plaid barrister's cape, black glasses, a neck-hair mantle of uncertain length, and a utility belt of annotated category definitions.
- **Why this pick:** The Boarz material makes him both the league's prolific nicknamer and its most relentless theme prosecutor. The neck-hair costume nods to the cast montage's JB gag.
- **Backup:** **The Mint** — coins a nickname so durable that even its owner starts using it.

## Grant Koziol (Koz) — The Flex Capacitor

- **Powers and features:** Converts any ordinary act into a subtle, hard, soft or anti flex. Can mute the entire battlefield, announce the mute, and return moments later to ask what happened.
- **Origin story:** A badly wired chat notification struck Grant while he was classifying a flex. The resulting surge gave him a complete taxonomy of showing off, but permanently broke the mute button.
- **Outfit:** Wide-shouldered blue jacket with adjustable width settings, “COMFY LUBE” name patch, soundproof headphones that leak notifications, and a belt of 20 flex citations.
- **Why this pick:** His dossier records the many flex rulings, repeated chat muting and return, plus the league's long nickname drift from Koziol to “comfy lube.”
- **Backup:** **The Abbreviator** — saves civilization three keystrokes at a time; announces victory with “btw.”

## Darren Paletz (DP) — The Phantom Correspondent

- **Powers and features:** Invisible in the group chat, highly visible in the ballot comments. Appears after 10 p.m., files a cutting review from an undisclosed location, and vanishes before anyone can ask a follow-up.
- **Origin story:** The league declared him missing after 72 chat messages. An editor found 31 fully formed reviews arriving from the same empty chair and realized the chair was his secret headquarters.
- **Outfit:** Press coat with three interchangeable nameplates—Paletz, Pallets and Palletz—plus a camera that photographs only an empty seat. His cape reads `NOT ON FILE` when he turns around.
- **Why this pick:** The dossiers place his sparse chat presence beside a much larger written-review output; the late-night appearances and three-way surname spelling split are unusually specific character material.
- **Backup:** **For F's Sake** — a four-line spoken-word attack delivered only when the mission has already gone sideways.

## Jimmy Troy — Captain Fresh of Breath Air

- **Powers and features:** Restores team morale by complimenting even the most doomed submission. Can correct a typo without erasing history: an asterisk appears in the air and amends the record for everyone.
- **Origin story:** When the ballot comments became a combat sport, Jimmy offered a sincere “Good job.” The kindness generated a force field. His next message began with an asterisk, and the field became permanent.
- **Outfit:** Bright varsity cape, grin-shaped shield, soft referee whistle, and a sash printed with his immortal review, “A fresh of breath air!”
- **Why this pick:** His dossier makes him the league's designated encourager, documents his follow-up asterisk corrections, and preserves that charming uncorrected review. He is the team's actual emotional support.
- **Backup:** **The Ratifier** — says “Yes” to a proposal so warmly that everyone briefly forgets it was a terrible idea.
