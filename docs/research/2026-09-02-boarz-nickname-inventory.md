# Boarz II Men — nickname inventory (attested only)

**Source:** `chat_messages` where `group_name LIKE 'Boarz%'` — 11,988 messages,
2026-07-11 → 2026-09-02.
**Method:** capitalized-token frequency + non-dictionary-token frequency +
vocative extraction (`^Name,` / `, name$` / `hey|thanks|fuck you + X`) +
name-adjacent bigrams, then every candidate verified in context.
**Rule applied:** only forms that actually appear in the chat body. Nothing invented.

Counts are message occurrences in the body text (sender field excluded). They run
a few high because the corpus contains Matt's double-sent/edited near-duplicates
(that's the R148 "The Editor" thing) — treat them as ranks, not exact tallies.

## Cross-cutting

- **Coiners:** Matt and JB produce nearly all of it, and they coin differently. Matt
  drifts by sound and synonym (Koziol → cozy oil → comfy lube; Clem → Clam → Clammie).
  JB coins by formal register (Kozh, Clem-Clem, "Special Clem-Clem", cjwookie,
  Davidov, Scamantha).
- **Receivers:** Grant and Clements take the most; JB, Jensen and Shane take almost none.
- **Spelling as fingerprint:** Pallets = Matt, Palletz = JB, Paletz = everyone else.
  Same for Kozh = JB.
- **Cast who are NOT league members** (they surface as strong name-like tokens and will
  poison any naive alias table): Marshall / Little Marshall, Hoff (Alex Hoff), Mova,
  Nima, Lex, Shinbori ("Sham Sham"), Denicke, Berto, Hively, Torres, Insley, Munkey /
  Munkeyman, Gauci, Rudrud, Baldo, Wagner, Gus, Roger the Cook. Plus dogs: Bubsy
  (a.k.a. Bubba, Busha, Susha), Bobsy, Dipshit, Skweeky, and the naming chain
  billy → bilbo → bilbacheese → billy beans → beansy → bubsy.
