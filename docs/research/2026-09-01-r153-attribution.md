## Signal inventory — what to feed a context builder (Project D)

Ranked by yield-per-token in this round.

### Carried real signal

1. **Chat messages in the 6 weeks before the submission deadline, filtered to artist/genre/band-name
   mentions.** Two attributions (Koziol/McCreadie, Jimmy/Stick Figure) came out of single messages
   posted 4–5 weeks early, each naming an artist that appears exactly once in the corpus. *This is the
   highest-value source by a wide margin.* A builder should extract every capitalised multi-word
   phrase and every music link from chat and check it against the round's artists and albums.
2. **Chat in the 72h window around playlist drop.** The rule "no upvotes on songs you've heard" turned
   8/29 into a public disclosure session. "I played the whole sound and color album yesterday morning,"
   "Dang someone already picked my song," "It's by far the best song on the list," "Unlike Old Town
   Road, I don't think I know that one" — these are near-confessions. **Include post-deadline chat, not
   just pre-deadline.** Most attribution tools would stop at the deadline and lose all of it.
3. **"Options I considered" posts.** Players volunteer their runner-up picks constantly (Clements ×3,
   Conor ×2, Shane ×1, Jimmy ×1). These reveal the *bin* a player was shopping in, which generalises
   better than their submission history does. Worth a dedicated extractor keyed on "another option,"
   "was considering," "almost submitted," "my next pick," "testing the waters."
4. **Past submission `comment` fields.** Rich for voice and for named obsessions — Jensen's 8-string
   guitar essay, JB's "my favorite country artist." Note that only ~half of comments survive to the
   ballot; the completed-round table has them all.
5. **Vote comments.** Excellent for *taste declarations* ("I'm a huge fan of that genre") and for
   *social graph* (who ribs whom). Conor's Shane jokes and JB's Shane politics are what made "For MAGA
   Shane" tractable. Also the best source of nicknames.
6. **Round description text.** Told me the theme had been swapped from an instrumental theme, which
   reframed two of the nine songs entirely. Cheap and essential; do not skip it.
7. **`players` + `player_identities`.** Required to resolve "CJ Wookie" = "Conor Johnston" = "~ Conor J"
   = "Conor" = the "Conner" other players keep misattributing. Without this join the chat is unusable.

### Carried little or none

8. **`player_profiles.taste_fingerprint`.** For the Boarz players these are **stale and near-useless** —
   all nine were generated off round 135 alone, eight of nine are `"confidence": "low"`, and they say
   things like "Shane's very limited history centers on an early-2000s UK garage submission … no clear
   favorites emerging." I read them and used none of them. By contrast the Mashew/missmara fingerprints
   (other leagues, regenerated later) are excellent. **Fix the refresh, or gate on `confidence` before
   putting these in a prompt** — a low-confidence fingerprint is worse than no fingerprint because it
   reads authoritative.
9. **Vote *points* (numeric).** I computed affinity and standings; the standings were mildly useful
   (Conor is dead last, which supports him trolling), but pairwise vote affinity told me nothing about
   authorship. Low priority.
10. **Other players' in-round guesses.** Tempting and mostly wrong. Round 148–149 vote comments are
    full of confident misattributions (djensen37 guessed "Conor?" on eight of nine songs; Mashew went
    0-for-9 by Clements' count: "Matt how did you miss every single song"). Conor's R153 claim about
    Paletz and Koz was half-right for a reason he may have gotten from the same chat log. **Feed these
    as weak priors at most, never as evidence.**
11. **`season_standings`, `round_theme_tags`, `song_metadata`, `song_audio_features`.** Not consulted /
    no bearing.

### The one structural gap

There is no field anywhere linking a chat message to a *song entity* except by raw text match, and
`chat_songs` did not contain any of the tracks players shared in this window. Resolving shared Spotify
links to artist/title at ingest time would have answered the "Feel Love" question outright and would
make source #1 above dramatically cheaper to exploit.
