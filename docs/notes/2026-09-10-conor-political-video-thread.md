## Implications for improving the concept

1. **Pre-clear the concept, not the cut.** Both Shane and Conor named this. One
   paragraph to "mom" before any credits are spent converts a spiked video into a
   shipped one. Cheapest possible fix.
2. **Cast composition is a prompt, not a reshoot.** The stated objection has a
   near-zero-cost remedy in an AI pipeline. Bake a diverse-cast requirement into
   the generation brief by default rather than fixing it in review.
3. **Local specificity is the quality signal.** The only unprompted praise of
   content was JB on the Sunset van scene. Lean into recognisable SF geography.
4. **The economics are better than assumed.** $40 against a "few hundo" budget.
   Iterating twice is still cheap; treat v1 as a draft rather than a one-shot.
5. **Keep length short.** Shane: difficulty rises *exponentially* with length.
   0:54 worked. Don't let round 2 creep past a minute.
6. **Round 2 has a script owner already** (Conor) and a hook already chosen
   (No Scrubs / housing votes). The pending dependency is the theme pre-approval,
   not the creative.
7. **Watch the incentive split.** A killed video costs Conor nothing and Shane
   $40 plus his time. Any repeat arrangement should settle who absorbs a spike.
