How the score & buckets work

Tastemaker score: each song gets a 0–100 discovery percentile — how obscure it is (from Last.fm play counts) vs. every other song this season. Your score is the median of your picks, so one mainstream night can't sink a discovery maven.

  • Radio Hit · obscurity <10 — the most familiar, mainstream picks.
  • Recognizable · 10–19 — known, but not the most obvious choice.
  • Curious Cut · 20–29 — discoverable without being ultra-obscure.
  • Rabbit Hole · 30+ — deep cuts, most likely a first-time discovery.

Buckets use each song's raw obscurity (absolute scale); the headline score uses the season-relative percentile.
