## 1. Podium — FILLED
*(pasted verbatim from `recap/drafts/podium_v1.md`; top-3 paragraph LOCKED — do not rewrite)*

Matt takes the Season 1 crown with 80, JB finishes three points back at 77, and an entirely independent analysis confirms that the co-commissioners finishing 1–2 in the league they run, name, and administer is completely normal; the relevant knowers have reviewed the naming culture and found no reason to ask further questions. It would have been trivial to engineer a co-commissioner tie for first; that one of them took second is proof enough both are beyond reproach. But Jimmy, third at 65, is the story of the season: he joined in week two, promptly posted zeroes with “Tequila” and “Booty Man,” then apparently decided he understood the assignment, winning three rounds—the league most—and taking the finale with 14 for Refused’s “New Noise.” He missed week one, started with back-to-back zeroes, and still came a c#&% hair short of the title. Hair joke over.

Behind them the field sorted itself: **Clements (64)** highest score never to win a week, wildfire-watcher posting at 4 a.m. between feedings; **Dave Jensen (59)** mild in the chat until the voting comments — then the sleeper cell left no survivors; **Shane (43)** showed up for every round and left almost no written trail (two words all season, one of which was "Asshole"), every pick ~80 hours early. Then the low-participation wing: **Darren (56)** — whatever he does for a living must be important, because he set the Music League record for minimum hours, about ten minutes a round to submit, vote, and peace out — won the opener, banked two wins and the second-most podiums, then zeroed the finale and still somehow finished sixth; and **Steiny (38)**, the other ghost, a little better on results — barely in the chat (a flex, the group ruled), but he **won a round with seven**, lowest winning score of the year, math shown, bare legs respected, a crown he only got because **Grant / Koz (31)**, auditor of what counts as a flex, forgot to vote and voided his own raw round win. And **Conor (8)** dead last: he could have done something truly memorable and finished the season on zero — or even negative, though zero would have been cleaner — but he couldn't even get that right. The Venn was simple — songs Conor likes ∩ songs that fit the theme ∩ songs the group likes enough — empty for nine rounds, then he stopped thinking about it and "Because the Night" landed in the middle for his best (and only) showing. Matt on top, Jon three points back, Jimmy a hair short, everyone else exactly as the tape remembers them — **Boarz II Men, Season One.**

**Potty talk.** JB's Toto Washlet evangelism became a season fixture — *“Toto Washlets are the opposite of a flex… a well-meaning plea for you to better your lives and your poop shoots”* (7/27), earning him **“mr butt water”** (Matt) and the self-applied **“hose drinkers.”** Jensen's running verdict: *“You'll know if someone has a bidet, they'll bring it up in every conversation… It's the new CrossFit.”* The CrossFit line went literal on 9/15 when the group litigated a competitor soiling the floor mid-race — Matt: *“she won the race - but I don;t think it was a fair outcome.”*

**Breeding.** Clements canvassed the parents on going from one kid to two — *“I have a new skin pet hatching in a couple weeks”* (7/31) — then announced *“Baby boy is here. 8 lb 13 oz. Not how I planned my Saturday… The bloodline goes on”* (8/22). Shane followed on 9/22: *“Also - I made a kid.”* JB, on afterbirth logistics: *“Are you putting the afterbirth in your deep freeze with your 1/4 cow purchase? Make sure everything is clearly labeled.”*

**Koz's Puerto Rico live diary.** Grant narrated a solo PR trip in real-time fragments — *“I'm in a bar in Puerto Rico and there is a 60 year old bar tender who needs my attention”* (7/24), *“If you hear about a drowning in San Juan don't look for me”* (7/27) — then disclosed the aftermath: *“I definitely came close to a Puerto Rican jail yday”* after knocking on the wrong resort door, getting jumped, and meeting *“a female police that came - she was not fucking about.”* Coda (8/29): *“I was texting in real time whilst almost getting booked… Fortunately I charmed the pants off the lesbian police chief.”*

**The Clam Scam.** Clements bought into a “hired refund” scheme and the group audited it for weeks — *“I find a good scam irresistible”* (8/3) → *“Up $0. Down $400. Down an hour of time… Then retire from scamming”* (8/18). It fused with the birth thread when Jimmy proposed naming the baby **Refund Johnson**; Matt endorsed *“refund johnson is a beautiful name,”* Steiny upgraded to **refundo/refundora**.

**College / the bros not in the league.** The chat kept relapsing into UCLA oral history — the two-shirtless-Marshalls fight (JB: *“Little Marshall throwing a punch that caused the guy's nose to explode in a shower of blood”*), a Fall '99 responsiveness scouting report, and Conor's definitive 17-name **coolness tribunal** (*“I'm judging dorkiness. I know a lot about it”*) — in which he ranked himself 12th and Mariani dead last at 17.
