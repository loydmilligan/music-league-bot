## Ledes by outcome

### LEDE A — "Jon steals it on the last song" *(Jon 1st; ~30%)*
The season-long frontrunner gets dethroned at the buzzer. Mashew led effectively
wire-to-wire, but a single point was all that separated them going into "These Go
to 11," and Jon's final submission out-scored Mashew's by the two points he needed.
Frame it as the quietest, cruelest kind of collapse — Mashew never lost the lead
until the one round where it counted. Note that Jon got here as the season's
*steadiest* scorer (two blowout round wins, few disasters) while Mashew's title run
was built on volatile spikes.

### LEDE B — "So close: Mashew survives, Jon runner-up" *(Jon 2nd / Mashew champion; ~69% — the most likely result)*
The modal, and most likely true, ending: Mashew holds on and takes Season 1 by the
narrowest possible margin, with Jon an agonizing point or two short in 2nd. This is
the wire-to-wire champion story — lead the digest with Mashew's consistency and the
fact that Jon was never able to land the one big final-round song he needed. Play
up the cruelty of the math: Jon spent the whole back half within a single point and
still couldn't close it.

### LEDE C — "Decided by one point — and the tiebreaker" *(exact points tie; ~4–5%)*
If Jon's final song beats Mashew's by *exactly one point*, the two finish level on
season score and it goes to the tiebreakers — total upvoters, then fewest
downvoters. Mashew leads both going in (upvoters 53–51, downvoters 11–12), and in
every "decided by one point" case across the season the tiebreak broke that way, so
this still crowns **Mashew**. The angle: the closest possible finish that *still*
isn't close enough for Jon — a photo finish where the replay confirms the leader.

### LEDE D — "Co-champions" *(shared 1st; <1%, longshot novelty)*
The vanishing-odds fairy tale: a points tie *and* a dead heat on both tiebreakers
would leave Mashew and Jon as **co-champions**, sharing the Season 1 crown (the
alphabetical rule is only listing order, not a winner). Never happened in the data —
keep this as a one-line "the only way everybody wins" aside, not a headline.

### LEDE E — "The anonymous kingmaker" *(the Danzig "Mother" angle — use only if the reveal supports it)*
Submissions are anonymous until the round closes. If Jon's mystery song turns out to
be Danzig's "Mother" (a genuine full-volume sing-along, textbook for this theme),
lean into the reveal: the song that either won or lost him the season was hiding in
plain sight on the playlist. Only run this once the actual submitter map is known —
do not assert it pre-reveal.

### LEDE F — "The Conor–Jon cold war" *(evergreen subplot, works under any outcome)*
Independent of the crown, the season's best running feud is the Conor/Jon voting
dynamic — Conor's own words: *"Jon, you and I have an interesting relationship where
I really like your songs and you hate mine."* In a season Jon lost (or won) by a
single point, whether Conor up- or down-voted Jon's final song is a delicious
sub-plot; if the margin is tight, one voter's ballot literally decided the title.
